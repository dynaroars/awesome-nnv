April 2019

CAV 2019 and ICML 2019 decisions: the SMT-based Marabou framework appears alongside randomized smoothing with a tight Gaussian ℓ2 guarantee. POPQORN certifies recurrent networks including LSTMs and GRUs, PROVEN takes a probabilistic approach to verification, and non-uniform bounds against adversarial attacks are certified.

5 NNV papers accepted in April 2019, at CAV 2019 and ICML 2019.

ICML 2019 4 papers

  • ICML 2019 Probabilistic certification Certified Adversarial Robustness via Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Jeremy Cohen, Elan Rosenfeld, Zico Kolter

    Proves a tight ℓ2 robustness guarantee for randomized smoothing with Gaussian noise, turning any noise-tolerant classifier into a certifiably robust one; reaches 49% certified top-1 accuracy on ImageNet for ℓ2 perturbations below 0.5.

  • ICML 2019 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation POPQORN: Quantifying Robustness of Recurrent Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Ching-Yun Ko, Zhaoyang Lyu, Tsui-Wei Weng, Luca Daniel, Ngai Wong, Dahua Lin

    POPQORN computes certified lower bounds on the minimum adversarial perturbation for recurrent networks, including vanilla RNNs, LSTMs and GRUs, handling multi-step inputs and gate-state interactions; per-step robustness quantification gives new insights.

CAV 2019 1 paper

  • CAV 2019 Complete verificationOther The Marabou Framework for Verification and Analysis of Deep Neural Networks
    Guy Katz, Derek A. Huang, Duligur Ibeling, Kyle Julian, Christopher Lazarus, Rachel Lim, Parth Shah, Shantanu Thakoor, Haoze Wu, Aleksandar Zeljic, David L. Dill, Mykel J. Kochenderfer, Clark Barrett

    Marabou is an SMT-based framework that verifies deep neural network properties by turning queries into constraint satisfaction problems, supporting various activations and topologies, high-level reasoning to prune search, parallel execution and TensorFlow inputs.
