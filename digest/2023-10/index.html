October 2023

ICSE 2024 and POPL 2024 decisions: WraLU over-approximates the ReLU convex hull for verification within PRIMA, and VeRe uses linear-relaxation-based verification to localize faulty neurons and repair deep neural networks.

2 NNV papers accepted in October 2023, at ICSE 2024 and POPL 2024.

POPL 2024 1 paper

  • POPL 2024 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation ReLU Hull Approximation
    Zhongkui Ma, Jiaying Li, Guangdong Bai

    WraLU over-approximates the convex hull of ReLU for neural network verification by wrapping it in a polytope whose upper faces are built from the edges and vertices of the lower faces; integrated into PRIMA, it cuts LP constraints by up to half without losing precision.

ICSE 2024 1 paper

  • ICSE 2024 Repair & patchingBound propagation & abstract interpretation VeRe: Verification Guided Synthesis for Repairing Deep Neural Networks
    Jianan Ma, Pengfei Yang, Jingyi Wang, Youcheng Sun, Cheng-Chao Huang, Zhen Wang

    VeRe repairs deep neural networks by using linear-relaxation-based verification to localize faulty neurons symbolically and then optimizing their parameters; it cuts backdoor attack success from 98.47% to 0.38% on average and repairs all 36 safety-property tasks.
