December 2025

FSE 2026 and OOPSLA 2026 decisions: complete ReLU verification with fewer binary variables through SART, precondition synthesis with statistical guarantees via StatPre, and structural robustness verification of DNNs with VeriS.

3 NNV papers accepted in December 2025, at FSE 2026 and OOPSLA 2026.

FSE 2026 2 papers

  • FSE 2026 Probabilistic certification Precondition Synthesis for Deep Neural Networks with Statistical Guarantees
    Zengyu Liu, Bai Xue, Pengfei Yang, Ji Wang

    Synthesizes preconditions for deep neural networks with statistical guarantees via StatPre, a select-and-solve framework using Box-based abstraction, cluster-based center selection and statistical model approximation; on 15 models and 27 properties from 6 benchmarks it gives broader coverage than 4 existing schemes.

  • FSE 2026 Beyond ℓp input propertiesBound propagation & abstract interpretation Verifying Structural Robustness of Deep Neural Network
    Hai Duong, Thanh Le, Lam Nguyen, ThanhVu Nguyen

    Verifies structural robustness of DNNs against coordinated linear position-invariant and position-varying perturbations; VeriS encodes these perturbations as subnetworks so existing local-robustness verifiers apply, verifying 78% of 5508 problems across image, audio and healthcare networks.

OOPSLA 2026 1 paper

  • OOPSLA 2026 Complete verification SART: Sign-Absolute Reformulation Theory for Binary Variable Reduction in Neural Network Verification
    Jin Xu, Miaomiao Zhang, Bowen Du

    SART cuts the binary variables needed for complete ReLU network verification below one per unstable neuron, proving final-layer variables can be halved; the LayerABS verifier extends the gain to all layers through progressive tightening and outperforms existing baselines across benchmarks.
