CCS 2024, ECCV 2024 and ISSTA 2024 decisions: vision-focused certification, including certifiably robust image watermarks and face recognition, cross-input certified training for universal perturbations and ProTIP for text-to-image diffusion models. Certified continual learning for neural network regression and a certified defense against backdoors in federated graph learning complete the picture.

7 NNV papers accepted in July 2024, at CCS 2024, ECCV 2024 and ISSTA 2024.

ECCV 2024 5 papers

  • ECCV 2024 oral 🎉 Probabilistic certification Adversarial Robustification via Text-to-Image Diffusion Models (arXiv)
    Daewon Choi, Jongheon Jeong, Huiwon Jang, Jinwoo Shin

    Achieves provable adversarial robustness without data by turning text-to-image diffusion models into adaptable denoisers in a denoise-and-classify pipeline, adapted with a few synthetic reference images; applied to pre-trained CLIP it raises certified robustness of its zero-shot classifiers while keeping accuracy, beating approaches that use full training data.

  • ECCV 2024 Probabilistic certification Certifiably Robust Image Watermark (arXiv)
    Zhengyuan Jiang, Moyang Guo, Yuepeng Hu, Jinyuan Jia, Neil Gong

    Extends randomized smoothing to image watermarking, deriving certified robustness guarantees against both removal and forgery attacks and algorithms to estimate them, and evaluates the resulting watermarks for certified and empirical robustness.

  • ECCV 2024 Certified trainingGlobal & relational properties Cross-Input Certified Training for Universal Perturbations (arXiv)
    Changming Xu, Gagandeep Singh

    Trains networks certifiably robust to universal adversarial perturbations with CITRUS, a cross-input certified training method; across datasets, architectures and perturbation sizes it improves standard accuracy by up to 10.3% over traditional certified training and reaches state-of-the-art certified UAP accuracy.

  • ECCV 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond linear output properties ProTIP: Probabilistic Robustness Verification on Text-to-Image Diffusion Models Against Stochastic Perturbation (arXiv)
    Yi Zhang, Yun Tang, Wenjie Ruan, Xiaowei Huang, Siddartha Khastgir, Paul A. Jennings, Xingyu Zhao

    Defines probabilistic robustness for text-to-image diffusion models and estimates it with statistical guarantees, using sequential analysis with efficacy and futility early stopping and adaptive concentration inequalities to pick the number of stochastic perturbations; ProTIP is effective and efficient on common models and ranks common defences.

ISSTA 2024 1 paper

  • ISSTA 2024 OtherCertified training Certified Continual Learning for Neural Network Regression
    Long H. Pham, Jun Sun

    Certified continual learning keeps the verified correctness properties of a neural network intact for as long as possible while it is retrained on new data, extending existing continual learning methods; across several networks, trained models keep their certified correctness and usually high utility.

CCS 2024 1 paper

  • CCS 2024 OtherBeyond feed-forward nets Distributed Backdoor Attacks on Federated Graph Learning and Certified Defenses
    Yuxin Yang, Qiang Li, Jinyuan Jia, Yuan Hong, Binghui Wang

    Proposes a subgraph-trigger backdoor attack on federated graph learning and a certified defense that splits test graphs into subgraphs and takes a majority vote, giving deterministic, provably tight robustness against triggers of any shape and location across six graph datasets.
