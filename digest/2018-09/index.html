September 2018

NeurIPS 2018 decisions: bound propagation and abstract interpretation lead, with CROWN for general activations, DeepZ, symbolic-interval safety analysis and semidefinite relaxations. A unified view of piecewise-linear verification brings a benchmark comparison, while Lipschitz estimation with AutoLip and SeqLip, Lipschitz-margin training and scaled provable adversarial defenses advance certified robustness.

8 NNV papers accepted in September 2018, at NeurIPS 2018.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Complete verification A Unified View of Piecewise Linear Neural Network Verification (arXiv)
    Rudy Bunel, Ilker Turkaslan, Philip Torr, Pushmeet Kohli, M. Pawan Kumar

    Presents a unified framework covering existing piecewise-linear network verifiers, yielding new combined methods with a two-orders-of-magnitude speedup, and a benchmark dataset used for the first experimental comparison of verification algorithms and problem hardness factors.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationComplete verification Efficient Formal Safety Analysis of Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Shiqi Wang, Kexin Pei, Justin Whitehouse, Junfeng Yang, Suman Jana

    Checks safety properties of neural networks, such as L-norm robustness, by estimating tight output bounds over input ranges and finding concrete counterexamples, outperforming existing approaches by orders of magnitude on networks 10 times larger.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Efficient Neural Network Robustness Certification with General Activation Functions (arXiv)
    Huan Zhang, Tsui-Wei Weng, Pin-Yu Chen, Cho-Jui Hsieh, Luca Daniel

    CROWN certifies robustness of networks with general activations, including ReLU, tanh, sigmoid and arctan, by bounding activations with adaptively chosen linear and quadratic functions, improving certified lower bounds over Fast-Lin on ReLU networks at similar cost.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Fast and Effective Robustness Certification
    Gagandeep Singh, Timon Gehr, Matthew Mirman, Markus Püschel, Martin Vechev

    DeepZ certifies neural network robustness with abstract interpretation sound under floating point, supporting ReLU, tanh and sigmoid in feedforward and convolutional networks; it verifies 97% on an 88,500-unit network under L∞ ε=0.1 in 133 seconds average.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Lipschitz regularity of deep neural networks: analysis and efficient estimation (arXiv)
    Aladin Virmaux, Kevin Scaman

    Shows exact Lipschitz constant computation is NP-hard even for two-layer networks, then gives AutoLip, a generic upper bound for any automatically differentiable function, and SeqLip, which tightens bounds for sequential networks by splitting per layer pair.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Certified training Lipschitz-Margin Training: Scalable Certification of Perturbation Invariance for Deep Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Yusuke Tsuzuku, Issei Sato, Masashi Sugiyama

    Lower-bounds the adversarial perturbation size needed to fool a network using Lipschitz constants and prediction margins, applicable to complicated architectures, plus Lipschitz-margin training that enlarges provably guarded areas, giving non-trivial guarantees on large networks.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Certified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretation Scaling provable adversarial defenses (arXiv)
    Eric Wong, Frank R. Schmidt, Jan Hendrik Metzen, Zico Kolter

    Scales provable adversarial training to general networks with skip connections and nonlinearities, using random projections and cascades; provable robust error falls from 5.8% to 3.1% on MNIST at ε=0.1 and from 80% to 36.4% on CIFAR at ε=2/255.

  • NeurIPS 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Semidefinite relaxations for certifying robustness to adversarial examples (arXiv)
    Aditi Raghunathan, Jacob Steinhardt, Percy Liang

    Certifies robustness of arbitrary ReLU networks to adversarial examples with a semidefinite relaxation that is tighter than previous relaxations and gives meaningful guarantees on three networks not trained against it.
