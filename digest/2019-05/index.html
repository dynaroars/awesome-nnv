May 2019

IJCAI 2019 decisions: Robustra trains provably robust networks over a reference model's adversarial space, while other titles address statistical robustness guarantees for Bayesian neural networks, differential privacy with provable robustness, and counterexample-guided strategy improvement for POMDPs using recurrent neural networks.

5 NNV papers accepted in May 2019, at IJCAI 2019 and S&P 2019. 1 of them is placed by conference date, as the notification date is not recorded.

IJCAI 2019 4 papers

  • IJCAI 2019 NN control systemsBeyond feed-forward nets Counterexample-Guided Strategy Improvement for POMDPs Using Recurrent Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Steven Carr, Nils Jansen, Ralf Wimmer, Alexandru Constantin Serban, Bernd Becker, Ufuk Topcu

    Synthesizes POMDP strategies that provably satisfy temporal logic constraints by training an RNN, restricting it to a finite-memory strategy, verifying the induced Markov chain and retraining from counterexamples, improving the state of the art by up to three orders of magnitude in solving time and model size.

  • IJCAI 2019 Probabilistic certification Heterogeneous Gaussian Mechanism: Preserving Differential Privacy in Deep Learning with Provable Robustness (arXiv)
    NhatHai Phan, Minh N. Vu, Yang Liu, Ruoming Jin, Dejing Dou, Xintao Wu, My T. Thai

    Preserves differential privacy in deep networks with a Heterogeneous Gaussian Mechanism that relaxes the privacy budget constraint to (0, ∞) and allows arbitrary noise redistribution; injecting this noise into the first hidden layer yields a tighter provable robustness bound against adversarial examples.

  • IJCAI 2019 Certified training Robustra: Training Provable Robust Neural Networks over Reference Adversarial Space
    Linyi Li, Zexuan Zhong, Bo Li, Tao Xie

    Robustra trains provably robust DNNs by solving the attacker-defender min-max game over a reference model's adversarial space through a linearized dual. On MNIST it cuts the ℓ∞ provable error bound from 2.74% to 2.09% at ε=0.1 and 24.19% to 16.91% at ε=0.3.

  • IJCAI 2019 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets Statistical Guarantees for the Robustness of Bayesian Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Luca Cardelli, Marta Kwiatkowska, Luca Laurenti, Nicola Paoletti, Andrea Patanè, Matthew Wicker

    Defines probabilistic robustness for Bayesian neural networks as the probability that an adversarial point exists in a bounded set, and estimates it with a priori error and confidence bounds using statistical verification, comparing approximate inference techniques on MNIST and a two-class subset of GTSRB.

S&P 2019 1 paper

  • S&P 2019 Probabilistic certification Certified Robustness to Adversarial Examples with Differential Privacy (by conference date)
    Mathias Lécuyer, Vaggelis Atlidakis, Roxana Geambasu, Daniel Hsu, Suman Jana

    PixelDP certifies robustness to norm-bounded adversarial examples using a connection to differential privacy, the first certified defense that scales to large networks and datasets such as Inception on ImageNet and applies to arbitrary model types.
