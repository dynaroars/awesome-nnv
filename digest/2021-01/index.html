January 2021

ICLR 2021 and USENIX Security 2021 decisions: certified training leads, with Cayley-transform orthogonal layers, compositional architectures and detection-aware IBP. Complete verification speeds up with GPU-based LiRPA branch-and-bound and geometric projections, while a floating-point attack shows how to fool a complete verifier. Patch defenses such as BagCert and PatchGuard, and collective certificates for graph neural networks, also appear.

  • ICLR 2021 Formal explanations Scaling Symbolic Methods using Gradients for Neural Model Explanation (arXiv)
    Subham Sekhar Sahoo, Subhashini Venugopalan, Li Li, Rishabh Singh, Patrick Riley

    Scales SMT-based analysis to large networks for model explanation by using Integrated Gradients to select first-layer neurons, then solving for minimal input masks that keep those activations above a threshold; resulting saliency maps on MNIST, ImageNet and Beer Reviews are sparser with higher saliency scores.

    Virtual · Poster · Mon 3 May, 16:00–18:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 OtherCertified trainingBeyond ℓp input properties Efficient Certified Defenses Against Patch Attacks on Image Classifiers (arXiv)
    Jan Hendrik Metzen, Maksym Yatsura

    BagCert combines a model architecture with a certification procedure for adversarial patches, plus a loss for end-to-end training of certified patch robustness; on CIFAR10 it certifies 10,000 examples in 43 seconds on one GPU, with 86% clean and 60% certified accuracy against 5x5 patches.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 4 May, 08:00–10:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Scaling the Convex Barrier with Active Sets
    Alessandro De Palma, Harkirat Singh Behl, Rudy Bunel, Philip Torr, M. Pawan Kumar

    A dual algorithm operating on a small active set of dual variables makes a tighter, exponentially sized linear relaxation of piecewise-linear activations practical, with GPU parallelism and anytime valid bounds; it gives better bounds than off-the-shelf solvers in a fraction of their time.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 4 May, 08:00–10:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Other Fantastic Four: Differentiable and Efficient Bounds on Singular Values of Convolution Layers
    Sahil Singla, Soheil Feizi

    Derives four provable, differentiable upper bounds on the spectral norm of 2D multi-channel convolution layers and takes their minimum as an efficient bound usable to bound Lipschitz constants or curvature; as a regularizer it improves generalization and robustness on MNIST and CIFAR-10.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 5 May, 00:00–02:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Other Fooling a Complete Neural Network Verifier
    Dániel Zombori, Balázs Bánhelyi, Tibor Csendes, István Megyeri, Márk Jelasity

    Shows floating-point roundoff can be exploited to build adversarial networks whose true robustness differs sharply from what a complete verifier reports, and to plant backdoors the verifier misses; a small weight perturbation is offered as a simple defense against this attack.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 5 May, 08:00–10:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 spotlight 🎉 Certified training Orthogonalizing Convolutional Layers with the Cayley Transform (arXiv)
    Asher Trockman, Zico Kolter

    Parameterizes orthogonal convolutional layers by applying the Cayley transform to skew-symmetric convolutions in the Fourier domain; networks with these layers outperform prior deterministic certified defenses against ℓ2-bounded adversaries while scaling to larger architectures.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 5 May, 16:00–18:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Certified training Provably robust classification of adversarial examples with detection
    Fatemeh Sheikholeslami, Ali Lotfi, Zico Kolter

    Jointly trains a provably robust classifier and detector by adding an abstain/detection class and extending interval bound propagation to this objective under ℓ∞ perturbations; it outperforms plain IBP on MNIST and CIFAR-10, especially for large perturbations such as 8/255 and 16/255.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 5 May, 16:00–18:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond feed-forward nets Estimating Lipschitz constants of monotone deep equilibrium models
    Chirag Pabbaraju, Ezra Winston, Zico Kolter

    Bounds the Lipschitz constants of monotone deep equilibrium models as a simple function of the strong monotonicity parameter, for both input-output and weight-output maps; the bounds are small relative to standard DNNs and yield depth-independent PAC-Bayes generalization bounds.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 6 May, 00:00–02:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Certified training Certify or Predict: Boosting Certified Robustness with Compositional Architectures
    Mark Niklas Müller, Mislav Balunovic, Martin Vechev

    Boosts certified robustness without retraining by pairing an accurate deep network with a smaller certified network and adaptively selecting which one processes each input; on CIFAR-10 at ℓ∞ 2/255 it reaches 90.1% natural accuracy with 27.5% certified robustness.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 6 May, 08:00–10:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Beyond feed-forward netsBeyond ℓp input properties Collective Robustness Certificates: Exploiting Interdependence in Graph Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Jan Schuchardt, Aleksandar Bojchevski, Johannes Klicpera, Stephan Günnemann

    Collective robustness certificates for Graph Neural Networks count how many predictions stay stable under a single shared perturbation, fusing single-node certificates using locality; on Citeseer node classification the average number of certifiable feature perturbations rises from 7 to 351.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 6 May, 08:00–10:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 NN control systems Enforcing robust control guarantees within neural network policies (arXiv)
    Priya L. Donti, Melrose Roderick, Mahyar Fazlyab, Zico Kolter

    Builds neural-network control policies that enforce the same provable robustness criteria as robust control by embedding convex-optimization-based projection layers, improving average-case performance over robust control and worst-case stability over non-robust deep RL on several domains.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 6 May, 16:00–18:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 spotlight 🎉 Complete verification Fast Geometric Projections for Local Robustness Certification (arXiv)
    Aymeric Fromherz, Klas Leino, Matt Fredrikson, Bryan Parno, Corina S. Pasareanu

    Checks ℓ2 local robustness of piecewise-linear feed-forward networks by searching for decision boundaries in nearby linear regions using simple geometric projections, implemented in parallel on GPU; it is more precise than many approximate verifiers and orders of magnitude faster than complete ones.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 7 May, 00:00–02:00 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2021 Complete verificationBound propagation & abstract interpretation Fast and Complete: Enabling Complete Neural Network Verification with Rapid and Massively Parallel Incomplete Verifiers (arXiv)
    Kaidi Xu, Huan Zhang, Shiqi Wang, Yihan Wang, Suman Jana, Xue Lin, Cho-Jui Hsieh

    Replaces linear programming in branch-and-bound complete verification with backward-mode LiRPA on GPUs, adding gradient-based bound tightening, batch splits and minimal LP use to keep completeness; on a single GPU it runs an order of magnitude faster than LP-based approaches.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 7 May, 00:00–02:00 (UTC)

  • USENIX Security 2021 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties PatchGuard: A Provably Robust Defense against Adversarial Patches via Small Receptive Fields and Masking (arXiv)
    Chong Xiang, Arjun Nitin Bhagoji, Vikash Sehwag, Prateek Mittal

    PatchGuard provably defends image classifiers against localized adversarial patches by using CNNs with small receptive fields to bound corrupted features and a robust masking aggregation, with strong provable and clean accuracy on ImageNet, ImageNette and CIFAR-10.
