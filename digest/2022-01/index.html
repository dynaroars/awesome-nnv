  • ICLR 2022 Certified training Boosting the Certified Robustness of L-infinity Distance Nets (arXiv)
    Bohang Zhang, Du Jiang, Di He, Liwei Wang

    Shows l-infinity-distance nets have an inherent certified robustness advantage and improves their training with a loss mixing scaled cross-entropy and clipped hinge loss, raising certified accuracy on CIFAR-10 at eps 8/255 from 33.30% to 40.06%.

    Virtual · Poster · Mon 25 Apr, 09:30–11:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 NN control systemsOther COPA: Certifying Robust Policies for Offline Reinforcement Learning against Poisoning Attacks (arXiv)
    Fan Wu, Linyi Li, Huan Zhang, Bhavya Kailkhura, Krishnaram Kenthapadi, Ding Zhao, Bo Li

    COPA certifies offline reinforcement learning policies against training-time poisoning, bounding how many manipulated trajectories are tolerated under per-state action stability and cumulative reward criteria, with new partition and aggregation protocols; some certificates are proved tight, others NP-complete.

    Virtual · Poster · Mon 25 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 NN control systemsProbabilistic certification CROP: Certifying Robust Policies for Reinforcement Learning through Functional Smoothing (arXiv)
    Fan Wu, Linyi Li, Zijian Huang, Yevgeniy Vorobeychik, Ding Zhao, Bo Li

    CROP certifies reinforcement learning policies at action and reward levels, using local smoothing of Q-function policies for per-state action robustness and global plus adaptive local smoothing for lower bounds on finite-horizon cumulative reward, evaluated on Atari, Highway and CartPole.

    Virtual · Poster · Mon 25 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Repair & patching Sound and Complete Neural Network Repair with Minimality and Locality Guarantees (arXiv)
    Feisi Fu, Wenchao Li

    REASSURE repairs ReLU networks by adding a patch network on the linear region of a buggy input, giving sound and complete repair that is minimal in function space and leaves outputs away from the repair region unchanged.

    Virtual · Poster · Mon 25 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Probabilistic certification Almost Tight L0-norm Certified Robustness of Top-k Predictions against Adversarial Perturbations (arXiv)
    Jinyuan Jia, Binghui Wang, Xiaoyu Cao, Hongbin Liu, Neil Gong

    Derives an almost tight l0-norm certified robustness guarantee for top-k predictions using randomized smoothing; on ImageNet the smoothed classifier reaches 69.2% certified top-3 accuracy when an attacker may change 5 pixels.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 26 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 spotlight 🎉 Certified training Improved deterministic l2 robustness on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 (arXiv)
    Sahil Singla, Surbhi Singla, Soheil Feizi

    Certifies 1-Lipschitz CNNs under l2 by relaxing orthogonalization of the last linear layer, adding a Householder activation and certificate regularization; provable robust accuracy rises 4.71% on CIFAR-100 and 5.81% on CIFAR-10.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 26 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Probabilistic certificationNN control systems Policy Smoothing for Provably Robust Reinforcement Learning (arXiv)
    Aounon Kumar, Alexander Levine, Soheil Feizi

    Policy smoothing adds Gaussian noise to observations so the total reward of a reinforcement learning agent is certified against adaptive adversaries, based on an adaptive Neyman-Pearson lemma; tested on Cartpole, Pong, Freeway and Mountain Car.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 26 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Provably Robust Adversarial Examples (arXiv)
    Dimitar I. Dimitrov, Gagandeep Singh, Timon Gehr, Martin Vechev

    Constructs provably robust adversarial examples, connected input regions certified to contain only adversarial inputs under pixel intensity or geometric perturbations, by iteratively refining sampled regions with volume-maximizing optimization until a verifier certifies them; PARADE finds regions with about 10^573 and 10^599 adversarial examples, and these regions are more effective against randomized smoothing defenses than the single attacks used to build them.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 26 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Complete verificationBound propagation & abstract interpretation Complete Verification via Multi-Neuron Relaxation Guided Branch-and-Bound (arXiv)
    Claudio Ferrari, Mark Niklas Müller, Nikola Jovanovic, Martin Vechev

    Builds a complete verifier that uses multi-neuron convex relaxations to sharply reduce the number of branch-and-bound subproblems and a GPU-based dual optimizer to solve the rest, achieving a new state of the art on established benchmarks and up to 28% certification gains on more accurate networks.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 27 Apr, 09:30–11:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Probabilistic certificationCertified training On the Certified Robustness for Ensemble Models and Beyond (arXiv)
    Zhuolin Yang, Linyi Li, Xiaojun Xu, Bhavya Kailkhura, Tao Xie, Bo Li

    Analyses certified robustness of ensembles, proving diversified gradients and large confidence margins are necessary and sufficient under model smoothness, and proposes Diversity Regularized Training, which yields higher certified L2 robustness than single and ensemble models on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and ImageNet.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 27 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Certified trainingBeyond feed-forward nets Certified Robustness for Deep Equilibrium Models via Interval Bound Propagation
    Colin Wei, Zico Kolter

    IBP-MonDEQ is a deep equilibrium layer whose robustness is verified by interval bounds obtained as the unique fixed point of an IBP-inspired equilibrium equation; such models reach l-infinity certified robustness comparable to similar-sized explicit networks.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 28 Apr, 01:30–03:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 spotlight 🎉 Probabilistic certification How to Robustify Black-Box ML Models? A Zeroth-Order Optimization Perspective (arXiv)
    Yimeng Zhang, Yuguang Yao, Jinghan Jia, Jinfeng Yi, Mingyi Hong, Shiyu Chang, Sijia Liu

    ZO-AE-DS defends black-box models using only queries by combining denoised smoothing with zeroth-order optimization and a prepended autoencoder to reduce gradient-estimate variance, improving accuracy, certified robustness and query complexity on image classification and reconstruction.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 28 Apr, 01:30–03:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Certified training On the Convergence of Certified Robust Training with Interval Bound Propagation (arXiv)
    Yihan Wang, Zhouxing Shi, Quanquan Gu, Cho-Jui Hsieh

    Analyses convergence of Interval Bound Propagation certified training, proving that for an overparameterized two-layer ReLU network with logistic loss, gradient descent converges linearly to zero robust training error with high probability given small perturbation radius and large width.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 28 Apr, 01:30–03:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 Probabilistic certification Adversarially Robust Conformal Prediction
    Asaf Gendler, Tsui-Wei Weng, Luca Daniel, Yaniv Romano

    Extends conformal prediction to adversarial inputs by smoothing the non-conformity score with Gaussian noise to bound its Lipschitz constant, giving prediction sets with finite-sample coverage under any l2-bounded attack; validated on CIFAR10, CIFAR100 and ImageNet.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 28 Apr, 09:30–11:30 (UTC)

  • ICLR 2022 spotlight 🎉 Probabilistic certification Boosting Randomized Smoothing with Variance Reduced Classifiers
    Miklós Z. Horváth, Mark Niklas Müller, Marc Fischer, Martin Vechev

    Uses ensembles as base models for randomized smoothing, since their lower variance under noise enlarges certified radii, and cuts sample complexity up to 55-fold; ensembles of 3 to 10 models reach average certified radius 0.86 on CIFAR10 and 1.11 on ImageNet.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 28 Apr, 17:30–19:30 (UTC)

ICRA 2022 2 papers

  • ICRA 2022 NN control systems Formal Verification of Stochastic Systems with ReLU Neural Network Controllers
    Shiqi Sun, Yan Zhang, Xusheng Luo, Panagiotis Vlantis, Miroslav Pajic, Michael M. Zavlanos

    Verifies safety of stochastic linear systems with Gaussian noise and ReLU network controllers by abstracting them as a graph, bounding transition probabilities with Satisfiability Modulo Convex formulas, and computing which initial states stay safe with given confidence over a horizon.

  • ICRA 2022 Certified trainingNN control systems ROMAX: Certifiably Robust Deep Multiagent Reinforcement Learning via Convex Relaxation
    Chuangchuang Sun, Dong-Ki Kim, Jonathan P. How

    ROMAX trains multiagent reinforcement learning policies robust to worst-case updates of other agents, solving the inner minimisation of a minimax objective with convex relaxation of neural networks, which also gives a certified bound; it beats prior methods on mixed cooperative-competitive tasks.

  • USENIX Security 2022 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties PatchCleanser: Certifiably Robust Defense against Adversarial Patches for Any Image Classifier (arXiv)
    Chong Xiang, Saeed Mahloujifar, Prateek Mittal

    PatchCleanser certifies any image classifier against adversarial patches through two rounds of pixel masking; on ImageNet it reaches 83.9% top-1 clean accuracy and 62.1% certified robust accuracy against a 2%-pixel square patch.
