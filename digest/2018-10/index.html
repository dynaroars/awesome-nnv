1 NNV paper accepted in October 2018, at POPL 2019.

  • POPL 2019 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation An abstract domain for certifying neural networks
    Gagandeep Singh, Timon Gehr, Markus Püschel, Martin Vechev

    DeepPoly certifies deep neural networks with an abstract domain combining floating-point polyhedra and intervals, with transformers for affine, ReLU, sigmoid, tanh and maxpool; more precise than prior work, and with trace partitioning it proves robustness to rotations.
