CVPR 2025 decisions: certification of human trajectory prediction, deterministic GNN certification against graph poisoning with PGNNCert, PatchDEMUX for multi-label patch defense, and tighter MaxPool verification with Ti-Lin.

4 NNV papers accepted in February 2025, at CVPR 2025.

CVPR 2025 · Nashville, United States 4 / 2,870 CVPR 2025 papers are NNV, by poster session

  • CVPR 2025 Beyond feed-forward netsOther Deterministic Certification of Graph Neural Networks against Graph Poisoning Attacks with Arbitrary Perturbations
    Jiate Li, Meng Pang, Yun Dong, Binghui Wang

    Certifies GNNs against training-time poisoning with PGNNCert, giving deterministic guarantees under arbitrary edge, node and node-feature perturbations for node and graph classification; it significantly outperforms prior certified defenses limited to edge or node perturbations.

    Nashville · Poster #464, ExHall D · Fri 13 Jun, 10:30–12:30 (Nashville time)

  • CVPR 2025 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties PatchDEMUX: A Certifiably Robust Framework for Multi-label Classifiers Against Adversarial Patches
    Dennis Jacob, Chong Xiang, Prateek Mittal

    Extends single-label certifiable patch defenses to multi-label classifiers with PatchDEMUX, treating the task as isolated binary problems, plus a tighter procedure for single-patch attackers; with PatchCleanser as backbone it achieves non-trivial robustness on MS-COCO and PASCAL VOC with high clean performance.

    Nashville · Poster #435, ExHall D · Fri 13 Jun, 16:00–18:00 (Nashville time)

  • CVPR 2025 highlight 🎉 Probabilistic certificationBeyond linear output properties Certified Human Trajectory Prediction
    Mohammadhossein Bahari, Saeed Saadatnejad, Amirhossein Askari-Farsangi, Seyed-Mohsen Moosavi-Dezfooli, Alexandre Alahi

    Certifies human trajectory prediction models against noisy or adversarial inputs, addressing unbounded and multi-modal outputs, with a diffusion-based trajectory denoiser to limit performance loss and new certified performance metrics; certified predictors show accuracy and robustness advantages over non-certified ones.

    Nashville · Poster #158, ExHall D · Sat 14 Jun, 10:30–12:30 (Nashville time)

  • CVPR 2025 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Tightening Robustness Verification of MaxPool-based Neural Networks via Minimizing the Over-Approximation Zone
    Yuan Xiao, Yuchen Chen, Shiqing Ma, Chunrong Fang, Tongtong Bai, Mingzheng Gu, Yuxin Cheng, Yanwei Chen, Zhenyu Chen

    Tightens robustness verification of MaxPool-based CNNs with Ti-Lin, giving provably neuron-wise tightest linear bounds for MaxPool; across LeNet, PointNet and networks on MNIST, CIFAR-10, Tiny ImageNet and ModelNet40 it improves certified accuracy by up to 78.6% at similar runtime.

    Nashville · Poster #465, ExHall D · Sat 14 Jun, 17:00–19:00 (Nashville time)
