AAAI 2026 decisions: neural control safety leads, with ReLU barrier certificate verification and falsification, LUCID and probabilistic policy verification via predicate abstraction. 14 NNV papers accepted in November 2025, at AAAI 2026 and EMNLP 2025. 1 of them is placed by conference date, as the notification date is not recorded.

AAAI 2026 13 papers

  • AAAI 2026 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties CertMask: Certifiable Defense Against Adversarial Patches via Theoretically Optimal Mask Coverage (arXiv)
    Xuntao Lyu, Ching-Chi Lin, Abdullah Al Arafat, Georg von der Brüggen, Jian-Jia Chen, Zhishan Guo

    CertMask certifies image classifiers against adversarial patches with a single round of masking whose mask set covers every patch location at least k times, cutting cost from O(n^2) to O(n); it raises certified robust accuracy by up to 13.4% over PatchCleanser.

  • AAAI 2026 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond ℓp input properties Certified L2-Norm Robustness of 3D Point Cloud Recognition in the Frequency Domain
    Liang Zhou, Qiming Wang, Tianze Chen

    FreqCert certifies 3D point cloud classifiers against global l2 perturbations by applying the graph Fourier transform, voting over spectrally partitioned sub-point clouds, and deriving a tight closed-form certified radius; it achieves higher certified accuracy on ModelNet40 and ScanObjectNN.

  • AAAI 2026 NN control systemsComplete verification Efficient Verification and Falsification of ReLU Neural Barrier Certificates (arXiv)
    Dejin Ren, Yiling Xue, Taoran Wu, Bai Xue

    States a necessary and sufficient condition for the correctness of ReLU neural barrier certificates for continuous-time systems, encodable as SMT or optimization, enabling both verification and, for the first time, falsification; numerical experiments confirm its validity and effectiveness.

  • AAAI 2026 Formal explanations Efficiently Computing Compact Formal Explanations
    Min Wu, Xiaofu Li, Haoze Wu, Clark Barrett

    VeriX+ shrinks and speeds up formal explanations for ML models using bound-propagation-based sensitivity, binary-search traversal with confidence ranking and an adapted QuickXplain; it cuts explanation size by 38% on GTSRB and time by 90% on MNIST, scaling to transformers.

  • AAAI 2026 Formal explanations Formal Abductive Latent Explanations for Prototype-Based Networks (arXiv)
    Jules Soria, Zakaria Chihani, Julien Girard-Satabin, Alban Grastien, Romain Xu-Darme, Daniela Cancila

    Abductive Latent Explanations give sufficient conditions on the latent representation of prototype-based case-based reasoning networks that imply the prediction, addressing misleading prototype explanations; a solver-free, scalable algorithm generates them for standard and fine-grained image classification.

  • AAAI 2026 Other HiQ-Lip: A Hierarchical Quantum-Classical Method for Global Lipschitz Constant Estimation of ReLU Networks (arXiv)
    Haoqi He, Yan Xiao, Wenzhi Xu, Ruoying Liu, Xiaokai Lin, Kai Wen

    HiQ-Lip estimates global Lipschitz constants of ReLU networks with a hybrid quantum-classical method, casting the problem as Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization with multilevel graph coarsening; on two-layer networks with 256 hidden neurons it doubles solving speed and gives tighter upper bounds than LiPopt.

  • AAAI 2026 NN control systemsProbabilistic certification LUCID: Learning-Enabled Uncertainty-Aware Certification of Stochastic Dynamical Systems (arXiv)
    Ernesto Casablanca, Oliver Schön, Paolo Zuliani, Sadegh Soudjani

    LUCID certifies safety of black-box stochastic dynamical systems from transition data by learning control barrier certificates in a Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Space with ambiguity sets, using a finite Fourier kernel expansion to turn a semi-infinite non-convex problem into a linear program.

  • AAAI 2026 NN control systemsProbabilistic certification Learning Vision-Based Neural Network Controllers with Semi-Probabilistic Safety Guarantees
    Xinhang Ma, Junlin Wu, Hussein Sibai, Yiannis Kantaros, Yevgeniy Vorobeychik

    Verifies and trains vision-based neural network controllers in a semi-probabilistic framework combining reachability analysis, conditional generative networks and distribution-free tail bounds, with a safety loss and curriculum learning; evaluated in X-Plane 11, CARLA, a physical F1Tenth setup and AirSim.

  • AAAI 2026 Probabilistic certificationBound propagation & abstract interpretation On the Probabilistic Learnability of Compact Neural Network Preimage Bounds
    Luca Marzari, Manuele Bicego, Ferdinando Cicalese, Alessandro Farinelli

    RF-ProVe approximates neural network preimages probabilistically, using an ensemble of randomized decision trees to propose input regions satisfying an output property and refining them by active resampling, with formal statistical guarantees on region purity and global coverage.

  • AAAI 2026 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond feed-forward nets Parameterized Abstract Interpretation for Transformer Verification
    Pei Huang, Dennis Wei, Omri Isac, Haoze Wu, Min Wu, Clark Barrett

    Proposes two parameterized linear abstract domains for inner products in transformer self-attention, one from symbolic quadratic bounds and tangents, one interpolating prior affine bounds; both verify robustness instances existing methods cannot and give tighter interval results, especially in deeper networks.

  • AAAI 2026 NN control systemsProbabilistic certification Probabilistic Safety Verification of Neural Policies via Predicate Abstraction
    Marcel Vinzent, Holger Hermanns, Jörg Hoffmann

    Extends policy predicate abstraction to probabilistic safety verification of neural action policies, adapting probabilistic CEGAR to neural policies; the algorithm decides whether a policy meets an unsafety-probability bound and derives upper and lower bounds where encodings into probabilistic model checkers prove ineffective.

  • AAAI 2026 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond feed-forward nets Tighter Truncated Rectangular Prism Approximation for RNN Robustness Verification (arXiv)
    Xingqi Lin, Liangyu Chen, Min Wu, Min Zhang, Zhenbing Zeng

    DeepPrism verifies RNN robustness by enclosing the Hadamard-product surface with a truncated rectangular prism of two linear planes, refined to minimize volume and surface area; it improves on existing approaches in image classification, speech recognition and sentiment analysis tasks.

  • AAAI 2026 Global & relational properties VeriFlow: Modeling Distributions for Neural Network Verification (arXiv)
    Faried Abu Zaid, Daniel Neider, Mustafa Yalçiner

    VeriFlow is a flow-based density model with a piecewise-affine transformation, letting linear-arithmetic verifiers restrict global properties such as fairness or robustness to a data distribution, since upper density level sets become linear constraints in the latent space.
