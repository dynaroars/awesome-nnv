January 2024

ICLR 2024 and FSE 2024 decisions: randomized smoothing and certified training lead, with conformal prediction certificates (COLEP, RSCP+), dual and incremental smoothing, and studies of IBP certified training and expressive losses. SDP-based Lipschitz estimation recurs, and FSE adds VeriStable for DPLL-based verification and CrossCert for patch robustness certification.

18 NNV papers accepted in January 2024, at FSE 2024 and ICLR 2024.

ICLR 2024 · Vienna, Austria 16 / 2,252 ICLR 2024 papers are NNV, by poster session

  • ICLR 2024 Certified training Expressive Losses for Verified Robustness via Convex Combinations (arXiv)
    Alessandro De Palma, Rudy Bunel, Krishnamurthy Dvijotham, M. Pawan Kumar, Robert Stanforth, Alessio Lomuscio

    Argues that loss expressivity, spanning lower and upper bounds of the worst-case loss through one coefficient, is key to verified training, showing simple convex combinations of adversarial attacks and IBP bounds reach state-of-the-art verified robustness across settings.

    Vienna · Poster #114, Halle B · Tue 7 May, 10:45–12:45 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Certified trainingBeyond linear output properties LipSim: A Provably Robust Perceptual Similarity Metric (arXiv)
    Sara Ghazanfari, Alexandre Araujo, Prashanth Krishnamurthy, Farshad Khorrami, Siddharth Garg

    LipSim is a perceptual similarity metric built on 1-Lipschitz networks that certifies its output against all l2-bounded perturbations, after showing ViT ensemble-based metrics are vulnerable to attacks; evaluated on natural and certified scores and image retrieval.

    Vienna · Poster #231, Halle B · Tue 7 May, 10:45–12:45 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Probabilistic certification Mitigating the Curse of Dimensionality for Certified Robustness via Dual Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Song Xia, Yi Yu, Xudong Jiang, Henghui Ding

    Dual Randomized Smoothing down-samples an image into two sub-images and smooths each in lower dimension, proving a tight l2 certified radius for the original input that shrinks more slowly with dimension; improves accuracy and certified robustness on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet.

    Vienna · Poster #118, Halle B · Tue 7 May, 10:45–12:45 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationCertified training Expressivity of ReLU-Networks under Convex Relaxations (arXiv)
    Maximilian Baader, Mark Niklas Müller, Yuhao Mao, Martin Vechev

    Studies how expressive ReLU networks are under common convex relaxations, showing more precise relaxations can exactly analyze more univariate functions, yet no single-neuron relaxation precisely analyzes ReLU networks for multivariate convex monotone piecewise-linear functions.

    Vienna · Poster #229, Halle B · Tue 7 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Certified training A Recipe for Improved Certifiable Robustness (arXiv)
    Kai Hu, Klas Leino, Zifan Wang, Matt Fredrikson

    Improves Lipschitz-based deterministic certification by exploring the design space, adding large Cholesky-orthogonalized residual dense layers to Lipschitz-controlled ResNets and filtered generative data augmentation, raising the best deterministic VRA by up to 8.5 percentage points.

    Vienna · Poster #159, Halle B · Wed 8 May, 10:45–12:45 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets COLEP: Certifiably Robust Learning-Reasoning Conformal Prediction via Probabilistic Circuits (arXiv)
    Mintong Kang, Nezihe Merve Gürel, Linyi Li, Bo Li

    COLEP certifies the coverage of conformal prediction under bounded adversarial perturbations by combining learned semantic models with reasoning via probabilistic circuits; certified coverage improves by up to 12% on GTSRB, 9% on CIFAR-10 and 14% on AwA2.

    Vienna · Poster #269, Halle B · Wed 8 May, 10:45–12:45 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets Certified Adversarial Robustness for Rate Encoded Spiking Neural Networks
    Bhaskar Mukhoty, Hilal AlQuabeh, Giulia De Masi, Huan Xiong, Bin Gu

    Gives the first provable l1-norm robustness guarantee for rate-encoded spiking neural networks by linking rate encoding to randomized smoothing, and adds adversarial training that improves empirical robust accuracy on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 and ImageNet-100.

    Vienna · Poster #129, Halle B · Wed 8 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Certified training Understanding Certified Training with Interval Bound Propagation (arXiv)
    Yuhao Mao, Mark Niklas Müller, Marc Fischer, Martin Vechev

    Studies why interval bound propagation (IBP) certified training works, using a new bound-tightness metric; derives conditions for exact IBP bounds that explain the robustness-accuracy trade-off, finds wider ReLU networks perform better, and shows tightness is neither necessary nor sufficient for certifiable robustness.

    Vienna · Poster #223, Halle B · Wed 8 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond ℓp input properties DRSM: De-Randomized Smoothing on Malware Classifier Providing Certified Robustness (arXiv)
    Shoumik Saha, Wenxiao Wang, Yigitcan Kaya, Soheil Feizi, Tudor Dumitras

    DRSM certifies malware detectors against contiguous adversarial bytes by adapting de-randomized smoothing to MalConv with a window ablation scheme, at a marginal accuracy cost, and releases PACE, a public dataset of 15.5K benign executables.

    Vienna · Poster #113, Halle B · Thu 9 May, 10:45–12:45 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Other Novel Quadratic Constraints for Extending LipSDP beyond Slope-Restricted Activations (arXiv)
    Patricia Pauli, Aaron J. Havens, Alexandre Araujo, Siddharth Garg, Farshad Khorrami, Frank Allgöwer, Bin Hu

    Extends LipSDP to GroupSort, MaxMin and Householder activations with new quadratic constraints, giving a unified SDP for l2 and l-infinity Lipschitz bounds of residual, non-residual and implicit networks that is less conservative than existing approaches.

    Vienna · Poster #118, Halle B · Thu 9 May, 10:45–12:45 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 oral 🎉 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond ℓp input properties GNNCert: Deterministic Certification of Graph Neural Networks against Adversarial Perturbations
    Zaishuo Xia, Han Yang, Binghui Wang, Jinyuan Jia

    GNNCert deterministically certifies graph classifiers against bounded perturbations to both graph structure and node features, guaranteeing the same label when perturbed edges and feature-perturbed nodes are limited; on 8 benchmark datasets it outperforms three prior methods.

    Vienna · Poster #216, Halle B · Thu 9 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time) Vienna · Oral, Halle A 7 · Thu 9 May, 15:45–16:00 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Probabilistic certification Incremental Randomized Smoothing Certification (arXiv)
    Shubham Ugare, Tarun Suresh, Debangshu Banerjee, Gagandeep Singh, Sasa Misailovic

    IRS incrementally certifies modified models, such as quantized or pruned versions, under randomized smoothing by reusing the original smoothed model's guarantees with very few samples, achieving up to 3x speedup over recertifying from scratch.

    Vienna · Poster #115, Halle B · Thu 9 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation On the Scalability and Memory Efficiency of Semidefinite Programs for Lipschitz Constant Estimation of Neural Networks
    Zi Wang, Bin Hu, Aaron J. Havens, Alexandre Araujo, Yang Zheng, Yudong Chen, Somesh Jha

    Recasts SDP-based Lipschitz constant estimation as an eigenvalue optimization solvable by first-order methods in autodiff frameworks, via redundant quadratic constraints and Lagrangian and Shor relaxations, using far less memory and scaling to large ImageNet networks.

    Vienna · Poster #144, Halle B · Thu 9 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Probabilistic certification Provably Robust Conformal Prediction with Improved Efficiency (arXiv)
    Ge Yan, Yaniv Romano, Tsui-Wei Weng

    Fixes the flawed guarantee of randomized smoothed conformal prediction with RSCP+, certifying coverage of conformal prediction sets under adversarial noise, and adds Post-Training Transformation and Robust Conformal Training to shrink sets, improving efficiency up to 16.9x on ImageNet.

    Vienna · Poster #117, Halle B · Thu 9 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 OtherFormal explanations Interpreting Robustness Proofs of Deep Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Debangshu Banerjee, Avaljot Singh, Gagandeep Singh

    Generates human-understandable interpretations of DNN robustness proofs, revealing that proofs for standard networks rely on spurious features, provably robust training filters out even meaningful features, and combined adversarial and provable training best balances the two.

    Vienna · Poster #200, Halle B · Fri 10 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

  • ICLR 2024 Probabilistic certificationCertified training The Lipschitz-Variance-Margin Tradeoff for Enhanced Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Blaise Delattre, Alexandre Araujo, Quentin Barthélemy, Alexandre Allauzen

    Improves randomized smoothing certificates by analysing how Monte-Carlo variance interacts with the base classifier's Lipschitz constant and margin, using a new logit-to-probability mapping, Bernstein's inequality and tighter Lipschitz bounds; raises certified accuracy and works zero-shot on pre-trained models.

    Vienna · Poster #104, Halle B · Fri 10 May, 16:30–18:30 (Vienna time)

FSE 2024 2 papers

  • FSE 2024 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties CrossCert: A Cross-Checking Detection Approach to Patch Robustness Certification for Deep Learning Models
    Qilin Zhou, Zhengyuan Wei, Haipeng Wang, Bo Jiang, Wing-Kwong Chan

    CrossCert cross-checks two certified recovery defenders against adversarial patches to give unwavering certification, guaranteeing a benign label without warnings under patching, with detection certification comparable to PatchCensor and slightly below ViP.

  • FSE 2024 Complete verification Harnessing Neuron Stability to Improve DNN Verification
    Hai Duong, Dong Xu, ThanhVu Nguyen, Matthew B. Dwyer

    VeriStable extends DPLL-based DNN verification by detecting neurons that become stable during search, cutting combinatorial cost, and adds multi-threading and restarts from SAT solvers; on MNIST and CIFAR FNNs, CNNs and ResNets it outperforms α-β-CROWN and MN-BaB.
