September 2020

NeurIPS 2020 decisions: randomized smoothing is prominent, from denoised smoothing for pretrained classifiers to patch, confidence and higher-order certificates. Bound propagation advances with auto_LiRPA, tightened single-neuron relaxations and memory-efficient SDP, alongside exact local Lipschitz computation. Certified monotonicity, including COMET, graph convolution networks, binarized neural networks with EEV and Revel's verified reinforcement-learning exploration broaden the scope.

25 NNV papers accepted in September 2020, at NeurIPS 2020.

  • NeurIPS 2020 Probabilistic certificationBeyond ℓp input properties (De)Randomized Smoothing for Certifiable Defense against Patch Attacks (arXiv)
    Alexander Levine, Soheil Feizi

    Certifies image classifiers against adversarial patches with a de-randomized smoothing scheme that yields deterministic certificates and trains faster than interval-bound methods; for 5x5 patches on CIFAR-10 it reaches about 57.6% certified accuracy versus at most 30.3% before, and scales to ImageNet.

  • NeurIPS 2020 Complete verification Certifying Strategyproof Auction Networks (arXiv)
    Michael J. Curry, Ping-Yeh Chiang, Tom Goldstein, John Dickerson

    Verifies strategyproofness of RegretNet learned auction mechanisms under a given valuation profile with neural network verification techniques, modifying the architecture so it can be encoded exactly as an integer program, and produces certificates even where optimal mechanisms are unknown.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Global & relational propertiesComplete verification Counterexample-Guided Learning of Monotonic Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Aishwarya Sivaraman, Golnoosh Farnadi, Todd D. Millstein, Guy Van den Broeck

    COMET provably enforces monotonicity constraints on general ReLU networks at prediction time through counterexample-guided techniques, and uses monotonicity counterexamples as an inductive bias during training; on real-world datasets it outperforms existing monotonic learners.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Probabilistic certification Denoised Smoothing: A Provable Defense for Pretrained Classifiers (arXiv)
    Hadi Salman, Mingjie Sun, Greg Yang, Ashish Kapoor, Zico Kolter

    Denoised smoothing makes any pretrained image classifier provably ℓp-robust by prepending a custom-trained denoiser and applying randomized smoothing, without modifying the classifier, in white-box and black-box settings; demonstrated on ImageNet, CIFAR-10 and the Azure, Google, AWS and ClarifAI APIs.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Enabling certification of verification-agnostic networks via memory-efficient semidefinite programming (arXiv)
    Sumanth Dathathri, Krishnamurthy Dvijotham, Alexey Kurakin, Aditi Raghunathan, Jonathan Uesato, Rudy Bunel, Shreya Shankar, Jacob Steinhardt, Ian J. Goodfellow, Percy Liang, Pushmeet Kohli

    A first-order dual SDP algorithm with memory linear in network activations, using iterative eigenvector methods via forward/backward passes on GPUs/TPUs, verifies networks not trained for verification; verified ℓ∞ accuracy rises from 1% to 88% on MNIST and 6% to 40% on CIFAR-10.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationComplete verification Exactly Computing the Local Lipschitz Constant of ReLU Networks (arXiv)
    Matt Jordan, Alex Dimakis

    Relates the local Lipschitz constant of nonsmooth functions to the generalized Jacobian, proves strong inapproximability for ReLU networks, and gives an algorithm computing the local Lipschitz constant exactly, used to assess the tightness of other Lipschitz estimators and the effects of regularized training.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Probabilistic certification Higher-Order Certification For Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Jeet Mohapatra, Ching-Yun Ko, Tsui-Wei Weng, Pin-Yu Chen, Sijia Liu, Luca Daniel

    Extends randomized smoothing certification by framing radius computation as nested optimization and using first-order and higher-order information of Gaussian-smoothed classifiers, certifying larger safety regions with gains on ℓ1 radii and color-space ℓ2 radii on CIFAR10 and ImageNet.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 NN control systems Neurosymbolic Reinforcement Learning with Formally Verified Exploration (arXiv)
    Greg Anderson, Abhinav Verma, Isil Dillig, Swarat Chaudhuri

    Revel enables provably safe exploration in reinforcement learning by mirror descent between a neurosymbolic policy class and a verifiable symbolic class, avoiding repeated verification of neural networks; it enforces safe exploration where Constrained Policy Optimization fails and finds better policies.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation The Convex Relaxation Barrier, Revisited: Tightened Single-Neuron Relaxations for Neural Network Verification (arXiv)
    Christian Tjandraatmadja, Ross Anderson, Joey Huchette, Will Ma, Krunal Patel, Juan Pablo Vielma

    Derives the tightest convex relaxation of a ReLU over a box-bounded multivariate pre-activation input, separable in linear time, and builds LP-based and fast propagation verifiers on it that verify significantly more instances for a modest extra cost.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 8 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Probabilistic certification Black-Box Certification with Randomized Smoothing: A Functional Optimization Based Framework (arXiv)
    Dinghuai Zhang, Mao Ye, Chengyue Gong, Zhanxing Zhu, Qiang Liu

    Casts randomized smoothing certification as functional optimization, allowing non-Gaussian smoothing distributions and attacks beyond ℓ2, including ℓ1 and ℓ∞; the framework exposes an accuracy-robustness trade-off, and its new distributions certify better than previous works.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 9 Dec, 05:00–07:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Global & relational propertiesComplete verification Certified Monotonic Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Xingchao Liu, Xing Han, Na Zhang, Qiang Liu

    Certifies monotonicity of general piecewise-linear neural networks with respect to chosen features by solving a mixed integer linear program, enabling training with increasing monotonicity regularization until certified; it is more accurate and efficient than methods like Deep Lattice Networks.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 9 Dec, 05:00–07:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Probabilistic certificationCertified training Consistency Regularization for Certified Robustness of Smoothed Classifiers (arXiv)
    Jongheon Jeong, Jinwoo Shin

    Improves certified ℓ2 robustness of randomized-smoothing classifiers by regularizing prediction consistency over Gaussian noise during training, giving results better than or comparable to prior methods across architectures and datasets with less training cost and fewer hyperparameters.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 9 Dec, 05:00–07:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Quantized & bit-levelComplete verification Efficient Exact Verification of Binarized Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Kai Jia, Martin C. Rinard

    EEV performs exact verification of binarized neural networks with a SAT solver that natively handles reified cardinality constraints, plus training for solver-friendly BNNs; it gives exact ℓ∞ robustness results on convolutional BNNs for MNIST and CIFAR10, hundreds to thousands of times faster than real-valued verification.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 9 Dec, 05:00–07:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Certified training Lipschitz-Certifiable Training with a Tight Outer Bound
    Sungyoon Lee, Jaewook Lee, Saerom Park

    Speeds up certifiable training with box constraint propagation (BCP), combining Lipschitz analysis and interval arithmetic for a tighter outer bound; it trains over 12 times faster than a dual relaxation method with comparable verification and reaches 20.1% verified accuracy on Tiny ImageNet (ℓ2, 36/255).

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 9 Dec, 05:00–07:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation An efficient nonconvex reformulation of stagewise convex optimization problems (arXiv)
    Rudy Bunel, Oliver Hinder, Srinadh Bhojanapalli, Krishnamurthy Dvijotham

    Reformulates stagewise convex problems, including neural network verification, as nonconvex problems with simple bound constraints solved by a modified projected gradient method that reaches the global optimum; it yields small duality gaps quickly and outpaces off-the-shelf and specialized verification solvers.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 9 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Global & relational propertiesCertified training Learning Certified Individually Fair Representations
    Anian Ruoss, Mislav Balunovic, Marc Fischer, Martin Vechev

    Learns representations certified for individual fairness: similar individuals are mapped within a certified ℓ∞ distance in latent space, so data consumers can certify fairness by proving ε-robustness of their classifier; evaluated on five real-world datasets with several fairness constraints.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 9 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationOther Automatic Perturbation Analysis for Scalable Certified Robustness and Beyond (arXiv)
    Kaidi Xu, Zhouxing Shi, Huan Zhang, Yihan Wang, Kai-Wei Chang, Minlie Huang, Bhavya Kailkhura, Xue Lin, Cho-Jui Hsieh

    Generalizes LiRPA bounds such as CROWN to arbitrary computational graphs in an automatic, differentiable framework with loss fusion, enabling certified defense on DenseNet, ResNeXt and Transformer and, for the first time, on Tiny ImageNet and Downscaled ImageNet; released as the auto_LiRPA library.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 10 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Certified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond linear output properties Certifiably Adversarially Robust Detection of Out-of-Distribution Data (arXiv)
    Julian Bitterwolf, Alexander Meinke, Matthias Hein

    Certifies worst-case out-of-distribution detection by using interval bound propagation to upper-bound classifier confidence over an ℓ∞-ball around OOD points and minimizing that bound in training; the guarantees generalize beyond the training OOD data with little accuracy loss.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 10 Dec, 17:00–19:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond ℓp input properties Certified Robustness of Graph Convolution Networks for Graph Classification under Topological Attacks

  • NeurIPS 2020 Probabilistic certificationBeyond linear output properties Certifying Confidence via Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)

  • NeurIPS 2020 Certified training Lipschitz Bounds and Provably Robust Training by Laplacian Smoothing (arXiv)
    Vishaal Krishnan, Abed AlRahman Al Makdah, Fabio Pasqualetti

    Trains models with provable adversarial robustness by framing learning as Lipschitz-constrained loss minimization whose Lagrangian saddle point solves a weighted Laplacian Poisson equation, solved by graph discretization and primal-dual updates; also shows a fundamental robustness-accuracy lower bound.

  • NeurIPS 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation On the Tightness of Semidefinite Relaxations for Certifying Robustness to Adversarial Examples (arXiv)
    Richard Y. Zhang

    Gives a geometric test of when the SDP relaxation certificate for ReLU network robustness is exact, viewing it as projecting a point onto a hyperbola; the certificate is proven exact for one hidden layer under mild assumptions and explained as usually conservative for several layers.

    Estimates global and local Lipschitz constants of multi-layer ReLU networks with a semidefinite programming hierarchy combining polynomial lifting of ReLU derivatives with a weak Putinar positivity certificate, sometimes giving better bounds in less time than linear programming approaches.
