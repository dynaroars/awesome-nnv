June 2025

OOPSLA 2025 and IROS 2025 decisions: tighter activation convex hull approximation with WraAct, mini-batch robustness verification with BaVerLy, and quantization guaranteed to match floating-point classifications with CoMPAQt. Certified individual fairness through training and the feasibility of real-time randomized smoothing certification round out the month.

5 NNV papers accepted in June 2025, at IROS 2025 and OOPSLA 2025.

OOPSLA 2025 4 papers

  • OOPSLA 2025 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Convex Hull Approximation for Activation Functions
    Zhongkui Ma, Zihan Wang, Guangdong Bai

    Builds tight over-approximations of activation function convex hulls with WraAct, which smooths functions using double-linear-piece functions; versus SBLM+PDDM it is on average 400X faster and 150X more precise, and within a verifier it beats PRIMA by up to 20 verified samples.

  • OOPSLA 2025 Certified trainingGlobal & relational properties Correct-by-Construction: Certified Individual Fairness through Neural Network Training
    Ruihan Zhang, Jun Sun

    Guarantees individual fairness throughout neural network training via provably fair initialization and a fairness-preserving training algorithm using randomized response mechanisms to protect sensitive attributes; resulting models are fair and accurate, and training is much faster than certified training that runs verification.

  • OOPSLA 2025 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Mini-Batch Robustness Verification of Deep Neural Networks
    Saar Tzour-Shaday, Dana Drachsler-Cohen

    Verifies local robustness of many ϵ-balls jointly with BaVerLy, a sound and complete verifier that groups balls with similar network computations into adaptively sized mini-batches and refines on failure; on MNIST and CIFAR-10 networks it is 2.3x faster on average, up to 4.1x.

  • OOPSLA 2025 Quantized & bit-level Quantization with Guaranteed Floating-Point Neural Network Classifications
    Anan Kabaha, Dana Drachsler-Cohen

    Guarantees quantized networks match floating-point classifications using CoMPAQt, a MILP encoding with custom relaxations that bounds the confidence of inconsistent inputs and drives correction mechanisms; one guarantees 100% consistency while cutting computational cost 3.8x on average, another reaches near-perfect consistency at 4.1x.

IROS 2025 1 paper

  • IROS 2025 Probabilistic certification Feasibility Analysis of real-time Robustness Certification
    Emmanouil Seferis, Stefanos D. Kollias

    Studies how cutting the number of randomized smoothing samples by orders of magnitude affects certified radius, finding only modest loss in average certified radius that off-the-shelf improvements can offset, enabling real-time robustness certification on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet.
