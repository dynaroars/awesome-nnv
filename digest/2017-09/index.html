September 2017

NeurIPS 2017 decisions: instance-specific formal lower bounds on adversarial robustness with Cross-Lipschitz regularization, and safe model-based reinforcement learning that learns neural network control policies with Lyapunov stability certificates.

2 NNV papers accepted in September 2017, at NeurIPS 2017.

  • NeurIPS 2017 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationCertified training Formal Guarantees on the Robustness of a Classifier against Adversarial Manipulation (arXiv)
    Matthias Hein, Maksym Andriushchenko

    Gives the first formal instance-specific lower bounds on the input change needed to alter a classifier's decision, and proposes Cross-Lipschitz regularization that improves robustness of kernel methods and neural networks without loss in prediction performance.

  • NeurIPS 2017 NN control systems Safe Model-based Reinforcement Learning with Stability Guarantees (arXiv)
    Felix Berkenkamp, Matteo Turchetta, Angela P. Schoellig, Andreas Krause

    Learns neural network control policies with provable Lyapunov stability certificates using statistical dynamics models with a Gaussian process prior, safely expanding the safe region; optimizes a policy on a simulated inverted pendulum without it ever falling.
