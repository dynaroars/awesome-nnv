September 2021

NeurIPS 2021 and POPL 2022 decisions: certified training and bound propagation lead, with Beta-CROWN, fast IBP training and local Lipschitz bounds. POPL contributed PRIMA multi-neuron abstractions, DeepJ for Clarke Jacobians and interval universal approximation. Other directions include center smoothing for structured outputs, SmoothMix, convex relaxations for simplex inputs and safety of Bayesian neural network policies.

16 NNV papers accepted in September 2021, at NeurIPS 2021 and POPL 2022.

NeurIPS 2021 · Virtual 13 / 2,334 NeurIPS 2021 papers are NNV, by poster session

  • NeurIPS 2021 Beyond linear output propertiesProbabilistic certification Center Smoothing: Certified Robustness for Networks with Structured Outputs (arXiv)
    Aounon Kumar, Tom Goldstein

    Center smoothing extends randomized smoothing to structured outputs such as sets, images and text, certifying that the output change, measured by a distance metric, stays small under any norm-bounded input perturbation, without much loss in base model performance.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 7 Dec, 16:30–18:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Certified training Fast Certified Robust Training with Short Warmup (arXiv)
    Zhouxing Shi, Yihan Wang, Huan Zhang, Jinfeng Yi, Cho-Jui Hsieh

    Speeds up IBP certified training with a new weight initialization, full batch normalization and regularization against exploded bounds and ReLU imbalance, reaching 65.03% verified error on CIFAR-10 and 82.36% on TinyImageNet in 160 and 80 epochs.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 7 Dec, 16:30–18:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Overcoming the Convex Barrier for Simplex Inputs
    Harkirat Singh Behl, M. Pawan Kumar, Philip Torr, Krishnamurthy Dvijotham

    Designs a tight convex relaxation for verifying robustness to perturbations within the probability simplex that stays linear in neuron count, improving l1 verified accuracy on CIFAR-10 and MNIST by up to 14.4% and verifying a multi-modal text-image classifier.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 7 Dec, 16:30–18:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Probabilistic certificationCertified training SmoothMix: Training Confidence-calibrated Smoothed Classifiers for Certified Robustness (arXiv)
    Jongheon Jeong, Sejun Park, Minkyu Kim, Heung-Chang Lee, Do-Guk Kim, Jinwoo Shin

    SmoothMix trains smoothed classifiers on self-mixup combinations of samples along adversarial directions, calibrating confidence of over-confident near off-class samples, and markedly improves certified l2 robustness of randomized smoothing over existing robust training methods.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 7 Dec, 16:30–18:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Certified training Relaxing Local Robustness (arXiv)
    Klas Leino, Matt Fredrikson

    Introduces relaxed top-k robustness and affinity robustness, properties that allow some label sets to be close, and builds models that can be efficiently certified for them, giving lower rejection rates and higher certified accuracies than standard local robustness.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 8 Dec, 00:30–02:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Certified training Training Certifiably Robust Neural Networks with Efficient Local Lipschitz Bounds (arXiv)
    Yujia Huang, Huan Zhang, Yuanyuan Shi, Zico Kolter, Anima Anandkumar

    Trains certifiably robust networks with a tighter, trainable local Lipschitz bound that removes rows and columns for activations constant near each input, plus clipped activations with learnable thresholds; it improves clean and certified accuracy on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and TinyImageNet.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 8 Dec, 00:30–02:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Certified training Towards Better Understanding of Training Certifiably Robust Models against Adversarial Examples
    Sungyoon Lee, Woojin Lee, Jinseong Park, Jaewook Lee

    Identifies smoothness of the loss landscape, besides bound tightness, as a key factor in certifiable training with linear relaxations, and designs a training method with both properties that performs well across a wide range of perturbation sizes.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 8 Dec, 08:30–10:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Complete verification Partition-Based Formulations for Mixed-Integer Optimization of Trained ReLU Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Calvin Tsay, Jan Kronqvist, Alexander Thebelt, Ruth Misener

    Partition-based mixed-integer formulations of trained ReLU networks group node inputs and take convex hulls over partitions via disjunctive programming, trading size against tightness; the smaller relaxations outperform existing formulations in experiments.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 9 Dec, 08:30–10:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties ScaleCert: Scalable Certified Defense against Adversarial Patches with Sparse Superficial Layers (arXiv)
    Husheng Han, Kaidi Xu, Xing Hu, Xiaobing Chen, Ling Liang, Zidong Du, Qi Guo, Yanzhi Wang, Yunji Chen

    ScaleCert certifies defenses against adversarial patches on high-resolution images by using superficial important neurons for network compression, cutting adversarial region search and prediction noise; certified accuracy on ImageNet rises from 36.3% to 60.4%.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 9 Dec, 08:30–10:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond feed-forward nets Semialgebraic Representation of Monotone Deep Equilibrium Models and Applications to Certification (arXiv)
    Tong Chen, Jean B. Lasserre, Victor Magron, Edouard Pauwels

    Gives a semialgebraic representation of ReLU monotone deep equilibrium models so semidefinite programming can certify robustness under general Lq norms, estimate Lipschitz constants and propagate ellipsoidal uncertainty, outperforming existing monDEQ certification.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 9 Dec, 08:30–10:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 Complete verificationBound propagation & abstract interpretation Beta-CROWN: Efficient Bound Propagation with Per-neuron Split Constraints for Neural Network Robustness Verification
    Shiqi Wang, Huan Zhang, Kaidi Xu, Xue Lin, Suman Jana, Cho-Jui Hsieh, Zico Kolter

    Encodes branch-and-bound neuron split constraints in bound propagation via optimizable β parameters, giving tighter bounds than LP verifiers at CROWN's GPU efficiency; with BaB it is up to three orders of magnitude faster than LP-based BaB, beats SDP verifiers with far less time, and powers α,β-CROWN, winner of VNN-COMP 2021.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 10 Dec, 16:30–18:00 (UTC)

  • NeurIPS 2021 NN control systems Infinite Time Horizon Safety of Bayesian Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Mathias Lechner, Dorde Zikelic, Krishnendu Chatterjee, Thomas A. Henzinger

    Verifies infinite-horizon safety of Bayesian neural network policies by training a separate deterministic certificate network, computing a safe weight set and rejecting posterior samples outside it; also extended to safe exploration in reinforcement learning.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 10 Dec, 16:30–18:00 (UTC)

POPL 2022 3 papers

  • POPL 2022 Other A dual number abstraction for static analysis of Clarke Jacobians
    Jacob Laurel, Rem Yang, Gagandeep Singh, Sasa Misailovic

    DeepJ soundly bounds the Clarke Jacobian of Lipschitz, possibly non-differentiable functions with an abstract domain over dual numbers and forward-mode dual interval evaluation, analysing Lipschitz robustness of networks under rotation, contrast, haze and composed perturbations.

  • POPL 2022 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Interval universal approximation for neural networks
    Zi Wang, Aws Albarghouthi, Gautam Prakriya, Somesh Jha

    Proves an interval universal approximation theorem for networks with squashable activations, so provably robust l-infinity networks exist, but shows the needed range approximation problem is Delta-2-intermediate, so building such networks efficiently is inherently hard.

  • POPL 2022 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation PRIMA: general and precise neural network certification via scalable convex hull approximations
    Mark Niklas Müller, Gleb Makarchuk, Gagandeep Singh, Markus Püschel, Martin Vechev

    PRIMA verifies neural networks with any non-linear activation using multi-neuron convex abstractions from polynomial convex hull approximation algorithms; it verifies up to 20%, 30% and 34% more images on ReLU, Sigmoid and Tanh networks.
