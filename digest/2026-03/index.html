PLDI 2026 and S&P 2026 decisions: SAIL uses LLMs to synthesize sound abstract transformers for neural network verification, and EnCP gives conformal prediction certified robustness against data poisoning.

2 NNV papers accepted in March 2026, at PLDI 2026 and S&P 2026.

PLDI 2026 1 paper

  • PLDI 2026 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation SAIL: Sound Abstract Interpreters with LLMs
    Qiuhan Gu, Avaljot Singh, Gagandeep Singh

    Sail uses LLMs to synthesize globally sound abstract transformers for neural network verification, guided by a cost function measuring unsoundness plus syntactic and semantic validation; it matches hand-designed transformers and produces sound, precise ones for complex non-linear operators absent from the literature.

S&P 2026 1 paper

  • S&P 2026 Other Ensemble Conformal Predictor (EnCP): A New Conformal Predictor with Robustness Guarantees Against Data Poisoning Attacks
    Yuxin Yang, Qiang Li, Runyang Feng, Liren Shan, Binghui Wang

    EnCP is an ensemble-based conformal predictor with provable robustness to data poisoning, bounding the influence of poisoned samples to derive certified coverage and certified prediction-set size; on image classification and LLM toxicity classification it keeps high coverage and compact sets under attack.
