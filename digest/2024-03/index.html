March 2024

CAV 2024 and S&P 2024 decisions: tools and control stand out, with Marabou 2.0, VELM's reachability-based safe exploration and neural barrier certificates for DNN-controlled systems. Other directions are few-pixel verification via covering designs, global two-safety properties, a Bayes-error bound on certified robust accuracy, and certified defenses for few-shot classification (FCert) and graph injection attacks.

8 NNV papers accepted in March 2024, at CAV 2024 and S&P 2024.

CAV 2024 6 papers

  • CAV 2024 Other Boosting Few-Pixel Robustness Verification via Covering Verification Designs
    Yuval Shapira, Naor Wiesel, Shahar Shabelman, Dana Drachsler-Cohen

    Speeds up L0 few-pixel robustness verification with covering verification designs, choosing among candidate coverings by predicting their block size distribution in closed form and building them on the fly in parallel; verification time drops by up to 5.1x on average.

  • CAV 2024 Other Certified Robust Accuracy of Neural Networks Are Bounded Due to Bayes Errors
    Ruihan Zhang, Jun Sun

    Derives an upper bound on certified robust accuracy from Bayes error under data distribution uncertainty, explaining why certified training lowers accuracy; for CIFAR10 the bound is 67.49%, while existing methods reached 62.84% by 2023.

  • CAV 2024 Complete verificationOther Marabou 2.0: A Versatile Formal Analyzer of Neural Networks
    Haoze Wu, Omri Isac, Aleksandar Zeljic, Teruhiro Tagomori, Matthew L. Daggitt, Wen Kokke, Idan Refaeli, Guy Amir, Kyle Julian, Shahaf Bassan, Pei Huang, Ori Lahav, Min Wu, Min Zhang, Ekaterina Komendantskaya, Guy Katz, Clark Barrett

    System description of version 2.0 of Marabou, a framework for formal analysis of neural networks, covering its architectural design and the main features and components added since the first release.

  • CAV 2024 NN control systems Safe Exploration in Reinforcement Learning by Reachability Analysis over Learned Models
    Yuning Wang, He Zhu

    VELM keeps reinforcement learning exploration safe by learning symbolic environment models, running formal reachability analysis on them, and shielding the agent to states verified safe; it cuts safety violations compared with other safe learning methods without lowering reward.

  • CAV 2024 NN control systemsProbabilistic certification Unifying Qualitative and Quantitative Safety Verification of DNN-Controlled Systems
    Dapeng Zhi, Peixin Wang, Si Liu, C.-H. Luke Ong, Min Zhang

    Verifies DNN-controlled systems by synthesizing neural barrier certificates, first for almost-sure safety and, if that fails, for lower and upper bounds on probabilistic safety over finite and infinite horizons, using k-inductive certificates and simulation-guided training on four systems.

  • CAV 2024 Global & relational properties Verifying Global Two-Safety Properties in Neural Networks with Confidence
    Anagha Athavale, Ezio Bartocci, Maria Christakis, Matteo Maffei, Dejan Nickovic, Georg Weissenbacher

    Verifies confidence-based 2-safety properties of DNNs, such as global robustness and global fairness, by self-composition with a new verifiable abstraction of softmax, proven sound and implemented on top of Marabou with evaluation on public benchmarks.

S&P 2024 2 papers

  • S&P 2024 Other FCert: Certifiably Robust Few-Shot Classification in the Era of Foundation Models
    Yanting Wang, Wei Zou, Jinyuan Jia

    FCert is a certified defense against data poisoning of few-shot classifiers built on foundation models, provably keeping the predicted label when the number of poisoned support samples is bounded; it outperforms existing certified poisoning defenses on vision and text benchmarks.

  • S&P 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond ℓp input properties Node-aware Bi-smoothing: Certified Robustness against Graph Injection Attacks
    Yuni Lai, Yulin Zhu, Bailin Pan, Kai Zhou

    Node-aware bi-smoothing certifies node classification against graph injection attacks with a model-agnostic smoothing scheme covering both evasion and poisoning; it derives certifiable conditions, extends two existing certification frameworks to node injection for comparison, and is also applied to recommendation systems.
