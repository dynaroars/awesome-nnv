    Samuel Pfrommer, Brendon G. Anderson, Julien Piet, Somayeh Sojoudi

    Yan Scholten, Jan Schuchardt, Aleksandar Bojchevski, Stephan Günnemann

  • USENIX Security 2024 Probabilistic certificationOther Machine Learning needs Better Randomness Standards: Randomised Smoothing and PRNG-based attacks (arXiv)
    Pranav Dahiya, Ilia Shumailov, Ross Anderson

    Shows that backdooring the random number generator used by randomised smoothing can make certification over- or underestimate robustness by up to 81 times, with tiny changes that NIST randomness tests miss, and argues for updated NIST guidelines for machine learning.

  • NDSS 2024 Other TextGuard: Provable Defense against Backdoor Attacks on Text Classification
    Hengzhi Pei, Jinyuan Jia, Wenbo Guo, Bo Li, Dawn Song

    TextGuard is a provable defense against backdoor attacks on text classification: it splits training sentences into sub-sentences to form sub-training sets, trains an ensemble, and guarantees unchanged predictions when the trigger length is below a threshold, beating prior certified defenses.
