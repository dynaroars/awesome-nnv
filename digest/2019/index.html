September 2019

NeurIPS 2019 15

NeurIPS 2019 decisions: convex relaxations come under scrutiny, with an exact study revealing an inherent barrier and k-ReLU's multi-neuron relaxations moving beyond it. Randomized smoothing gains adversarial training and tighter certificates, and other directions include DEEPG for geometric robustness, certificates for graph perturbations, GeoCert's exact pointwise robustness, SDP-based Lipschitz bounds and Neural Lyapunov Control.
