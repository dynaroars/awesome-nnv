  • ICML 2021 Probabilistic certificationBeyond linear output properties Scalable Certified Segmentation via Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Marc Fischer, Maximilian Baader, Martin Vechev

    Certifies image and point cloud segmentation with randomized smoothing, using multiple-testing correction and allowing abstention on single pixels or points; achieves competitive accuracy with guarantees on Pascal Context, Cityscapes and ShapeNet.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 22 Jul, 16:00–18:00 (UTC)

  • ICML 2021 Certified training Skew Orthogonal Convolutions (arXiv)
    Sahil Singla, Soheil Feizi

    Skew Orthogonal Convolution builds orthogonal, gradient-norm-preserving convolution layers from the exponential of a skew-symmetric Jacobian with a bounded Taylor approximation, training provably Lipschitz CNNs faster with better standard and certified accuracy on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 22 Jul, 16:00–18:00 (UTC)

  • ICML 2021 Probabilistic certification CRFL: Certifiably Robust Federated Learning against Backdoor Attacks (arXiv)
    Chulin Xie, Minghao Chen, Pin-Yu Chen, Bo Li

    CRFL trains federated learning models with certified robustness against backdoors by clipping and smoothing model parameters, giving sample-wise certificates tied to poisoning ratio, attacker count and training iterations, and the first benchmark for this setting.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 23 Jul, 04:00–06:00 (UTC)

  • ICML 2021 Formal explanations Explanations for Monotonic Classifiers (arXiv)
    João Marques-Silva, Thomas Gerspacher, Martin C. Cooper, Alexey Ignatiev, Nina Narodytska

    Gives algorithms polynomial in classifier run time and feature count to compute one formal explanation of a black-box monotonic classifier, plus a practical model-agnostic algorithm for enumerating formal explanations.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 23 Jul, 04:00–06:00 (UTC)

  • ICML 2021 Certified training Globally-Robust Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Klas Leino, Zifan Wang, Matt Fredrikson

    Builds globally-robust networks by integrating efficient global Lipschitz bounds into training, producing models certifiable by construction with low time and memory cost; a large Tiny-Imagenet model trains in hours, and local bounds are proved not to raise maximum verifiable accuracy.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 23 Jul, 04:00–06:00 (UTC)

  • ICML 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Provable Lipschitz Certification for Generative Models (arXiv)
    Matt Jordan, Alex Dimakis

    Upper bounds the Lipschitz constant of generative models by approximating the set of vector-Jacobian products with layerwise zonotope abstractions, giving tight bounds on small networks and scaling to VAE and DCGAN architectures.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 23 Jul, 04:00–06:00 (UTC)

  • ICML 2021 Certified training Towards Certifying L-infinity Robustness using Neural Networks with L-inf-dist Neurons (arXiv)
    Bohang Zhang, Tianle Cai, Zhou Lu, Di He, Liwei Wang

    Introduces l-infinity-dist neurons whose networks are 1-Lipschitz under the l-infinity norm, giving certified robustness from output margins; reaches 93.09% certified accuracy on MNIST, 35.42% on CIFAR-10 and 16.31% on TinyImageNet.

    Virtual · Poster · Fri 23 Jul, 04:00–06:00 (UTC)

  • RSS 2021 NN control systems Lyapunov-stable neural-network control
    Hongkai Dai, Benoit Landry, Lujie Yang, Marco Pavone, Russ Tedrake

    Synthesizes a Lyapunov-stable neural-network controller together with a neural Lyapunov function, verifying the Lyapunov condition as a mixed-integer linear program that either certifies it or returns counterexamples; also inner-approximates the region of attraction, tested on pendulum and quadrotor robots.
