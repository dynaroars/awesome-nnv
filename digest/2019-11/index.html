November 2019

AAAI 2020 and USENIX Security 2020 decisions: Venus prunes MILP search through ReLU dependency analysis, Fastened CROWN tightens certificates, and randomized smoothing and ablation address sparse attacks. Certification also extends to weight perturbations, with robustness-aware quantization, and to verifiably robust PDF malware classifiers.

9 NNV papers accepted in November 2019, at AAAI 2020, EMNLP 2019, ICCAD 2019 and USENIX Security 2020. 3 of them are placed by conference date, as the notification date is not recorded.

AAAI 2020 5 papers

  • AAAI 2020 Complete verification Efficient Verification of ReLU-Based Neural Networks via Dependency Analysis
    Elena Botoeva, Panagiotis Kouvaros, Jan Kronqvist, Alessio Lomuscio, Ruth Misener

    Venus verifies ReLU feed-forward networks by exploiting dependency relations between ReLU nodes to prune the MILP search tree, combined with input domain splitting and symbolic interval propagation; evaluated on ACAS collision-avoidance, MNIST and CIFAR-10 models with considerable gains.

  • AAAI 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Fastened CROWN: Tightened Neural Network Robustness Certificates (arXiv)
    Zhaoyang Lyu, Ching-Yun Ko, Zhifeng Kong, Ngai Wong, Dahua Lin, Luca Daniel

    Proves that CROWN's solution is optimal for a given linear program under mild constraints, making costly LP-based certification unnecessary, and proposes FROWN, an optimization-based algorithm that tightens CROWN robustness certificates to certify larger robust regions across various networks.

  • AAAI 2020 Probabilistic certificationCertified training Regularized Training and Tight Certification for Randomized Smoothed Classifier with Provable Robustness (arXiv)
    Huijie Feng, Chunpeng Wu, Guoyang Chen, Weifeng Zhang, Yang Ning

    Trains base classifiers for Gaussian randomized smoothing with a regularized risk that adaptively encourages accuracy and robustness of the smoothed classifier, usable with standard or adversarial training, plus a certification algorithm that exploits the regularization for tighter high-probability ℓ2 robustness bounds on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet.

  • AAAI 2020 Probabilistic certification Robustness Certificates for Sparse Adversarial Attacks by Randomized Ablation (arXiv)
    Alexander Levine, Soheil Feizi

    Extends randomized smoothing to the ℓ0 threat model by randomly ablating input features instead of adding noise, certifying over 50% of MNIST images against any 8-pixel distortion and doubling or better the median certificates of a noise-based approach (8 vs 4 pixels on MNIST, 16 vs 1 on ImageNet).

  • AAAI 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Towards Certificated Model Robustness Against Weight Perturbations
    Tsui-Wei Weng, Pu Zhao, Sijia Liu, Pin-Yu Chen, Xue Lin, Luca Daniel

    Certifies robustness of neural networks to weight perturbations by computing a bound within which outputs cannot be adversarially changed, and links this to weight quantization, yielding a robustness-aware quantization scheme that improves generalization of quantized networks.

USENIX Security 2020 1 paper

  • USENIX Security 2020 Certified training On Training Robust PDF Malware Classifiers (arXiv)
    Yizheng Chen, Shiqi Wang, Dongdong She, Suman Jana

    Trains PDF malware classifiers with formally verified robustness properties over subtree insertions and deletions using verifiably robust training, achieving 92.27% average verified robust accuracy with 99.74% accuracy and 0.56% false positives, and raising evasion cost for unrestricted attackers.
