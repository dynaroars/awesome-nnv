June 2023

OOPSLA 2023 decisions: Calzone, a sound and complete few-pixel (L0) robustness verifier, and perception contracts that bound perception errors to keep controllers using neural perception safe.

2 NNV papers accepted in June 2023, at OOPSLA 2023.

  • OOPSLA 2023 Complete verification Deep Learning Robustness Verification for Few-Pixel Attacks
    Yuval Shapira, Eran Avneri, Dana Drachsler-Cohen

    Calzone is a sound and complete L0 (few-pixel) robustness verifier that predicts the largest provable pixel-set size, covers the image with covering designs and checks each set with an L∞ verifier; it typically finishes in minutes, while a MILP baseline fails at t=3.

  • OOPSLA 2023 NN control systems Perception Contracts for Safety of ML-Enabled Systems
    Angello Astorga, Chiao Hsieh, P. Madhusudan, Sayan Mitra

    Introduces perception contracts, bounds on perception errors that preserve safety invariants of controllers using neural perception, with symbolic learning algorithms that synthesize them from images; evaluated on lane tracking for an electric vehicle and a crop-row-following agricultural robot.
