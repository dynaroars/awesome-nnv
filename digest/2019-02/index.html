February 2019

PLDI 2019 decisions: inductive synthesis of programs and invariants to verify and shield reinforcement-learning neural policies, and Charon's combination of gradient-based counterexample search with abstraction-based proof search for local robustness.

2 NNV papers accepted in February 2019, at PLDI 2019.

  • PLDI 2019 NN control systems An inductive synthesis framework for verifiable reinforcement learning
    He Zhu, Zikang Xiong, Stephen Magill, Suresh Jagannathan

    Verifies reinforcement learning neural policies by synthesizing simpler deterministic programs plus inductive invariants through counterexample- and syntax-guided synthesis; the programs monitor the network at runtime and block unsafe actions, with low overhead across cyber-physical applications.

  • PLDI 2019 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationComplete verification Optimization and abstraction: a synergistic approach for analyzing neural network robustness
    Greg Anderson, Shankara Pailoor, Isil Dillig, Swarat Chaudhuri

    Charon verifies local robustness of neural networks by combining gradient-based counterexample search with abstraction-based proof search guided by a learned verification policy, giving a sound and δ-complete procedure that outperforms AI^2, Reluplex and Reluval.
