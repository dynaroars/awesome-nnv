December 2024

AAAI 2025, OOPSLA 2025 and USENIX Security 2025 decisions: neural network control systems lead, with barrier certificates from Fourier Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks, verified multi-agent path finding and certificate repair via runtime monitoring. Randomized smoothing certifies sensor fusion and speaker recognition, while OOPSLA work covers soundness of DNN certifiers (ProveSound), incremental verification (Olive) and bit-flip attacks on quantized neural networks (BFAVerifier).

18 NNV papers accepted in December 2024, at AAAI 2025, OOPSLA 2025 and USENIX Security 2025.

AAAI 2025 11 papers

  • AAAI 2025 Beyond ℓp input propertiesProbabilistic certificationBeyond linear output properties COMMIT: Certifying Robustness of Multi-Sensor Fusion Systems Against Semantic Attacks
    Zijian Huang, Wenda Chu, Linyi Li, Chejian Xu, Bo Li

    COMMIT certifies multi-sensor fusion perception in autonomous vehicles against semantic transformations such as rotation and shifting, using anisotropic randomized smoothing on multi-modal data with grid-based splitting; on CARLA, fusion models certify up to 48.39% higher than single-modal ones.

  • AAAI 2025 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets Certification of Speaker Recognition Models to Additive Perturbations
    Dmitrii Korzh, Elvir Karimov, Mikhail Pautov, Oleg Y. Rogov, Ivan V. Oseledets

    Certifies speaker recognition models against norm-bounded additive perturbations by transferring and improving randomized smoothing certification from images to audio, for both classification and few-shot settings, with evaluation of several models on VoxCeleb 1 and 2.

  • AAAI 2025 Probabilistic certification Certified Causal Defense with Generalizable Robustness
    Yiran Qiao, Yu Yin, Chen Chen, Jing Ma

    GLEAN is a certified defense that adds certifiable causal factor learning to separate causal from spurious features, then certifies robustness against attacks on latent causal factors, so that certified robustness carries over to domains with distribution shift on benchmark datasets.

  • AAAI 2025 NN control systems Formal Synthesis of Barrier Certificates Using Fourier Kolmogorov-Arnold Network
    Xiongqi Zhang, Junwei Xu, Yang Wang, Dongming Xiang, Wang Lin, Zuohua Ding

    Synthesizes barrier certificates for cyber-physical systems with Fourier Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks instead of feed-forward networks, then formally checks candidates with a Lipschitz method and Satisfiability Modulo Theories; the KAN4BC tool improves verification efficiency and success rate on benchmarks.

  • AAAI 2025 NN control systems Learning Verified Safe Neural Network Controllers for Multi-Agent Path Finding
    Mingyue Zhang, Nianyu Li, Yi Chen, Jialong Li, Xiaoyi Zhang, Hengjun Zhao, Jiamou Liu, Wu Chen

    VSMANC jointly trains decentralized control barrier functions and neural controllers for multi-agent path finding, with a formal verification-guided retraining loop that checks the barrier functions and feeds counterexamples back, giving verified safety in shape formation and UAV simulations.

  • AAAI 2025 NN control systemsRepair & patching Neural Control and Certificate Repair via Runtime Monitoring
    Emily Yu, Dorde Zikelic, Thomas A. Henzinger

    Certifies and repairs neural control policies and their certificate functions, such as barrier or Lyapunov functions, when system dynamics are unknown, using runtime monitoring that detects violations and turns them into retraining data; raises safety rates on two control tasks.

  • AAAI 2025 Probabilistic certification PROSAC: Provably Safe Certification for Machine Learning Models under Adversarial Attacks
    Chen Feng, Ziquan Liu, Zhuo Zhi, Ilija Bogunovic, Carsten Gerner-Beuerle, Miguel Rodrigues

    PROSAC certifies that a model's adversarial population risk stays below a threshold through a calibration-based hypothesis test with Bayesian optimization over attacks; applied to ViT and ResNet models, it finds ViTs and larger models generally more robust.

  • AAAI 2025 NN control systems Probabilistic Shielding for Safe Reinforcement Learning
    Edwin Hamel-De le Court, Francesco Belardinelli, Alexander W. Goodall

    Gives safe reinforcement learning strict formal guarantees when the MDP safety dynamics are known and safety is an undiscounted probabilistic avoidance property, by augmenting the MDP state and shielding the agent's actions; the agent stays safe in both training and testing.

  • AAAI 2025 NN control systems Scalable Surrogate Verification of Image-Based Neural Network Control Systems Using Composition and Unrolling
    Feiyang Cai, Chuchu Fan, Stanley Bak

    Verifies image-based neural network control systems with a cGAN surrogate by composing dynamics, generator and controller into one network and unrolling several control steps, so existing verifiers yield tighter reachable sets; the prior baseline's set is 175% larger on aircraft taxiing.

  • AAAI 2025 Certified training Sparse Transfer Learning Accelerates and Enhances Certified Robustness: A Comprehensive Study
    Zhangheng Li, Tianlong Chen, Linyi Li, Bo Li, Zhangyang Wang

    Speeds up L2-norm certified robustness verification on downstream tasks by reusing pre-training verification results with sparse updates, dynamic sparse mask selection and a stability regularizer called DiffStab, cutting verification time by up to 70-80% with slight certified accuracy loss.

  • AAAI 2025 Certified training Training Verification-Friendly Neural Networks via Neuron Behavior Consistency
    Zongxin Liu, Zhe Zhao, Fu Song, Jun Sun, Pengfei Yang, Xiaowei Huang, Lijun Zhang

    Trains verification-friendly networks by keeping neuron activation states consistent within a local input neighborhood, reducing unstable neurons and tightening bounds; on MNIST, Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 the networks stay verifiable at larger radii where other tools fail.

OOPSLA 2025 4 papers

  • OOPSLA 2025 Other Automated Verification of Soundness of DNN Certifiers
    Avaljot Singh, Yasmin Sarita, Charith Mendis, Gagandeep Singh

    Automatically verifies the soundness of abstract-interpretation-based DNN certifiers written in the ConstraintFlow DSL with ProveSound, which introduces symbolic DNNs to reduce soundness over arbitrary architectures to a tractable form checkable by standard SMT solvers, covering both existing and new certifiers.

  • OOPSLA 2025 Complete verification Efficient Incremental Verification of Neural Networks Guided by Counterexample Potentiality
    Guanqin Zhang, Zhenya Zhang, H. M. N. Dilum Bandara, Shiping Chen, Jianjun Zhao, Yulei Sui

    Accelerates incremental verification of a modified network with Olive, which reuses a branch-and-bound template from a similar network and orders subproblems by counterexample potentiality, in greedy and balanced variants; on 1445 MNIST and CIFAR-10 instances it beats classic and incremental verifiers, especially on falsifiable ones.

  • OOPSLA 2025 Global & relational properties Guarding the Privacy of Label-Only Access to Neural Network Classifiers via iDP Verification
    Anan Kabaha, Dana Drachsler-Cohen

    Provides individual differential privacy for label-only access to classifiers with LUCID, which computes an iDP deterministic bound using MILP encoding, hyper-network abstraction, branch-and-bound and linear relaxation, adding noise only where needed; perfect 0-iDP costs 1.4% accuracy versus 12.7% for DP training.

  • OOPSLA 2025 Quantized & bit-level Verification of Bit-Flip Attacks against Quantized Neural Networks
    Yedi Zhang, Lei Huang, Pengfei Gao, Fu Song, Jun Sun, Jin Song Dong

    Formally verifies resilience of quantized neural networks to bit-flip attacks, or finds all vulnerable parameters, with BFAVerifier, combining reachability analysis over symbolic parameters in a new abstract domain with an equivalent MILP encoding when abstraction fails, making it sound and complete.

USENIX Security 2025 3 papers

  • USENIX Security 2025 Beyond feed-forward netsOtherBeyond ℓp input properties AGNNCert: Defending Graph Neural Networks against Arbitrary Perturbations with Deterministic Certification (arXiv)
    Jiate Li, Binghui Wang

    Certifies GNNs against arbitrary combined edge, node and node-feature perturbations with AGNNCert, giving deterministic guarantees for both node and graph classification and covering earlier certified defenses as special cases; it also outperforms prior defenses on edge-only and node-only perturbations.

  • USENIX Security 2025 Certified trainingNN control systems CAMP in the Odyssey: Provably Robust Reinforcement Learning with Certified Radius Maximization (arXiv)

    Trains certifiably robust deep RL policies with CAMP, which optimizes a surrogate loss tied to local certified radii under policy smoothing and stabilizes training through policy imitation; it achieves up to twice the certified expected return of baselines.
