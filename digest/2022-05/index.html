May 2022

ICML 2022 decisions: randomized smoothing dominates, from Double Sampling Randomized Smoothing and bounded-support smoothing to GSmooth and TPC for semantic transformations, plus watermarks, NLP and image retrieval. Lipschitz and certified training work includes the Convex Potential Layer, CerDEQ and linearity grafting, with FairSmooth for group fairness and Finite Aggregation against data poisoning.

16 NNV papers accepted in May 2022, at ICML 2022.

  • ICML 2022 oral 🎉 Certified training A Dynamical System Perspective for Lipschitz Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Laurent Meunier, Blaise Delattre, Alexandre Araujo, Alexandre Allauzen

    Builds 1-Lipschitz neural networks by viewing residual networks as continuous-time dynamical systems, introducing the Convex Potential Layer from convex-potential ResNet flows; experiments show scalability and benefits as a provable l2 defense against adversarial examples.

  • ICML 2022 Certified trainingBeyond feed-forward nets CerDEQ: Certifiable Deep Equilibrium Model
    Mingjie Li, Yisen Wang, Zhouchen Lin

    CerDEQ enables certified training of deep equilibrium models by bounding outputs through bound propagation with the adjoint DEQ, plus weight orthogonalisation; at epsilon 8/255 it reaches 64.72% certified error on CIFAR-10 and 94.45% on Tiny ImageNet.

  • ICML 2022 Probabilistic certification Certified Adversarial Robustness Under the Bounded Support Set
    Yiwen Kou, Qinyuan Zheng, Yisen Wang

    Generalises f-divergence-based randomized smoothing to Wasserstein and total-variation frameworks that handle smoothing measures with bounded support, such as uniform distributions; proves negative certified robustness results for lq perturbations, validated on CIFAR-10 with ResNet.

  • ICML 2022 Probabilistic certification Certified Neural Network Watermarks with Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Arpit Bansal, Ping-Yeh Chiang, Michael J. Curry, Rajiv Jain, Curtis Wigington, Varun Manjunatha, John Dickerson, Tom Goldstein

    Certifiable watermarking of neural networks using randomized smoothing over model parameters, guaranteeing the watermark cannot be removed unless parameters change by more than an l2 threshold; also more empirically robust than earlier watermarking methods.

  • ICML 2022 Probabilistic certificationBeyond ℓp input properties Certified Robustness Against Natural Language Attacks by Causal Intervention (arXiv)
    Haiteng Zhao, Chang Ma, Xinshuai Dong, Anh Tuan Luu, Zhi-Hong Deng, Hanwang Zhang

    CISS certifies NLP models against word substitution attacks by learning causal effects through smoothing in latent semantic space; on YELP its certified robustness beats the runner-up by 6.7%, with 79.4% empirical robustness under syntactic attacks.

  • ICML 2022 Probabilistic certification Certifying Out-of-Domain Generalization for Blackbox Functions (arXiv)
    Maurice Weber, Linyi Li, Boxin Wang, Zhikuan Zhao, Bo Li, Ce Zhang

    Certifies distributional robustness of black-box models with bounded losses using the Hellinger distance, scaling to ImageNet and non-smooth losses; gives the first non-vacuous certified out-of-domain generalization on ImageNet and beats prior methods on smaller tasks.

  • ICML 2022 Probabilistic certification Double Sampling Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Linyi Li, Jiawei Zhang, Tao Xie, Bo Li

    Double Sampling Randomized Smoothing (DSRS) tightens l2 certificates using probabilities from an additional smoothing distribution, provably certifying radius of order sqrt(d) under mild assumptions; certifies larger radii than baselines on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and ImageNet.

  • ICML 2022 Beyond ℓp input propertiesProbabilistic certification GSmooth: Certified Robustness against Semantic Transformations via Generalized Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Zhongkai Hao, Chengyang Ying, Yinpeng Dong, Hang Su, Jian Song, Jun Zhu

    GSmooth generalises randomized smoothing to certify robustness against general semantic transformations, including ones without closed forms such as defocus blur and pixelate, using dimension augmentation and a surrogate image-to-image network to approximate transformations.

  • ICML 2022 Other Improved Certified Defenses against Data Poisoning with (Deterministic) Finite Aggregation (arXiv)
    Wenxiao Wang, Alexander Levine, Soheil Feizi

    Finite Aggregation certifies robustness against data poisoning by training base classifiers on overlapping unions of small disjoint subsets, improving on DPA; certified fractions rise by up to 3.05%, 3.87% and 4.77% on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and GTSRB.

  • ICML 2022 Global & relational propertiesProbabilistic certification Input-agnostic Certified Group Fairness via Gaussian Parameter Smoothing (arXiv)
    Jiayin Jin, Zeru Zhang, Yang Zhou, Lingfei Wu

    FairSmooth certifies group fairness of classifiers independent of input distribution using Gaussian parameter smoothing and averaged per-group smooth classifiers; nonlinear functional analysis shows the resulting manifold has an input-independent global Lipschitz constant.

  • ICML 2022 Probabilistic certification Intriguing Properties of Input-Dependent Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Peter Súkeník, Aleksei Kuvshinov, Stephan Günnemann

    Shows input-dependent randomized smoothing methods lack formal guarantees and suffer from the curse of dimensionality, then gives a framework and concrete variance design that permits certified input-dependent smoothing under strict restrictions, tested on CIFAR10 and MNIST.

  • ICML 2022 Certified training Linearity Grafting: Relaxed Neuron Pruning Helps Certifiable Robustness (arXiv)
    Tianlong Chen, Huan Zhang, Zhenyu Zhang, Shiyu Chang, Sijia Liu, Pin-Yu Chen, Zhangyang Wang

    Linearity grafting linearises insignificant ReLU neurons and optimises their slopes and intercepts to tighten certified bounds, improving certifiable robustness by over 30% on CIFAR-10 without certified training and scaling complete verification to 17M-parameter models.

  • ICML 2022 OtherProbabilistic certification On Collective Robustness of Bagging Against Data Poisoning (arXiv)
    Ruoxin Chen, Zenan Li, Jie Li, Junchi Yan, Chentao Wu

    Certifies general bagging collectively against global data poisoning by solving a binary integer linear program for the maximum number of simultaneously changed predictions, bounds the tolerable poison budget for vanilla bagging, and proposes hash bagging, a deterministic hash-based subsampling that improves robustness almost for free.

  • ICML 2022 Probabilistic certificationBeyond linear output properties RetrievalGuard: Provably Robust 1-Nearest Neighbor Image Retrieval (arXiv)
    Yihan Wu, Hongyang Zhang, Heng Huang

    RetrievalGuard is a provably robust 1-nearest-neighbour image retrieval method that smooths the retrieval model to a bounded Lipschitz constant, keeping Recall@1 invariant to l2 adversarial perturbations within a computable radius.

  • ICML 2022 oral 🎉 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond linear output properties Robustness Verification for Contrastive Learning
    Zekai Wang, Weiwei Liu

    RVCL is a robustness verification framework for contrastive learning encoders that avoids dependence on attacks, labels and downstream tasks, using extreme value theory to relate encoder robust radius to downstream radius; evaluates robustness of both models and images.

  • ICML 2022 Beyond ℓp input propertiesProbabilistic certification TPC: Transformation-Specific Smoothing for Point Cloud Models (arXiv)
    Wenda Chu, Linyi Li, Bo Li

    TPC certifies point cloud models against semantic transformations such as rotation, shearing and tapering with transformation-specific smoothing, covering additive, composable and indirectly composable categories; certified accuracy against z-axis twisting within 20 degrees rises from 20.3% to 83.8%.
