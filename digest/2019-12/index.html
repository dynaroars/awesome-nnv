December 2019

ICLR 2020 and ICSE 2020 decisions: certified training is prominent, with COLT, CROWN-IBP, MACER and certified patch defenses, while randomized smoothing gains f-divergence and top-k certificates. Other directions include robustness verification for Transformers, learned branching for branch-and-bound, SAT-friendly binarized neural networks, LiPopt Lipschitz estimation and ReluDiff's differential verification.

15 NNV papers accepted in December 2019, at ICLR 2020 and ICSE 2020.

ICLR 2020 14 papers

  • ICLR 2020 Probabilistic certification A Framework for robustness Certification of Smoothed Classifiers using F-Divergences
    Krishnamurthy Dvijotham, Jamie Hayes, Borja Balle, Zico Kolter, Chongli Qin, András György, Kai Yuanqing Xiao, Sven Gowal, Pushmeet Kohli

    Generalises randomized smoothing to arbitrary smoothing measures and perturbation classes, certifying robustness of black-box smoothed classifiers via f-divergences; reports state-of-the-art certified robustness on MNIST, CIFAR-10, ImageNet and Librispeech audio classification.

  • ICLR 2020 Certified training Adversarial Training and Provable Defenses: Bridging the Gap
    Mislav Balunovic, Martin Vechev

    COLT, convex layerwise adversarial training, trains certifiably robust networks by having an adversary search inside the verifier's convex relaxation at each layer. Reaches 60.5% certified robustness and 78.4% accuracy on CIFAR-10 at 2/255 L∞, beating concurrent results.

  • ICLR 2020 Certified trainingOtherBeyond ℓp input properties Certified Defenses for Adversarial Patches
    Ping-Yeh Chiang, Renkun Ni, Ahmed Abdelkader, Chen Zhu, Christoph Studer, Tom Goldstein

    Shows existing preprocessing defenses against adversarial patches break under simple white-box attacks, then gives the first certified defense against patch attacks with faster training methods, reporting good robustness transfer across patch shapes and preliminary results for sparse attacks.

  • ICLR 2020 Probabilistic certification Certified Robustness for Top-k Predictions against Adversarial Perturbations via Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Jinyuan Jia, Xiaoyu Cao, Binghui Wang, Neil Gong

    Extends randomized smoothing certificates from top-1 to top-k predictions, deriving a tight ℓ2 robustness radius under Gaussian noise. On ImageNet it reaches 62.8% certified top-5 accuracy for perturbations with ℓ2 norm below 0.5; also evaluated on CIFAR10.

  • ICLR 2020 Quantized & bit-level In Search for a SAT-friendly Binarized Neural Network Architecture
    Nina Narodytska, Hongce Zhang, Aarti Gupta, Toby Walsh

    Studies how binarized neural network architecture and training choices affect SAT-based reasoning, and proposes changes that yield SAT-friendly BNNs without accuracy loss, scaling existential and probabilistic queries to larger networks with significant speedups on all tested datasets.

  • ICLR 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Lipschitz constant estimation of Neural Networks via sparse polynomial optimization (arXiv)
    Fabian Latorre, Paul Rolland, Volkan Cevher

    LiPopt computes increasingly tight upper bounds on neural network Lipschitz constants via polynomial optimization reduced to LP or SDP, exploiting sparse connectivity for convolutional and pruned networks; gives better ℓ∞-Lipschitz estimates than baselines on random and MNIST networks.

  • ICLR 2020 Certified trainingProbabilistic certification MACER: Attack-free and Scalable Robust Training via Maximizing Certified Radius (arXiv)
    Runtian Zhai, Chen Dan, Di He, Huan Zhang, Boqing Gong, Pradeep Ravikumar, Cho-Jui Hsieh, Liwei Wang

    MACER trains provably robust smoothed classifiers by directly maximizing the certified ℓ2 radius from randomized smoothing, without adversarial attacks. On CIFAR-10, ImageNet, MNIST and SVHN it trains faster than adversarial training and achieves larger average certified radius.

  • ICLR 2020 Complete verification Neural Network Branching for Neural Network Verification (arXiv)
    Jingyue Lu, M. Pawan Kumar

    Learns a branching strategy for branch-and-bound neural network verification with a graph neural network that imitates strong branching on the verified network's graph, roughly halving branches and verification time on convolutional networks and transferring to harder properties and larger networks.

  • ICLR 2020 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation Robustness Verification for Transformers (arXiv)
    Zhouxing Shi, Huan Zhang, Kai-Wei Chang, Minlie Huang, Cho-Jui Hsieh

    The first robustness verification algorithm for Transformers, handling the cross-nonlinearity and cross-position dependency of self-attention layers. Its certified bounds are much tighter than naive Interval Bound Propagation and reflect word importance in sentiment analysis.

  • ICLR 2020 Certified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretation Towards Stable and Efficient Training of Verifiably Robust Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Huan Zhang, Hongge Chen, Chaowei Xiao, Sven Gowal, Robert Stanforth, Bo Li, Duane S. Boning, Cho-Jui Hsieh

    CROWN-IBP trains verifiably robust networks by combining fast IBP bounds in a forward pass with tight CROWN linear-relaxation bounds in a backward pass, reaching 7.02% verified error on MNIST at ε=0.3 and 66.94% on CIFAR-10 at ε=8/255.

  • ICLR 2020 Beyond ℓp input propertiesCertified trainingBeyond feed-forward nets Towards Verified Robustness under Text Deletion Interventions
    Johannes Welbl, Po-Sen Huang, Robert Stanforth, Sven Gowal, Krishnamurthy Dvijotham, Martin Szummer, Pushmeet Kohli

    Formally verifies that decomposable-attention natural language inference models do not grow more confident when arbitrary word subsets are deleted, using interval bound propagation. IBP training raises verified samples on SNLI from 2.8% to 18.4%.

  • ICLR 2020 OtherBeyond linear output properties Towards neural networks that provably know when they don't know
    Alexander Meinke, Matthias Hein

  • ICLR 2020 OtherBound propagation & abstract interpretation Universal Approximation with Certified Networks
    Maximilian Baader, Matthew Mirman, Martin Vechev

    Proves a universal approximation theorem for interval-certified ReLU networks: any continuous function can be approximated by a network whose interval bound propagation over a region is arbitrarily close to the function's optimal output range there.

ICSE 2020 1 paper

  • ICSE 2020 Global & relational propertiesBound propagation & abstract interpretation ReluDiff: differential verification of deep neural networks
    Brandon Paulsen, Jingbo Wang, Chao Wang

    ReluDiff performs differential verification of two closely related networks, such as an original and its compressed version, using a forward interval pass that exploits their similarity and gradient-guided backward refinement, giving orders-of-magnitude speedups and more proved properties.
