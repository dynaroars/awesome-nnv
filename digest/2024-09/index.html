September 2024

NeurIPS 2024 decisions: randomized smoothing leads, extended to multi-step defences, regression models, Deep Equilibrium Models and diffusion purification. Safe control is the other strand, with Dynamic Model Predictive Shielding, VerSAILLE's differential dynamic logic and SEEV's ReLU barrier functions, while BICCOS adds cutting planes to branch-and-bound and RABBit targets relational verification.

18 NNV papers accepted in September 2024, at NeurIPS 2024.

  • NeurIPS 2024 spotlight 🎉 Probabilistic certification Adaptive Randomized Smoothing: Certified Adversarial Robustness for Multi-Step Defences
    Saiyue Lyu, Shadab Shaikh, Frederick Shpilevskiy, Evan Shelhamer, Mathias Lécuyer

    Adaptive Randomized Smoothing certifies test-time adaptive, multi-step image classifiers against L-infinity adversarial examples by analyzing randomized smoothing with f-Differential Privacy; it improves standard accuracy by 1 to 15 points and ImageNet certified accuracy by up to 1.6 points.

    Vancouver · Poster #2205, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Wed 11 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets Certified Robustness for Deep Equilibrium Models via Serialized Random Smoothing
    Weizhi Gao, Zhichao Hou, Han Xu, Xiaorui Liu

    Brings randomized smoothing certification to Deep Equilibrium Models, with Serialized Randomized Smoothing reusing historical information to cut redundant computation and a new certified radius estimate; certification of DEQs runs up to 7x faster with almost no loss in certified accuracy.

    Vancouver · Poster #2204, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Wed 11 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 Probabilistic certification Consistency Purification: Effective and Efficient Diffusion Purification towards Certified Robustness
    Yiquan Li, Zhongzhu Chen, Kun Jin, Jiongxiao Wang, Jiachen Lei, Bo Li, Chaowei Xiao

    Consistency Purification improves diffusion-based purification for randomized smoothing by using a one-step consistency model, fine-tuned with an LPIPS loss, to keep purified images on the data manifold and semantically aligned, giving better certified robustness and efficiency than baselines.

    Vancouver · Poster #7300, West Ballroom A-D · Wed 11 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 Complete verification Scalable Neural Network Verification with Branch-and-bound Inferred Cutting Planes (arXiv)
    Duo Zhou, Christopher Brix, Grani A. Hanasusanto, Huan Zhang

    BICCOS strengthens branch-and-bound neural network verification with cutting planes inferred from neuron relationships in verified subproblems, plus influence-based strengthening and multi-tree search; it verifies more instances than other verifiers, scales to large networks and is part of α,β-CROWN.

    Vancouver · Poster #2206, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Wed 11 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 Probabilistic certification Treatment of Statistical Estimation Problems in Randomized Smoothing for Adversarial Robustness
    Václav Vorácek

    Examines statistical estimation in randomized smoothing certification, giving confidence-sequence procedures with optimal sample complexity and the same guarantees as standard methods for deciding robustness at a radius, plus a randomized Clopper-Pearson interval that yields strictly stronger certificates.

    Vancouver · Poster #4911, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Wed 11 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 NN control systems Verified Safe Reinforcement Learning for Neural Network Dynamic Models
    Junlin Wu, Huan Zhang, Yevgeniy Vorobeychik

    Learns control policies for nonlinear neural network dynamical systems that are verified safe via finite-horizon reachability, using a curriculum that lengthens the verified horizon, incremental verification and multiple initial-state-dependent controllers; verified horizons are up to an order of magnitude longer than baselines.

    Vancouver · Poster #6611, West Ballroom A-D · Wed 11 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 Other Achieving Domain-Independent Certified Robustness via Knowledge Continuity (arXiv)
    Alan Sun, Chiyu Ma, Kenneth Ge, Soroush Vosoughi

    Defines knowledge continuity, a Lipschitz-inspired notion whose robustness certificates depend only on the loss and the network's learned intermediate metric spaces, not on domain, norm or distribution; shows expressiveness need not conflict with it, and applies it to regularization, a certification algorithm and localizing vulnerable components.

    Vancouver · Poster #2203, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Wed 11 Dec, 16:30–19:30 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 NN control systemsComplete verification SEEV: Synthesis with Efficient Exact Verification for ReLU Neural Barrier Functions
    Hongchao Zhang, Zhizhen Qin, Sicun Gao, Andrew Clark

    SEEV synthesizes ReLU neural control barrier functions with a regularizer that reduces activation regions on the safety boundary, and verifies them exactly using tight over-approximations of the safety conditions per linear segment, improving verification efficiency while keeping barrier function quality.

  • NeurIPS 2024 NN control systems Dynamic Model Predictive Shielding for Provably Safe Reinforcement Learning
    Arko Banerjee, Kia Rahmani, Joydeep Biswas, Isil Dillig

    Dynamic Model Predictive Shielding keeps reinforcement learning provably safe during and after training by using a local planner, informed by the neural policy's reward estimates, to choose recovery actions; recovery regret falls exponentially with planning depth and rewards exceed baselines.

    Vancouver · Poster #6502, West Ballroom A-D · Thu 12 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 spotlight 🎉 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation ECLipsE: Efficient Compositional Lipschitz Constant Estimation for Deep Neural Networks
    Yuezhu Xu, S. Sivaranjani

    ECLipsE estimates Lipschitz constants of deep feed-forward networks compositionally, splitting the large matrix verification problem into per-layer subproblems solved by small SDPs or in closed form; it runs up to thousands of times faster with bounds close to or better than prior methods.

    Vancouver · Poster #2105, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Thu 12 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 OtherBeyond feed-forward nets FedGMark: Certifiably Robust Watermarking for Federated Graph Learning (arXiv)
    Yuxin Yang, Qiang Li, Yuan Hong, Binghui Wang

    Protects federated graph learning models with certified backdoor-based watermarks: FedGMark learns customized watermarks from graph structure and client information and uses a graph learning architecture that defends against empirical and provably worst-case watermark removal attacks, with experiments confirming empirical and provable watermarking performance.

    Vancouver · Poster #4610, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Thu 12 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 NN control systems Provably Safe Neural Network Controllers via Differential Dynamic Logic
    Samuel Teuber, Stefan Mitsch, André Platzer

    VerSAILLE derives neural network specifications from differential dynamic logic control envelopes so that a network proof yields infinite-time safety of the closed loop; Mosaic verifies the resulting polynomial arithmetic properties on piecewise-linear networks, proving safety on airborne collision avoidance benchmarks.

    Vancouver · Poster #4201, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Thu 12 Dec, 16:30–19:30 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 Repair & patching Provable Editing of Deep Neural Networks using Parametric Linear Relaxation
    Zhe Tao, Aditya V. Thakur

    Provably edits a DNN so that every input in a polytope maps into an output polytope while minimizing parameter change, relaxing the NP-hard problem to a linear program via Parametric Linear Relaxation; PREPARED fixes VNN-COMP property violations within 45 seconds and beats prior editing methods on image, BERT and physics-constrained networks.

    Vancouver · Poster #4411, East Exhibit Hall A-C · Fri 13 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (Vancouver time)

  • NeurIPS 2024 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Verifiably Robust Conformal Prediction
    Linus Jeary, Tom Kuipers, Mehran Hosseini, Nicola Paoletti

    VRCP restores conformal prediction coverage under adversarial attacks using neural network verification instead of randomized smoothing, supporting l1, l2 and l-infinity perturbations and regression; on CIFAR10, CIFAR100, TinyImageNet and deep RL tasks it keeps above-nominal coverage with tighter prediction regions.

    Vancouver · Poster #6210, West Ballroom A-D · Fri 13 Dec, 16:30–19:30 (Vancouver time)
