January 2018

ICLR 2018 decisions: certified training emerges, with a differentiable semidefinite relaxation that certifies and trains one-hidden-layer networks, and principled adversarial training over a Wasserstein ball that certifies distributional robustness.

2 NNV papers accepted in January 2018, at ICLR 2018.

  • ICLR 2018 Certified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretation Certified Defenses against Adversarial Examples (arXiv)
    Aditi Raghunathan, Jacob Steinhardt, Percy Liang

    Certifies one-hidden-layer networks against adversarial attacks via a differentiable semidefinite relaxation bound that is also optimized during training; on MNIST the resulting certificate guarantees no attack within ε=0.1 per pixel exceeds 35% test error.

  • ICLR 2018 Certified training Certifying Some Distributional Robustness with Principled Adversarial Training (arXiv)
    Aman Sinha, Hongseok Namkoong, John C. Duchi

    Trains models with distributionally robust optimization over a Wasserstein ball via a Lagrangian penalty, augmenting updates with worst-case data perturbations; for smooth losses this provably gives moderate robustness and certifies robustness of the population loss at low cost.
