February 2024

CVPR 2024, PLDI 2024 and USENIX Security 2024 decisions: certified defenses for vision, including PatchCURE against adversarial patches, MMCert for multi-modal models, median randomized smoothing for super-resolution and a comparison of 1-Lipschitz layers. MaxLin tightens linear approximation for MaxPool-based CNNs, and RaVeN verifies input-relational properties with the DiffPoly domain.

6 NNV papers accepted in February 2024, at CVPR 2024, PLDI 2024 and USENIX Security 2024.

CVPR 2024 · Seattle, United States 4 / 2,716 CVPR 2024 papers are NNV, by poster session

  • CVPR 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond linear output properties Universal Robustness via Median Randomized Smoothing for Real-World Super-Resolution
    Zakariya Chaouai, Mohamed Tamaazousti

    Compares robustness methods for real-world image super-resolution and finds median randomized smoothing generalizes across adversarial attacks better than adversarial training, while also helping SR models handle blur, Gaussian noise and natural real-world corruptions.

    Seattle · Poster #413, Arch 4A-E · Wed 19 Jun, 17:00–18:30 (Seattle time)

  • CVPR 2024 Certified training 1-Lipschitz Layers Compared: Memory, Speed, and Certifiable Robustness
    Bernd Prach, Fabio Brau, Giorgio C. Buttazzo, Christoph H. Lampert

    Compares 1-Lipschitz layer methods for certifiably robust networks on computational complexity, memory, time per epoch, accuracy and certified robust accuracy, and gives guidelines for choosing a method based on the available resources.

    Seattle · Poster #34, Arch 4A-E · Fri 21 Jun, 17:00–18:30 (Seattle time)

  • CVPR 2024 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets MMCert: Provable Defense Against Adversarial Attacks to Multi-Modal Models
    Yanting Wang, Hongye Fu, Wei Zou, Jinyuan Jia

    MMCert is a certified defense for multi-modal models that lower-bounds performance under bounded perturbations to every modality, such as changed pixels in RGB and depth images; on road segmentation and emotion recognition it beats a certified defense extended from unimodal models.

    Seattle · Poster #42, Arch 4A-E · Fri 21 Jun, 17:00–18:30 (Seattle time)

  • CVPR 2024 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Towards General Robustness Verification of MaxPool-Based Convolutional Neural Networks via Tightening Linear Approximation
    Yuan Xiao, Shiqing Ma, Juan Zhai, Chunrong Fang, Jinyuan Jia, Zhenyu Chen

    MaxLin verifies robustness of MaxPool-based CNNs by tightening the linear approximation of MaxPool to certify larger lower bounds; on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and Tiny ImageNet networks it improves the certified bound by up to 110.60% with up to 5.13x speedup.

    Seattle · Poster #53, Arch 4A-E · Fri 21 Jun, 17:00–18:30 (Seattle time)

USENIX Security 2024 1 paper

  • USENIX Security 2024 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties PatchCURE: Improving Certifiable Robustness, Model Utility, and Computation Efficiency of Adversarial Patch Defenses (arXiv)
    Chong Xiang, Tong Wu, Sihui Dai, Jonathan Petit, Suman Jana, Prateek Mittal

    PatchCURE is a tunable framework of certified defenses against adversarial patches that trades off certifiable robustness, utility and computation; at efficiency close to undefended models it gains 16-23% absolute clean and certified robust accuracy over prior defenses.

  • PLDI 2024 Global & relational properties Input-Relational Verification of Deep Neural Networks
    Debangshu Banerjee, Changming Xu, Gagandeep Singh

    RaVeN verifies input-relational DNN properties such as universal adversarial perturbation robustness and monotonicity with DiffPoly, an abstract domain tracking output differences between executions at every layer, combined with linear programming; it is substantially more precise than baselines.
