  • NeurIPS 2025 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationComplete verification Clip-and-Verify: Linear Constraint-Driven Domain Clipping for Accelerating Neural Network Verification (arXiv)
    Duo Zhou, Jorge Chavez, Hesun Chen, Grani A. Hanasusanto, Huan Zhang

    Speeds up branch-and-bound neural network verification with Clip-and-Verify, which uses linear constraints from bound propagation to clip verified or irrelevant input regions and tighten intermediate bounds on GPU; integrated into α,β-CROWN, it cuts subproblems by up to 96%.

  • NeurIPS 2025 Certified training MIBP-Cert: Certified Training against Data Perturbations with Mixed-Integer Bilinear Programs
    Tobias Lorenz, Marta Kwiatkowska, Mario Fritz

    Certifies training against data errors, corruptions and poisoning with MIBP-Cert, which computes the set of parameters reachable from perturbed data via mixed-integer bilinear programming and a sound per-step relaxation, giving deterministic robustness bounds for continuous and discrete data under complex threat models.

    San Diego · Poster #3908, Exhibit Hall C,D,E · Thu 4 Dec, 11:00–14:00 (San Diego time)

  • NeurIPS 2025 Formal explanationsBeyond feed-forward nets Sound Logical Explanations for Mean Aggregation Graph Neural Networks
