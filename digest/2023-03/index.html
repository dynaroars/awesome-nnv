March 2023

S&P 2023 decisions: certified defenses against patch hiding attacks on object detection (ObjectSeeker) and against backdoor attacks (RAB), plus a systematization of certified robustness for deep neural networks.

3 NNV papers accepted in March 2023, at S&P 2023.

  • S&P 2023 OtherBeyond ℓp input propertiesBeyond linear output properties ObjectSeeker: Certifiably Robust Object Detection against Patch Hiding Attacks via Patch-agnostic Masking
    Chong Xiang, Alexander Valtchanov, Saeed Mahloujifar, Prateek Mittal

    ObjectSeeker certifies object detectors against patch hiding attacks through patch-agnostic masking that removes the patch without knowing its shape, size or location; certified robustness improves by about 10-40% absolute over prior work with roughly 1% clean performance drop.

  • S&P 2023 OtherProbabilistic certification RAB: Provable Robustness Against Backdoor Attacks
    Maurice Weber, Xiaojun Xu, Bojan Karlas, Ce Zhang, Bo Li

    RAB certifies robustness against backdoor attacks through a randomized smoothing framework and smoothed training with a proven tight bound, plus an exact smooth-training algorithm for K-NN; gives the first certified backdoor robustness benchmark on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and ImageNette.

  • S&P 2023 Other SoK: Certified Robustness for Deep Neural Networks
    Linyi Li, Tao Xie, Bo Li

    Systematization of certifiably robust approaches for deep neural networks: a taxonomy of robustness verification and robust training methods, their strengths, limits and connections, open challenges, and an open-source platform benchmarking more than 20 representative approaches.
