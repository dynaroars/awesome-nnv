November 2024

ICSE 2025 decisions: fairness certification and repair. Fairquant certifies and quantifies individual fairness of deep neural networks via symbolic interval analysis, and Patchpro repairs local robustness by attaching patch modules guided by formal verification.

2 NNV papers accepted in November 2024, at ICSE 2025.

  • ICSE 2025 Global & relational properties Fairquant: Certifying and Quantifying Fairness of Deep Neural Networks
    Brian Hyeongseok Kim, Jingbo Wang, Chao Wang

    Certifies and quantifies individual fairness of deep neural networks by abstracting symbolic interval analysis and refining it guided by the fairness property, reporting the percentage of provably fair individuals; on four fairness datasets it is more accurate and orders of magnitude faster than prior techniques.

  • ICSE 2025 Repair & patching Patch Synthesis for Property Repair of Deep Neural Networks
    Zhiming Chi, Jianan Ma, Pengfei Yang, Cheng-Chao Huang, Renjue Li, Jingyi Wang, Xiaowei Huang, Lijun Zhang

    Patchpro repairs local robustness of DNNs by attaching patch modules that fix all samples in a robustness neighborhood, using formal verification and a heuristic patch allocation; it achieves provable property-level repair in 100% of cases across several high-dimensional datasets.
