IJCAI 2018 decisions: reachability analysis of deep neural networks with provable guarantees.

1 NNV paper accepted in April 2018, at IJCAI 2018.

  • IJCAI 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Reachability Analysis of Deep Neural Networks with Provable Guarantees (arXiv)
    Wenjie Ruan, Xiaowei Huang, Marta Kwiatkowska

    Computes lower and upper bounds of a Lipschitz-continuous function over a DNN's outputs for a set of inputs using adaptive nested optimisation, which covers safety verification, output range analysis and robustness measurement, and handles a broader class of networks than prior verifiers efficiently.
