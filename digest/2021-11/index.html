November 2021

AAAI 2022 decisions: neural-network control systems lead, with neural supermartingales for stability, RNN controller synthesis, GoTube statistical reachability, and Taylor models combined with zonotopes. Probabilistic certification work includes CC-CERT, DeformRS and input-specific sampling for randomized smoothing, alongside SDP verification tightened with linear cuts.

13 NNV papers accepted in November 2021, at AAAI 2022 and EMNLP 2021. 3 of them are placed by conference date, as the notification date is not recorded.

AAAI 2022 10 papers

  • AAAI 2022 Probabilistic certification CC-CERT: A Probabilistic Approach to Certify General Robustness of Neural Networks
    Mikhail Pautov, Nurislam Tursynbek, Marina Munkhoeva, Nikita Muravev, Aleksandr Petiushko, Ivan V. Oseledets

    CC-CERT gives probabilistic robustness certificates for neural networks under general attacks, including semantic transformations such as rotation and translation, by estimating failure probability under a sampled attack distribution with Chernoff-Cramer bounds.

  • AAAI 2022 Beyond ℓp input propertiesProbabilistic certification DeformRS: Certifying Input Deformations with Randomized Smoothing
    Motasem Alfarra, Adel Bibi, Naeemullah Khan, Philip Torr, Bernard Ghanem

    DeformRS-VF and DeformRS-Par certify networks against vector-field and parameterized input deformations, such as rotations, translations, scaling and affine maps, via randomized smoothing; DeformRS-Par reaches 39% certified accuracy for rotations within 10 degrees on ImageNet.

  • AAAI 2022 NN control systemsProbabilistic certification Distillation of RL Policies with Formal Guarantees via Variational Abstraction of Markov Decision Processes
    Florent Delgrange, Ann Nowé, Guillermo A. Pérez

    Distills deep RL policies into discrete latent Markov decision process models learned by a variational autoencoder, with bisimulation bounds and PAC guarantees that let formal MDP methods check reachability, safety-constrained reachability and discounted-reward objectives.

  • AAAI 2022 NN control systemsProbabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets GoTube: Scalable Statistical Verification of Continuous-Depth Models
    Sophie A. Gruenbacher, Mathias Lechner, Ramin M. Hasani, Daniela Rus, Thomas A. Henzinger, Scott A. Smolka, Radu Grosu

    GoTube statistically verifies continuous-depth neural models by solving global optimization problems over time to build a reachable tube that is conservative up to chosen probability and tightness, avoiding the wrapping effect and outscaling existing reachability tools.

  • AAAI 2022 Probabilistic certification Input-Specific Robustness Certification for Randomized Smoothing
    Ruoxin Chen, Jie Li, Junchi Yan, Ping Li, Bin Sheng

    Input-Specific Sampling speeds up randomized smoothing certification by adapting the sample size to each input while bounding the radius loss; it certifies over three times faster on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet at a cost of 0.05 certified radius.

  • AAAI 2022 NN control systems Recurrent Neural Network Controllers Synthesis with Stability Guarantees for Partially Observed Systems
    Fangda Gu, He Yin, Laurent El Ghaoui, Murat Arcak, Peter J. Seiler, Ming Jin

    Synthesizes recurrent neural network controllers for partially observed nonlinear uncertain systems with stability guarantees, using convex conditions from integral quadratic constraints and the S-lemma enforced by a projected policy gradient method that needs fewer samples.

  • AAAI 2022 NN control systems Stability Verification in Stochastic Control Systems via Neural Network Supermartingales
    Mathias Lechner, Dorde Zikelic, Krishnendu Chatterjee, Thomas A. Henzinger

    Verifies almost-sure asymptotic stability of nonlinear stochastic control systems with neural network policies by learning neural ranking supermartingales, which also give the first bounds on stabilization time; tested on stochastic reinforcement learning environments.

  • AAAI 2022 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationComplete verification Tight Neural Network Verification via Semidefinite Relaxations and Linear Reformulations
    Jianglin Lan, Yang Zheng, Alessio Lomuscio

    Tightens SDP-based neural network verification by adding linear cuts from the reformulation-linearisation technique to a layerwise SDP relaxation; on MNIST networks of about 10k nodes the share of certified cases rises from 0% to 82%.

  • AAAI 2022 Formal explanationsProbabilistic certification Towards Automating Model Explanations with Certified Robustness Guarantees
    Mengdi Huai, Jinduo Liu, Chenglin Miao, Liuyi Yao, Aidong Zhang

    A concept-based interpretation method that automatically produces prototype-based concept explanations without human intervention and gives certified robustness guarantees that these explanations hold under adversarial perturbations, tested on real-world datasets.

  • AAAI 2022 NN control systems Verification of Neural-Network Control Systems by Integrating Taylor Models and Zonotopes
    Christian Schilling, Marcelo Forets, Sebastián Guadalupe

    Verifies closed-loop neural-network control systems by chaining Taylor-model reachability for the dynamics with zonotope analysis for the network without losing dependency information; the tool is the first to analyse all benchmarks of an annual NNCS reachability competition.
