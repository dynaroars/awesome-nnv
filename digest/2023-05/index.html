  • USENIX Security 2023 Other PORE: Provably Robust Recommender Systems against Data Poisoning Attacks (arXiv)
    Jinyuan Jia, Yupei Liu, Yuepeng Hu, Neil Gong

    PORE makes any recommender system provably robust to untargeted data poisoning: it guarantees that at least r of the top-N items recommended without attack remain recommended, where r depends on the number of fake users and is computed efficiently per user.

  • USENIX Security 2023 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Precise and Generalized Robustness Certification for Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Yuanyuan Yuan, Shuai Wang, Zhendong Su

    GCERT certifies neural network robustness against semantic image mutations, such as weather filters, style transfer and perceptual changes, by expressing them as directions in a generative model's latent space; it plugs into complete, incomplete and quantitative certification frameworks.

ISSTA 2023 1 paper

  • ISSTA 2023 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation A Tale of Two Approximations: Tightening Over-Approximation for DNN Robustness Verification via Under-Approximation
    Zhiyi Xue, Si Liu, Zhaodi Zhang, Yiting Wu, Min Zhang

    DualApp tightens linear over-approximations of activation functions for DNN robustness verification by using an under-approximated actual domain, computed via Monte Carlo simulation or gradient descent; it improves verified robustness ratio by 100%-1000% and certified lower bounds by 10.64% on average.
