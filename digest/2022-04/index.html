April 2022

CAV 2022, IJCAI 2022 and ISSTA 2022 decisions: CAV papers target verifier efficiency through synthesised linear approximations and shared certificates, and verified safe reinforcement learning through Trainify and RINO reachability. IJCAI brought individual fairness guarantees and neuro-symbolic verification, while ISSTA defined epsilon-weakened robustness; backdoor verification also appears.

10 NNV papers accepted in April 2022, at CAV 2022, IJCAI 2022 and ISSTA 2022.

CAV 2022 6 papers

  • CAV 2022 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Example Guided Synthesis of Linear Approximations for Neural Network Verification
    Brandon Paulsen, Chao Wang

    Automatically synthesises sound linear approximations of activation functions for neural network verification by learning from generated examples, then bounding violations with global optimisation to restore soundness; solves more verification problems than hand-crafted approximations.

  • CAV 2022 Certified trainingOther Neural Network Robustness as a Verification Property: A Principled Case Study
    Marco Casadio, Ekaterina Komendantskaya, Matthew L. Daggitt, Wen Kokke, Guy Katz, Guy Amir, Idan Refaeli

    Sets out general principles for empirically analysing neural network robustness as a mathematical property across training, verification and deployment, comparing the many existing robustness notions, and applies them in a case study showing practical benefits.

  • CAV 2022 NN control systems RINO: Robust INner and Outer Approximated Reachability of Neural Networks Controlled Systems
    Eric Goubault, Sylvie Putot

    RINO computes inner and outer approximations of reachable sets for discrete- and continuous-time systems controlled by neural networks, combining zonotopes with mean-value AE extensions and Taylor methods; it is at least an order of magnitude faster than Verisig 2.0 and ReachNN*.

  • CAV 2022 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Shared Certificates for Neural Network Verification (arXiv)
    Marc Fischer, Christian Sprecher, Dimitar I. Dimitrov, Gagandeep Singh, Martin Vechev

    Shared certificates reduce neural network verification cost by reusing intermediate-layer abstractions that overlap or contain each other across different inputs and perturbations, without precision loss; evaluated on image classifiers under patch and geometric perturbations.

  • CAV 2022 NN control systemsCertified training Trainify: A CEGAR-Driven Training and Verification Framework for Safe Deep Reinforcement Learning
    Peng Jin, Jiaxu Tian, Dapeng Zhi, Xuejun Wen, Min Zhang

    Trainify trains deep reinforcement learning systems on coarse abstract state spaces and refines them from counterexamples in a CEGAR loop until all properties verify; on six control systems it yields provably safe policies without sacrificing reward or robustness.

  • CAV 2022 OtherProbabilistic certification Verifying Neural Networks Against Backdoor Attacks
    Long H. Pham, Jun Sun

    Verifies that a neural network is free of backdoors at a given success rate by combining statistical sampling with abstract interpretation, either proving absence of a backdoor or generating trigger inputs, avoiding the accuracy loss of randomized smoothing.

IJCAI 2022 3 papers

  • IJCAI 2022 Beyond ℓp input propertiesProbabilistic certification Certified Robustness via Randomized Smoothing over Multiplicative Parameters of Input Transformations
    Nikita Muravev, Aleksandr Petiushko

    Randomized smoothing over multiplicative parameters of input transformations certifies classifiers against gamma correction perturbations; an asymmetric Rayleigh distribution gives better certificates than Gaussian, Laplace or uniform smoothing for some perturbation values.

  • IJCAI 2022 Global & relational properties Individual Fairness Guarantees for Neural Networks
    Elias Benussi, Andrea Patanè, Matthew Wicker, Luca Laurenti, Marta Kwiatkowska

    Certifies epsilon-delta individual fairness of feed-forward neural networks under learnt metrics such as Mahalanobis distance, globally bounding nonlinearities with piecewise-linear functions encoded as MILP; a modified loss yields networks orders of magnitude fairer than prior methods.

  • IJCAI 2022 Global & relational properties Neuro-Symbolic Verification of Deep Neural Networks
    Xuan Xie, Kristian Kersting, Daniel Neider

    Neuro-symbolic verification lets neural networks serve as part of logical specifications, extending verification to complex real-world properties such as stopping before a stop sign, and can be implemented on top of existing neural network verification infrastructure.

ISSTA 2022 1 paper

  • ISSTA 2022 Probabilistic certification ε-weakened robustness of deep neural networks (arXiv)
    Pei Huang, Yuting Yang, Minghao Liu, Fuqi Jia, Feifei Ma, Jian Zhang

    Defines epsilon-weakened robustness, bounding the proportion of adversarial examples in a region, proves its decision problem PP-complete and gives statistical decision and maximum-radius algorithms with user-controlled error, polynomial in network dimension and size.
