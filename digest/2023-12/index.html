7 NNV papers accepted in December 2023, at AAAI 2024 and OOPSLA 2024.

AAAI 2024 6 papers

  • AAAI 2024 Formal explanations Axiomatic Aggregations of Abductive Explanations
    Gagan Biradar, Yacine Izza, Elita A. Lobo, Vignesh Viswanathan, Yair Zick

    Aggregates the many valid abductive explanations of a prediction into feature importance scores, using two power indices from cooperative game theory and a causal strength measure, each characterized axiomatically; the resulting scores resist attacks that fool SHAP and LIME.

  • AAAI 2024 Certified training DRF: Improving Certified Robustness via Distributional Robustness Framework
    Zekai Wang, Zhengyu Zhou, Weiwei Liu

    DRF links adversarial-training-based randomized smoothing methods to distributional robustness, showing they are special cases of its framework; this gives control over the trade-off between clean accuracy and certified robustness of smoothed classifiers and substantially improves certified robustness.

  • AAAI 2024 Certified trainingComplete verification DeepSaDe: Learning Neural Networks That Guarantee Domain Constraint Satisfaction
    Kshitij Goyal, Sebastijan Dumancic, Hendrik Blockeel

    DeepSaDe trains neural networks that provably satisfy domain constraints on all predictions, extending constraint-satisfaction-based learning of linear models with constraint propagation across layers and weight updates that mix gradient descent and CSP solving; it enforces varied constraints across several tasks.

  • AAAI 2024 Formal explanations Delivering Inflated Explanations
    Yacine Izza, Alexey Ignatiev, Peter J. Stuckey, João Marques-Silva

    Defines inflated abductive explanations, which give each selected feature a set of values under which the decision stays unchanged, making formal explanations more informative; they cost little more than abductive explanations to compute, and duality results extend to them.

  • AAAI 2024 Repair & patchingGlobal & relational properties REGLO: Provable Neural Network Repair for Global Robustness Properties
    Feisi Fu, Zhilu Wang, Weichao Zhou, Yixuan Wang, Jiameng Fan, Chao Huang, Qi Zhu, Xin Chen, Wenchao Li

    REGLO repairs pretrained ReLU networks to satisfy global robustness and individual fairness; it proves counterexamples must have large gradients, uses this to find violating linear regions, and solves a robust convex optimization problem for a minimal weight change that provably repairs them.

  • AAAI 2024 NN control systems Robustness Verification of Deep Reinforcement Learning Based Control Systems Using Reward Martingales
    Dapeng Zhi, Peixin Wang, Cheng Chen, Min Zhang

    Verifies robustness of deep reinforcement learning control systems under state perturbations with reward martingales, giving certified bounds on expected cumulative reward and on its tails; the martingales are trained as neural networks, and the bounds tightly enclose simulation outcomes.

OOPSLA 2024 1 paper

  • OOPSLA 2024 Global & relational properties Verification of Neural Networks' Global Robustness
    Anan Kabaha, Dana Drachsler-Cohen

    VHAGaR is an anytime verifier that computes the minimal globally robust bound of a classifier by mixed-integer programming, pruned with perturbation and network dependencies and generalized adversarial attacks; within three hours its bound gap averages 1.9 versus 154.7 for a prior verifier, 130.6x faster.
