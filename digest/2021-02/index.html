February 2021

CVPR 2021, ICRA 2021 and PLDI 2021 decisions: PLDI brought DeepT for certifying transformers, provable repair of DNNs and GenProve with generative models. CVPR work centres on certified training, including linear bound propagation and covariance propagation, plus PointGuard for point clouds, while ICRA covered reachability of closed-loop systems including Reachable Polyhedral Marching.

9 NNV papers accepted in February 2021, at CVPR 2021, ICRA 2021 and PLDI 2021.

CVPR 2021 4 papers

  • CVPR 2021 Certified training Achieving Robustness in Classification Using Optimal Transport With Hinge Regularization
    Mathieu Serrurier, Franck Mamalet, Alberto González-Sanz, Thibaut Boissin, Jean-Michel Loubes, Eustasio del Barrio

    Learns 1-Lipschitz binary and multi-class classifiers with a hinge-regularized Kantorovich-Rubinstein loss grounded in optimal transport, which yields a certifiable robustness bound; experiments show the guarantees hold without significant accuracy loss, and adversarial examples become visually meaningful.

  • CVPR 2021 Probabilistic certificationBeyond ℓp input properties PointGuard: Provably Robust 3D Point Cloud Classification
    Hongbin Liu, Jinyuan Jia, Neil Gong

    PointGuard certifies 3D point cloud classifiers against adversarially modified, added or deleted points by majority voting over random point subsamples; the derived bound on the number of tolerated points is proven tight, with evaluation on ModelNet40 and ScanNet.

  • CVPR 2021 Certified trainingProbabilistic certification Simpler Certified Radius Maximization by Propagating Covariances
    Xingjian Zhen, Rudrasis Chakraborty, Vikas Singh

    Maximizes the certified radius of smoothed classifiers by propagating the covariance of the Gaussian smoothing distribution through the network instead of Monte Carlo sampling, giving runtime savings on moderately deep networks for CIFAR-10, ImageNet and Places365 at a small accuracy cost.

  • CVPR 2021 Certified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretation Towards Evaluating and Training Verifiably Robust Neural Networks
    Zhaoyang Lyu, Minghao Guo, Tong Wu, Guodong Xu, Kehuan Zhang, Dahua Lin

    Proves CROWN is always tighter than interval bound propagation with suitable bounding lines, proposes linear bound propagation (LBP) to verify large networks with lower verified error, and a ParamRamp activation that improves verified robustness on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and Tiny-ImageNet.

PLDI 2021 3 papers

  • PLDI 2021 Beyond feed-forward nets Fast and precise certification of transformers
    Gregory Bonaert, Dimitar I. Dimitrov, Maximilian Baader, Martin Vechev

    DeepT certifies Transformer networks via abstract interpretation with a Multi-norm Zonotope domain covering ℓ1 and ℓ2 perturbations, including transformers for softmax and dot product; it certifies radii 28x larger than prior work and handles synonym attacks on long word sequences.

  • PLDI 2021 Repair & patching Provable repair of deep neural networks
    Matthew Sotoudeh, Aditya V. Thakur

    Repairs a DNN so it provably satisfies a specification: Provable Point Repair finds minimal repairs for finite point sets with any activation, and Provable Polytope Repair handles convex polytopes for piecewise-linear networks, both reduced to linear programming via a Decoupled DNN architecture.

  • PLDI 2021 Beyond ℓp input properties Robustness certification with generative models
    Matthew Mirman, Alexander Hägele, Pavol Bielik, Timon Gehr, Martin Vechev

    GenProve certifies classifiers against semantic image transformations produced by generative models, such as changing age or hair colour, by capturing infinite non-convex sets of activations and distributions over them, giving sound deterministic and probabilistic guarantees on realistic networks.

ICRA 2021 2 papers

  • ICRA 2021 NN control systems Efficient Reachability Analysis of Closed-Loop Systems with Neural Network Controllers
    Michael Everett, Golnaz Habibi, Jonathan P. How

    Computes forward reachable sets of closed-loop systems with neural network controllers through a linear programming formulation with input set partitioning and support for measurement and process noise; it gives 10x less conservatism in half the computation time of prior methods, shown on a quadrotor.

  • ICRA 2021 NN control systemsComplete verification Reachable Polyhedral Marching (RPM): A Safety Verification Algorithm for Robotic Systems with Deep Neural Network Components
    Joseph A. Vincent, Mac Schwager

    Reachable Polyhedral Marching computes exact forward and backward reachable sets of ReLU networks over multiple steps by incrementally enumerating polyhedral input cells, with early exit on unsafe cells; on ACAS Xu it finds unsafe actions many times faster than the fastest existing method.
