April 2024

IJCAI 2024 decisions: verification of parameterised neural-symbolic multi-agent systems against a bounded CTL fragment, and Truth Table Net, convolutional networks that convert into compact CNF Boolean circuits for fast exact robustness verification.

2 NNV papers accepted in April 2024, at IJCAI 2024.

  • IJCAI 2024 NN control systems Formal Verification of Parameterised Neural-symbolic Multi-agent Systems
    Panagiotis Kouvaros, Elena Botoeva, Cosmo De Bonis-Campbell

    Verifies multi-agent systems with arbitrarily many agents, each with a neural network perception unit and programmed action selection, against a bounded CTL fragment, using an abstraction that reduces the problem to Neural Interpreted Systems with a bounded number of agents.

  • IJCAI 2024 Certified training Truth Table Net: Scalable, Compact & Verifiable Neural Networks with a Dual Convolutional Small Boolean Circuit Networks Form
    Adrien Benamira, Thomas Peyrin, Trevor Yap, Tristan Guérand, Bryan Hooi

    TTnet builds convolutional networks from Learning Truth Table filters that convert exactly into compact CNF Boolean circuits, making formal verification fast; it reaches 41% ImageNet accuracy and performs exact robustness verification in 10ms versus 100s for traditional DNN solvers.
