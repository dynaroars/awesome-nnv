    Alaa Anani, Tobias Lorenz, Bernt Schiele, Mario Fritz, Jonas Fischer

    Certified Circuits wrap any circuit discovery method with randomized data subsampling to certify that component inclusion is stable under bounded edits to the concept dataset; on ResNet, ViT and GPT-2, circuits reach up to 56% higher accuracy with up to 80% fewer components.

IJCAI 2026 7 papers

  • IJCAI 2026 Complete verificationOther ExLipBaB: Exact Lipschitz Constant Computation for Piecewise Linear Neural Networks
    Tom A. Splittgerber

    ExLipBaB generalizes the LipBaB algorithm to compute exact Lipschitz constants of arbitrary continuous piecewise linear networks under p-norms, covering ReLU, LeakyReLU, GroupSort, MaxMin, FullSort and MaxPool, for uses such as benchmarking approximations and robustness guarantees for small models.

  • IJCAI 2026 Certified training Leveraging Over-Parameterization to Improve the Verifiability of Neural Networks
    Andrea Gimelli, Luca Oneto, Armando Tacchella

    Shows that over-parameterization can reduce neuron instability, a key factor in verification cost, so wider networks can become easier to verify; experiments suggest it may help reconcile the long-standing trade-off between generalization and verifiability of neural networks.

  • IJCAI 2026 Complete verification Lookahead Branching for Neural Network Verification
    Liam Davis, Duo Zhou, Huan Zhang, Guy Katz, Clark Barrett, Haoze Wu

    Adds lookahead branching to branch-and-bound neural network verifiers, framing the FSB heuristic as a special case and deriving extra lemmas that speed up search; implemented in Marabou and α-β-CROWN, it gives consistent speedups and up to 57% more solved instances.

  • IJCAI 2026 NN control systemsProbabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets Probabilistic Verification of Recurrent Neural Networks for Single and Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning
    Luca Marzari, Enrico Marchesini

    RNN-ProVe estimates the probability of undesired behavior in recurrent RL policies by sampling the hidden states feasible under the trained policy and attaching statistical error bounds, giving high-confidence guarantees in partially observable single-agent and cooperative multi-agent tasks.

  • IJCAI 2026 Formal explanationsBeyond feed-forward nets Semifactual Explanations for GNN-based Classification: Formal Foundations, Complexity and Computation
    Gianvincenzo Alfano, Sergio Greco, Domenico Mandaglio, Francesco Parisi, Reza Shahbazian, Irina Trubitsyna

    Studies semifactual explanations for GNN classifiers, the maximal subgraph perturbations that leave a prediction unchanged; analyzes the computational complexity of related reasoning and optimization problems and proposes a learning architecture that computes high-quality semifactuals.

  • IJCAI 2026 Beyond feed-forward netsComplete verification Synthesis and Verification of Transformer Programs
    Hongjian Jiang, Matthew Hague, Philipp Rümmer, Anthony W. Lin

    Verifies C-RASP transformer programs by reducing them to Lustre synchronous dataflow programs checked with SMT-based model checkers, and learns C-RASPs from examples via local search; demonstrated on transformer program minimization and constrained learning from partial specifications.

  • IJCAI 2026 Quantized & bit-level The Complexity of Verifying Feedforward Neural Networks in Quantised Settings
    Eric Alsmann, Martin Lange, Marco Sälzer

    Maps the complexity of verifying feedforward networks with rational, quantised and dynamically quantised weights under linear-programming and bit-vector specifications: fixed-precision quantised verification is NP-complete for both, and upper bounds are given for dynamically quantised networks with bit-vector specifications.

  • CAV 2026 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation Precise Verification of Transformers Through ReLU-Catalyzed Abstraction Refinement (arXiv)
    Hengjie Liu, Zhenya Zhang, Jianjun Zhao

    Verifies transformers more precisely by expressing self-attention dot products as ReLU-based non-linear bounds, then reusing ReLU convex relaxations in rule-based and optimization-based frameworks; on sentiment-analysis robustness properties it improves precision over the baseline at an acceptable efficiency cost.

  • CAV 2026 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation QAV-FT: Quadratic Approximation-Based Neural Network Verification via Fourier Series and Taylor Truncation
    Han Wang, Xuyang Ding, Ying Xie, Yakun Sheng

    QAV-FT verifies networks with high-frequency activations such as Swish, GELU and Mish using Fourier-based quadratic abstractions and second-order propagation with Taylor remainder error control, reaching an average certification accuracy of 96.8% on MNIST-FC and other benchmarks.

  • CAV 2026 Global & relational propertiesOther The Rocq-NN-Roll Prover: Soundly Verifying Hyperproperties of Neural Networks in Rocq
    Andrei Aleksandrov, Malte Jackisch, Kim Völlinger

    Rocq-NN-Roll is a formally verified prover, built in Rocq, for rational-valued piecewise-affine neural networks that checks properties including hyperproperties by reducing them to linear inequalities over polyhedral regions, giving the first automated network proof support in an interactive theorem prover.

  • CAV 2026 Other VNN-LIB 2.0: Rigorous Foundations for Neural Network Verification
    Ann Roy, Allen Antony, Andrea Gimelli, Matthew L. Daggitt

    Gives VNN-LIB 2.0 formal foundations: a network theory that abstracts the semantics needed from model formats such as ONNX, plus a more expressive query language with formal syntax, type system and semantics, all mechanised in the Agda theorem prover.
