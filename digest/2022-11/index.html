November 2022

AAAI 2023 decisions: formal explanations stand out, with background knowledge for shorter explanations and a feature relevancy algorithm. Other work covers CertiFair for global fairness of ReLU classifiers, confidence-aware training of smoothed classifiers, pointwise certification against poisoning, and control policies with reach-avoid guarantees for stochastic systems.

6 NNV papers accepted in November 2022, at AAAI 2023.

  • AAAI 2023 Global & relational properties CertiFair: A Framework for Certified Global Fairness of Neural Networks
    Haitham Khedr, Yasser Shoukry

    CertiFair is a sound and complete verifier for global individual fairness of ReLU classifiers under distance-based similarity, returning counterexamples when violated, plus a fairness loss for provably fair training; global individual fairness improves by 96% without significant accuracy loss.

  • AAAI 2023 Probabilistic certificationCertified training Confidence-Aware Training of Smoothed Classifiers for Certified Robustness
    Jongheon Jeong, Seojin Kim, Jinwoo Shin

    Training method for randomized-smoothing classifiers that uses accuracy under Gaussian noise as a per-sample proxy for robustness, filtering out samples unlikely to benefit from the adversarial objective; consistently improves certified l2 robustness over earlier training methods.

  • AAAI 2023 Formal explanations Eliminating the Impossible, Whatever Remains Must Be True: On Extracting and Applying Background Knowledge in the Context of Formal Explanations
    Jinqiang Yu, Alexey Ignatiev, Peter J. Stuckey, Nina Narodytska, João Marques-Silva

    Uses background knowledge extracted from data with rule induction to make formal explanations of ML predictions shorter for why questions and more accurate for why-not questions, dropping the assumption that features are independent and uniformly distributed.

  • AAAI 2023 Other Enhancing the Antidote: Improved Pointwise Certifications against Poisoning Attacks
    Shijie Liu, Andrew C. Cullen, Paul Montague, Sarah M. Erfani, Benjamin Rubinstein

    Pointwise certification against poisoning attacks that modify a bounded number of training samples, built on differential privacy and the Sampled Gaussian Mechanism; the certified robustness guarantees are more than twice as large as those of earlier certifications.

  • AAAI 2023 NN control systems Learning Control Policies for Stochastic Systems with Reach-Avoid Guarantees
    Dorde Zikelic, Mathias Lechner, Thomas A. Henzinger, Krishnendu Chatterjee

    Learns controllers for discrete-time nonlinear stochastic systems with infinite-horizon probabilistic reach-avoid guarantees, using neural reach-avoid supermartingales as certificates; supports learning policies from scratch, verifying fixed policies or fine-tuning, validated on 3 stochastic RL tasks.

  • AAAI 2023 Formal explanations Solving Explainability Queries with Quantification: The Case of Feature Relevancy
    Xuanxiang Huang, Yacine Izza, João Marques-Silva

    Algorithm for the feature relevancy problem, deciding whether a feature can appear in some explanation of a prediction, applicable to any classifier meeting minor requirements; on random forests it outperforms QBF solvers by orders of magnitude.
