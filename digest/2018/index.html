December 2018

ICLR 2019 5

ICLR 2019 decisions: mixed integer programming gives exact adversarial accuracy for piecewise-linear networks, while training for ReLU stability makes verification faster. Other directions include boosting robustness certification, statistical robustness estimates via multi-level splitting, and verification of nonlinear specifications such as energy conservation in learned dynamics.

AAAI 2019 5

AAAI 2019 decisions: bound propagation grows with CNN-Cert and RecurJac's Jacobian bounds, alongside verification of RNN-based agent-environment systems. Abduction-based explanations and inverse abstraction of neural network pre-images via symbolic interpolation point toward explanation and interpretable analysis.

POPL 2019 1

POPL 2019 decisions: DeepPoly, an abstract domain combining floating-point polyhedra and intervals with transformers for ReLU, sigmoid, tanh and maxpool, certifies deep neural networks and, with trace partitioning, robustness to rotations.

September 2018

NeurIPS 2018 8

NeurIPS 2018 decisions: bound propagation and abstract interpretation lead, with CROWN for general activations, DeepZ, symbolic-interval safety analysis and semidefinite relaxations. A unified view of piecewise-linear verification brings a benchmark comparison, while Lipschitz estimation with AutoLip and SeqLip, Lipschitz-margin training and scaled provable adversarial defenses advance certified robustness.
