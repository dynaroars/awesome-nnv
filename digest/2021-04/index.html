April 2021

CAV 2021 and IJCAI 2021 decisions: complete verification and tooling lead, with DNNV, PEREGRiNN, DeepSplit and dependency-based branching. Verification extends to recurrent networks with Prover, binarized networks with BDD4BNN and programs invoking neural networks, while Verisig 2.0 and barrier certificates address controllers. NNrepair and robust explanations for NLP models round out the month.

15 NNV papers accepted in April 2021, at CAV 2021 and IJCAI 2021.

CAV 2021 9 papers

  • CAV 2021 NN control systems An Iterative Scheme of Safe Reinforcement Learning for Nonlinear Systems via Barrier Certificate Generation
    Zhengfeng Yang, Yidan Zhang, Wang Lin, Xia Zeng, Xiaochao Tang, Zhenbing Zeng, Zhiming Liu

    SRLBC synthesizes safe DNN controllers for nonlinear systems by alternating deep RL training with a verifier that computes a maximal safe initial region and barrier certificate via polynomial abstraction and bilinear matrix inequalities, handling systems up to dimension 12.

  • CAV 2021 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation Automated Safety Verification of Programs Invoking Neural Networks
    Maria Christakis, Hasan Ferit Eniser, Holger Hermanns, Jörg Hoffmann, Yugesh Kothari, Jianlin Li, Jorge A. Navas, Valentin Wüstholz

    Verifies system-level safety of programs that call neural networks by tightly integrating program analysis and network analysis in an abstract interpretation framework, evaluated on 26 variants of a restricted autonomous-driving benchmark.

  • CAV 2021 Quantized & bit-level BDD4BNN: A BDD-Based Quantitative Analysis Framework for Binarized Neural Networks
    Yedi Zhang, Zhe Zhao, Guangke Chen, Fu Song, Taolue Chen

    BDD4BNN encodes binarized neural networks into Binary Decision Diagrams through cardinality constraints over blocks, enabling precise quantitative robustness analysis and interpretability of BNNs, with a prototype tool showing effectiveness and efficiency.

  • CAV 2021 Complete verificationOther DNNV: A Framework for Deep Neural Network Verification
    David Shriver, Sebastian Elbaum, Matthew B. Dwyer

    DNNV standardizes input and output formats for DNN verifiers, offers a DSL for properties, and applies simplification and reduction operations so more verifiers can run existing benchmarks, raising benchmark support from 30% to 74%.

  • CAV 2021 Repair & patching NNrepair: Constraint-Based Repair of Neural Network Classifiers
    Muhammad Usman, Divya Gopinath, Youcheng Sun, Yannic Noller, Corina S. Pasareanu

    NNrepair repairs neural network classifiers by fault localization followed by constraint solving to modify parameters at an intermediate or last layer; on MNIST and CIFAR-10 it improves accuracy by 45.56 points on poisoned and 10.40 on adversarial data.

  • CAV 2021 Complete verification PEREGRiNN: Penalized-Relaxation Greedy Neural Network Verifier
    Haitham Khedr, James Ferlez, Yasser Shoukry

    PEREGRiNN verifies polytopic input-output specifications of ReLU networks using a convex solver both as feasibility checker and to penalize relaxation, pushing large relaxations toward the input; it verifies more properties faster on an MNIST robustness suite.

  • CAV 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond linear output properties Robustness Verification of Semantic Segmentation Neural Networks Using Relaxed Reachability
    Hoang-Dung Tran, Neelanjana Pal, Patrick Musau, Diego Manzanas Lopez, Nathaniel Hamilton, Xiaodong Yang, Stanley Bak, Taylor T. Johnson

    Verifies robustness of semantic segmentation networks in NNV via a relaxed reachability method that solves only a chosen share of LPs and supports up-sampling layers, measuring robust pixel percentage and robust IoU on M2NIST with reduced verification time.

  • CAV 2021 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation Scalable Polyhedral Verification of Recurrent Neural Networks
    Wonryong Ryou, Jiayu Chen, Mislav Balunovic, Gagandeep Singh, Andrei Marian Dan, Martin Vechev

    Prover, a verifier for recurrent neural networks, computes polyhedral abstractions of nonlinear recurrent updates via sampling, optimization and Fermat's theorem, refining them by gradient descent; it certifies RNNs for vision, speech (with abstractions of the preprocessing pipeline) and motion-sensor classification beyond prior tools.

  • CAV 2021 NN control systems Verisig 2.0: Verification of Neural Network Controllers Using Taylor Model Preconditioning
    Radoslav Ivanov, Taylor J. Carpenter, James Weimer, Rajeev Alur, George J. Pappas, Insup Lee

    Verisig 2.0 checks closed-loop systems with tanh/sigmoid neural network controllers using Taylor-model reachability with preconditioning and shrink wrapping, parallelized for larger networks; on 10 benchmarks it is more accurate and faster than three tools, with speed-ups up to 21x and 268x.

IJCAI 2021 6 papers

  • IJCAI 2021 Complete verification DEEPSPLIT: An Efficient Splitting Method for Neural Network Verification via Indirect Effect Analysis
    Patrick Henriksen, Alessio Lomuscio

    DeepSplit is a complete verifier for ReLU networks based on symbolic interval propagation that picks split nodes by their indirect effect on successor relaxations and encodes splits efficiently in linear programming, giving 1 to 2 orders of magnitude speedups.

  • IJCAI 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationComplete verification Efficient Neural Network Verification via Layer-based Semidefinite Relaxations and Linear Cuts
    Ben Batten, Panagiotis Kouvaros, Alessio Lomuscio, Yang Zheng

    Verifies local robustness of neural networks with a layer-based semidefinite relaxation combined with linear cuts and chordal graph decompositions, provably tighter than prior approaches and an order of magnitude faster than earlier SDP methods.

  • IJCAI 2021 Formal explanations On Guaranteed Optimal Robust Explanations for NLP Models
    Emanuele La Malfa, Rhiannon Michelmore, Agnieszka M. Zbrzezny, Nicola Paoletti, Marta Kwiatkowska

    Computes optimal robust explanations for NLP neural networks as word subsets that guarantee unchanged prediction under bounded embedding perturbations of the other words, using implicit hitting sets and maximum universal subsets, evaluated on SST, Twitter and IMDB.

  • IJCAI 2021 Formal explanations Probabilistic Sufficient Explanations
    Eric Wang, Pasha Khosravi, Guy Van den Broeck

    Defines probabilistic sufficient explanations, the simplest feature subsets that give strong probabilistic guarantees the classifier behaves the same under the data distribution, found with probabilistic circuits and expected predictions; compared favourably with Anchors and logical explanations.

  • IJCAI 2021 Complete verification Towards Scalable Complete Verification of Relu Neural Networks via Dependency-based Branching
    Panagiotis Kouvaros, Alessio Lomuscio

    Complete verification of ReLU networks that branches on ReLU states according to dependencies between nodes, giving MILP sub-problems with fewer integer constraints; on the fully connected networks of the first verification competition it reports 145% gains over prior complete methods.

  • IJCAI 2021 NN control systems Verifying Reinforcement Learning up to Infinity
    Edoardo Bacci, Mirco Giacobbe, David Parker

    Verifies time-unbounded safety of neural network controllers for dynamical systems with abstract interpretation that builds template polyhedra via MILP and interval arithmetic, yielding safe invariant overapproximations of the reach set for linear, piecewise linear and nonlinear dynamics.
