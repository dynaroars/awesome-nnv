April 2017

CAV 2017 decisions: complete verification arrives with Reluplex, an SMT solver extending the simplex method to ReLU networks and evaluated on ACAS Xu collision-avoidance networks, alongside safety verification of deep neural networks.

2 NNV papers accepted in April 2017, at CAV 2017.

  • CAV 2017 Complete verification Reluplex: An Efficient SMT Solver for Verifying Deep Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Guy Katz, Clark Barrett, David L. Dill, Kyle Julian, Mykel J. Kochenderfer

    Reluplex verifies properties of deep ReLU networks, or finds counterexamples, by extending the simplex method to handle the non-convex ReLU; on ACAS Xu collision-avoidance networks it proves properties of networks an order of magnitude larger than before.

  • CAV 2017 Complete verification Safety Verification of Deep Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Xiaowei Huang, Marta Kwiatkowska, Sen Wang, Min Wu

    Verifies safety of feed-forward image classifiers with an SMT-based framework that discretizes a neighbourhood of an input under manipulations such as scratches, camera angle or lighting, and propagates the analysis layer by layer, guaranteeing to find adversarial examples if any exist; implemented with Z3.
