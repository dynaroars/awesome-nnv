September 2022

NeurIPS 2022 28

NeurIPS 2022 decisions: randomized smoothing leads, extended to graph neural networks, multi-label and few-shot models and model invariances. Lipschitz networks and certified training follow with LOT and CRT, while complete verification advances through GCP-CROWN cutting planes and VPN for polynomial networks.
