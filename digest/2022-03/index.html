March 2022

CVPR 2022 decisions: certified patch defenses built on vision transformers within smoothing-based certification, and 3DeformRS, which specialises randomized smoothing to spatial deformations of point clouds.

3 NNV papers accepted in March 2022, at CVPR 2022.

  • CVPR 2022 Beyond ℓp input propertiesProbabilistic certification 3DeformRS: Certifying Spatial Deformations on Point Clouds
    Gabriel Pérez S., Juan C. Pérez, Motasem Alfarra, Silvio Giancola, Bernard Ghanem

    3DeformRS certifies point cloud DNNs against parameterised spatial deformations such as rotation and twisting by specialising randomized smoothing to vector-field deformations; for PointNet under 3-degree z-rotation it gives a 3x larger certificate 20x faster than prior work.

  • CVPR 2022 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond ℓp input properties Certified Patch Robustness via Smoothed Vision Transformers
    Hadi Salman, Saachi Jain, Eric Wong, Aleksander Madry

    Uses vision transformers within smoothing-based certified patch defenses, exploiting their ability to handle heavily masked images to obtain better certified patch robustness, faster inference and no substantial drop in standard accuracy.

  • CVPR 2022 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond ℓp input properties Towards Practical Certifiable Patch Defense with Vision Transformer
    Zhaoyu Chen, Bo Li, Jianghe Xu, Shuang Wu, Shouhong Ding, Wenqiang Zhang

    Certifies image classifiers against patch attacks by placing a Vision Transformer inside Derandomized Smoothing, trained with progressive smoothed image modeling and using isolated band unit self-attention; on ImageNet it reaches 41.70% certified accuracy under 2% patches, against 26.00% before.
