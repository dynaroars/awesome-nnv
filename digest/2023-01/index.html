  • ICLR 2023 Other On the Perils of Cascading Robust Classifiers (arXiv)
    Ravi Mangal, Zifan Wang, Chi Zhang, Klas Leino, Corina S. Pasareanu, Matt Fredrikson

    Shows that the robustness certifier of cascading ensembles of certifiably robust networks is unsound and presents the CasA attack, which finds adversarial inputs for up to 88% of samples claimed certifiably robust and drops accuracy to as low as 11%.

  • ICLR 2023 Beyond feed-forward netsCertified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretation Efficient Certified Training and Robustness Verification of Neural ODEs (arXiv)
    Mustafa Zeqiri, Mark Niklas Müller, Marc Fischer, Martin Vechev

    Verifies and certifiably trains high-dimensional neural ODEs with GAINS, which combines ODE solvers with variable but discrete time steps, a graph representation of solver trajectories and an abstraction over that graph, cutting runtime from O(exp(d)+exp(T)) to O(d+T² log²T); evaluated on MNIST, FMNIST and PHYSIO-NET.

    Kigali · Poster #135, MH1-2-3-4 · Tue 2 May, 11:30–13:30 (Kigali time)

  • ICLR 2023 Probabilistic certification (Certified!!) Adversarial Robustness for Free! (arXiv)
    Nicholas Carlini, Florian Tramèr, Krishnamurthy Dvijotham, Leslie Rice, Mingjie Sun, Zico Kolter

    Achieves certified ℓ2 robustness with only off-the-shelf models through denoised smoothing that pairs a pretrained diffusion model with a standard classifier, certifying 71% ImageNet accuracy at radius 0.5, 14 points above the prior best, without fine-tuning.

    Kigali · Poster #140, MH1-2-3-4 · Tue 2 May, 16:30–18:30 (Kigali time)

  • ICLR 2023 oral 🎉 Certified training A Unified Algebraic Perspective on Lipschitz Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Alexandre Araujo, Aaron J. Havens, Blaise Delattre, Alexandre Allauzen, Bin Hu

    Unifies many 1-Lipschitz layer designs as analytical solutions of a common SDP condition and, with the Gershgorin circle theorem, derives new parameterizations called SDP-based Lipschitz Layers (SLL), which outperform previous approaches in certified robust accuracy on image classification.

    Kigali · Poster #29, MH1-2-3-4 · Tue 2 May, 16:30–18:30 (Kigali time) Kigali · Oral, Auditorium · Tue 2 May, 15:40–15:50 (Kigali time)

  • ICLR 2023 oral 🎉 Probabilistic certification Localized Randomized Smoothing for Collective Robustness Certification (arXiv)
    Jan Schuchardt, Tom Wollschläger, Aleksandar Bojchevski, Stephan Günnemann

    Localized randomized smoothing gives collective robustness certificates for multi-output models such as segmentation and node classification, scaling noise per input region by its importance to each output; it Pareto-dominates existing certificates in accuracy and certificate strength.

    Matthew Wicker, Juyeon Heo, Luca Costabello, Adrian Weller

    Certifies the robustness of gradient-based explanations against bounded input or parameter manipulation by propagating symbolic intervals through forward and backward passes; the differentiable bounds enable training, yielding explanation-robustness certificates on all six datasets tested.

  • ICLR 2023 oral 🎉 Certified training Certified Training: Small Boxes are All You Need (arXiv)
    Mark Niklas Müller, Franziska Eckert, Marc Fischer, Martin Vechev

    Trains certifiably robust networks with SABR, which propagates interval bounds over a small, carefully selected subset of the adversarial input region to approximate the worst-case loss with less approximation error, outperforming existing certified defenses in both standard and certified accuracy across perturbation sizes and datasets.

    Kigali · Poster #119, MH1-2-3-4 · Wed 3 May, 16:30–18:30 (Kigali time) Kigali · Oral, AD10 · Wed 3 May, 16:00–16:10 (Kigali time)

  • ICLR 2023 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond ℓp input propertiesBeyond linear output properties Certified Defences Against Adversarial Patch Attacks on Semantic Segmentation (arXiv)
    Maksym Yatsura, Kaspar Sakmann, N. Grace Hua, Matthias Hein, Jan Hendrik Metzen

  • ICLR 2023 Probabilistic certificationCertified training Denoising Masked Autoencoders Help Robust Classification (arXiv)

    Yi Zeng, Zhouxing Shi, Ming Jin, Feiyang Kang, Lingjuan Lyu, Cho-Jui Hsieh, Ruoxi Jia

ICRA 2023 2 papers

  • ICRA 2023 NN control systems One-Shot Reachability Analysis of Neural Network Dynamical Systems
    Shaoru Chen, Victor M. Preciado, Mahyar Fazlyab

    Reachability analysis of neural network dynamical systems that verifies the unrolled system in one shot rather than recursively propagating bounds, proven to reduce compounding over-approximation error; the performance gap is characterised and demonstrated on a cart-pole system.

  • ICRA 2023 NN control systemsBound propagation & abstract interpretation ReachLipBnB: A branch-and-bound method for reachability analysis of neural autonomous systems using Lipschitz bounds
    Taha Entesari, Sina Sharifi, Mahyar Fazlyab

    ReachLipBnB computes reachable sets of neural networks and neural-network-controlled systems using directional Lipschitz bounds from a convex program inside branch-and-bound, with PCA on sampled trajectories to choose polyhedral directions; evaluated in open- and closed-loop settings.

USENIX Security 2023 1 paper

  • USENIX Security 2023 Probabilistic certification DiffSmooth: Certifiably Robust Learning via Diffusion Models and Local Smoothing (arXiv)
    Jiawei Zhang, Zhongzhu Chen, Huan Zhang, Chaowei Xiao, Bo Li

    DiffSmooth certifies classifiers by purifying inputs with diffusion models and then applying local smoothing, backed by analysis bounding recovered instances near the original; it raises certified ImageNet accuracy at ℓ2 radius 1.5 from 36.0% to 53.0%.
