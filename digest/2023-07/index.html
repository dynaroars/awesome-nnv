ASE 2023, ICCV 2023 and S&P 2024 decisions: faster verification through network reduction and HOBAT's batch verification of homogeneous networks, Text-CRS certification against word-level textual attacks, TrajPAC for pedestrian trajectory prediction, and disaggregated measures of certified robustness.

7 NNV papers accepted in July 2023, at ACL 2023, ASE 2023, DAC 2023, ICCV 2023 and S&P 2024. 2 of them are placed by conference date, as the notification date is not recorded.

S&P 2024 2 papers

  • S&P 2024 Probabilistic certification It's Simplex! Disaggregating Measures to Improve Certified Robustness
    Andrew C. Cullen, Paul Montague, Shijie Liu, Sarah M. Erfani, Benjamin Rubinstein

    Argues that aggregate metrics hide per-sample behaviour of certified robustness and proposes dataset-independent and dataset-dependent analyses based on the output space of certified models; these lead to new certification approaches that certify 9% more samples at noise scale σ = 1.

  • S&P 2024 Beyond ℓp input propertiesProbabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets Text-CRS: A Generalized Certified Robustness Framework against Textual Adversarial Attacks
    Xinyu Zhang, Hanbin Hong, Yuan Hong, Peng Huang, Binghui Wang, Zhongjie Ba, Kui Ren

    Text-CRS certifies text classifiers against synonym substitution, word reordering, insertion and deletion via randomized smoothing, modelling each word-level operation as permutation plus embedding transformation with tailored noise distributions; it also gives the first certified accuracy benchmark for these four operations.

ASE 2023 2 papers

  • ASE 2023 Other Expediting Neural Network Verification via Network Reduction
    Yuyi Zhong, Ruiwei Wang, Siau-Cheng Khoo

    Speeds up neural network verification by removing stable ReLU neurons and converting networks into sequential ReLU and affine layers before verification; applied with α,β-crown, VeriNet and PRIMA, it shrinks networks, accelerates verification and lets tools handle more architectures.

  • ASE 2023 Other HOBAT: Batch Verification for Homogeneous Structural Neural Networks
    Jingyang Li, Guoqiang Li

    HOBAT verifies batches of structurally homogeneous neural networks, such as candidates produced by continual learning, by abstracting same-position neurons across the batch into one neuron and refining iteratively; combined with bound propagation, it significantly improves on the vanilla approach.

ICCV 2023 1 paper

  • ICCV 2023 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward netsBeyond linear output properties TrajPAC: Towards Robustness Verification of Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction Models
    Liang Zhang, Nathaniel Xu, Pengfei Yang, Gaojie Jin, Cheng-Chao Huang, Lijun Zhang

    TrajPAC verifies robustness of pedestrian trajectory prediction models with a probably approximately correct (PAC) framework, after formally defining label and pure robustness; it finds counterexamples and evaluates Trajectron++, MemoNet, AgentFormer and MID on ETH/UCY and Stanford Drone scenes.
