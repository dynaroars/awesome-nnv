  • ECCV 2026 Certified trainingBeyond linear output properties Fast and Flexible Robustness Certificates for Semantic Segmentation (arXiv)
    Thomas Massena, Corentin Friedrich, Franck Mamalet, Mathieu Serrurier

    Builds semantic segmentation networks with built-in Lipschitz constraints and a framework that generalizes robustness certificates to segmentation, computing worst-case performance under ℓ2 attacks for many measures; it reaches competitive pixel accuracy on Cityscapes and certifies about 600 times faster than randomized smoothing with comparable certificates.

    Malmö · Poster #429, ExHall · Thu 10 Sep, 16:30–18:30 (Malmö time)

  • ECCV 2026 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation Pay Attention to Attention Distribution: A New Local Lipschitz Bound for Transformers (arXiv)
    Nikolay Yudin, Sergei Kudriashov, Alexander Gaponov, Maxim V. Rakhuba

    Derives a tighter upper bound on the local Lipschitz constant of dot-product self-attention from refined closed-form bounds on the softmax Jacobian's singular values, showing how the attention distribution shapes it, and uses this to build JaSMin, a lightweight regularizer controlling the local Lipschitz constant of each block and the whole transformer.

    Malmö · Poster #394, ExHall · Thu 10 Sep, 16:30–18:30 (Malmö time)

  • ECCV 2026 Beyond linear output propertiesBeyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation IoUCert: Robustness Verification for Anchor-Based Object Detectors (arXiv)
    Benedikt Brückner, Alejandro J. Mercado, Yanghao Zhang, Panagiotis Kouvaros, Alessio Lomuscio

    Verifies robustness of anchor-based object detectors in single-object settings with a coordinate transformation that avoids loose relaxations of box prediction and an Interval Bound Propagation method yielding optimal IoU bounds over anchor offsets, verifying tractable variants of SSD, YOLOv2 and YOLOv3 against various input perturbations.

    Malmö · Poster #199, ExHall · Fri 11 Sep, 10:30–12:30 (Malmö time)

IROS 2026 1 paper

  • IROS 2026 NN control systems Safety-Constrained Reinforcement Learning with Post-Training Reachability Verification for Robot Navigation
    Qisong He, Xinmiao Huang, Jinwei Hu, Zhuoyun Li, Yi Dong, Changshun Wu, Xiaowei Huang

    Trains robot navigation policies with CVaR-constrained TD3, then checks them by computing action reachable sets under bounded observation noise with Taylor Models; across ten scenarios it reaches 98.3% success and the highest safety verification rate, and transfers to a physical Jackal robot.
