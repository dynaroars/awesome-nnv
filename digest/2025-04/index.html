April 2025

CAV 2025, IJCAI 2025 and USENIX Security 2025 decisions: verification tools lead, with NeuralSAT, StarV, ModelVerification.jl and a Dafny-verified robustness certifier. Neural control work brings logarithmic neural certificates, deductive RL synthesis and KAN controllers with barrier certificates; theory covers floating-point universal approximation and PSPACE-completeness for quantized GNNs, alongside space explanations and FaVeR fairness repair.

15 NNV papers accepted in April 2025, at CAV 2025, IJCAI 2025 and USENIX Security 2025.

CAV 2025 8 papers

  • CAV 2025 Other A Formally Verified Robustness Certifier for Neural Networks
    James Tobler, Hira Taqdees Syeda, Toby Murray

    Implements and formally verifies in Dafny a certification function for globally-robust neural networks, after showing previous unverified implementations can be exploitably unsound and that approximations like power iteration do not guarantee soundness; describes specifications, design decisions and practical experience.

  • CAV 2025 NN control systems Deductive Synthesis of Reinforcement Learning Agents for Infinite Horizon Tasks
    Yuning Wang, He Zhu

    Synthesizes reinforcement learning agents that provably satisfy infinite-horizon temporal reach-avoid specifications by decomposing them into finite-horizon subtasks with separate policies, using formal verification to guarantee the composition; synthesized agents beat standard RL in performance and specification compliance.

  • CAV 2025 Quantized & bit-levelBound propagation & abstract interpretation Floating-Point Neural Networks are Provably Robust Universal Approximators
    Geonho Hwang, Wonyeol Lee, Yeachan Park, Sejun Park, Feras Saad

    Proves the first interval universal approximation theorem for floating-point neural networks, showing their interval abstract semantics can exactly capture the direct image map of any rounded target function; corollaries include existence of provably robust floating-point networks and a computational completeness result.

  • CAV 2025 Other ModelVerification.jl: A Comprehensive Toolbox for Formally Verifying Deep Neural Networks
    Tianhao Wei, Hanjiang Hu, Luca Marzari, Kai S. Yun, Peizhi Niu, Xusheng Luo, Changliu Liu

    Presents ModelVerification.jl, a toolbox collecting a suite of methods for formally verifying different types of DNNs against varied input-output specifications, aimed at giving developers and practitioners a single self-contained verification framework.

  • CAV 2025 Complete verification NeuralSAT: A High-Performance Verification Tool for Deep Neural Networks
    Hai Duong, ThanhVu Nguyen, Matthew B. Dwyer

    Presents the latest version of NeuralSAT, a DNN verification tool based on the DPLL(T) framework that supports many architectures and activation functions; after a New Participant Award and 4th place at VNN-COMP'23, it placed second at VNN-COMP'24.

  • CAV 2025 NN control systems Policy Verification in Stochastic Dynamical Systems Using Logarithmic Neural Certificates
    Thom Badings, Wietze Koops, Sebastian Junges, Nils Jansen

    Verifies neural network policies in discrete-time stochastic systems against reach-avoid specifications using learned neural certificates, introducing logarithmic reach-avoid supermartingales and weighted-norm Lipschitz bounds to shrink Lipschitz constants; it verifies specifications with probabilities as high as 99.9999%.

  • CAV 2025 Formal explanations Space Explanations of Neural Network Classification
    Faezeh Labbaf, Tomás Kolárik, Martin Blicha, Grigory Fedyukovich, Michael Wand, Natasha Sharygina

    Introduces space explanations, logic-based explanations of neural network classification that give provable guarantees over continuous input regions, generated with Craig interpolation and unsatisfiable cores; case studies of various sizes show them more meaningful than existing explanations.

  • CAV 2025 OtherNN control systemsProbabilistic certification StarV: A Qualitative and Quantitative Verification Tool for Learning-Enabled Systems
    Hoang-Dung Tran, Sung Woo Choi, Yuntao Li, Qing Liu, Hideki Okamoto, Bardh Hoxha, Georgios Fainekos

    Presents StarV, a star-reachability tool for verifying DNNs and learning-enabled cyber-physical systems, adding ProbStar reachability and ProbStar Temporal Logic for quantitative verification and a SparseImageStar representation for memory-efficient CNN and segmentation verification; it beats existing tools in speed, scalability and memory.

IJCAI 2025 6 papers

  • IJCAI 2025 Global & relational propertiesRepair & patching Efficient Counterexample-Guided Fairness Verification and Repair of Neural Networks Using Satisfiability Modulo Convex Programming
    Arya Fayyazi, Yifeng Xiao, Pierluigi Nuzzo, Massoud Pedram

    Verifies and repairs individual fairness in pre-trained neural networks with FaVeR, which uses counterexamples from satisfiability modulo convex programming to locate high-sensitivity neurons and adjust their weights; it repairs 100% of models with under 2.27% accuracy loss.

  • IJCAI 2025 Formal explanations Efficient and Rigorous Model-Agnostic Explanations
    João Marques-Silva, Jairo A. Lefebre-Lobaina, Maria Vanina Martinez

    Proposes efficient algorithms for symbolic, model-agnostic explanations that explain samples of a black-box model's behavior, keeping the rigor of logic-based explainability without needing access to model internals; experiments confirm the algorithms scale.

  • IJCAI 2025 NN control systems Formal Synthesis of Safe Kolmogorov-Arnold Network Controllers with Barrier Certificates
    Xiongqi Zhang, Ning Lv, Wang Lin, Zuohua Ding

    Synthesizes safe controllers and control barrier certificates using Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks instead of feed-forward networks, then prunes and symbolizes them so candidates can be formally verified with Satisfiability Modulo Theories; the KAN4CBC tool handles higher system dimensions with better efficiency.

  • IJCAI 2025 OtherBeyond feed-forward nets GraphProt: Certified Black-Box Shielding Against Backdoored Graph Models
    Xiao Yang, Yuni Lai, Kai Zhou, Gaolei Li, Jianhua Li, Hang Zhang

    Shields GNN graph classifiers against backdoor attacks with GraphProt, a certified black-box defense that filters topology and features, samples subgraphs, and takes a majority vote over subgraph predictions; it lowers attack success rates while preserving clean classification accuracy.

  • IJCAI 2025 Complete verification Neuron Similarity-Based Neural Network Verification via Abstraction and Refinement
    Yuehao Liu, Yansong Dong, Liang Zhao, Wensheng Wang, Cong Tian

    Speeds up DNN verification with ARVerifier, which merges similar neurons based on interval outputs into a smaller abstract network and refines on spurious counterexamples; on ACAS Xu and MNIST it cuts verification time by 11.61%, 18.70% and 12.20% for α,β-CROWN, Verinet and Marabou.

  • IJCAI 2025 Quantized & bit-levelBeyond feed-forward nets Verifying Quantized Graph Neural Networks is PSPACE-complete
    Marco Sälzer, François Schwarzentruber, Nicolas Troquard

    Shows that verifying quantized graph neural networks through the linear-constrained validity problem (LVP) is PSPACE-complete for reasonable activation functions, via a translation into a logical language, a proof system, and a matching hardness proof.
