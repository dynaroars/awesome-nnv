April 2020

CAV 2020 and IJCAI 2020 decisions: complete verification and tooling lead, with abstraction-refinement integrated into Marabou, faster geometric path enumeration, the NNV tool with ImageStar verification of large CNNs, and GDVB benchmark generation. IJCAI adds individual fairness, metamorphic testing with certified fairness mitigation for NLP models, and verifiable RNN-based policies for POMDPs.

8 NNV papers accepted in April 2020, at CAV 2020 and IJCAI 2020.

CAV 2020 5 papers

  • CAV 2020 Complete verification An Abstraction-Based Framework for Neural Network Verification
    Yizhak Yisrael Elboher, Justin Gottschlich, Guy Katz

    Shrinks neural networks by over-approximation so properties proven on the smaller abstract network hold for the original, refining with counterexample guidance on spurious results; integrated with Marabou, it significantly improves verification performance on larger networks.

  • CAV 2020 Complete verification Improved Geometric Path Enumeration for Verifying ReLU Neural Networks
    Stanley Bak, Hoang-Dung Tran, Kerianne Hobbs, Taylor T. Johnson

    Speeds up exact geometric path enumeration verification of ReLU networks through a series of optimizations, several algorithmically new, running sometimes hundreds of times faster on ACAS Xu and often fastest on harder instances, even beating over-approximating methods.

  • CAV 2020 OtherNN control systemsBound propagation & abstract interpretation NNV: The Neural Network Verification Tool for Deep Neural Networks and Learning-Enabled Cyber-Physical Systems
    Hoang-Dung Tran, Xiaodong Yang, Diego Manzanas Lopez, Patrick Musau, Luan Viet Nguyen, Weiming Xiang, Stanley Bak, Taylor T. Johnson

    NNV is a set-based verification tool for deep neural networks and learning-enabled cyber-physical systems, using polyhedra, star sets, zonotopes and abstract domains for exact and over-approximate reachability, with closed-loop analysis via CORA; case studies cover ACAS Xu and adaptive cruise control.

  • CAV 2020 Other Systematic Generation of Diverse Benchmarks for DNN Verification
    Dong Xu, David Shriver, Matthew B. Dwyer, Sebastian Elbaum

    GDVB generates diverse neural network verification benchmarks by systematically varying problem factors that affect verifier performance, offering richer and less biased sets of verification problems for comparing verifiers.

  • CAV 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Verification of Deep Convolutional Neural Networks Using ImageStars
    Hoang-Dung Tran, Stanley Bak, Weiming Xiang, Taylor T. Johnson

    Verifies robustness of large CNNs such as VGG16 and VGG19 on ImageNet with the ImageStar set representation, combining concrete image operations with linear programming for exact and over-approximate analysis in NNV; it is less conservative and faster than zonotope and polytope methods.

IJCAI 2020 3 papers

  • IJCAI 2020 Global & relational propertiesProbabilistic certification Individual Fairness Revisited: Transferring Techniques from Adversarial Robustness (arXiv)
    Samuel Yeom, Matt Fredrikson

    Finds, for a given model, a metric under which it is individually fair: introduces minimal metrics and characterizes model behavior with them, then applies randomized smoothing to make complex models individually fair under a weighted Lᵖ metric, giving interpretable fairness guarantees at little utility cost.

  • IJCAI 2020 Global & relational propertiesProbabilistic certification Metamorphic Testing and Certified Mitigation of Fairness Violations in NLP Models
    Pingchuan Ma, Shuai Wang, Jin Liu

    Tests NLP models for fairness violations with metamorphic testing, then defines (ε, k)-fairness and smooths predictions to give certified fairness guarantees. Flags thousands of discriminatory inputs in popular commercial NLP models and adds certification at modest cost.

  • IJCAI 2020 NN control systemsBeyond feed-forward nets Verifiable RNN-Based Policies for POMDPs Under Temporal Logic Constraints (arXiv)
    Steven Carr, Nils Jansen, Ufuk Topcu

    Extracts a finite-state controller from an RNN policy for POMDPs with an iterative quantized bottleneck insertion, verifies it against temporal logic specifications with formal tools, and uses verification diagnostics to adjust memory or retrain the RNN; it beats traditional POMDP synthesis by 3 orders of magnitude while staying within 2% of optimal values.
