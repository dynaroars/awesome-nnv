CVPR 2026 decisions: verification beyond ℓp perturbations, with Lipschitz optimization bounding homography-transformed pixels under 3D camera motion and robustness verification under dual perturbations.

2 NNV papers accepted in February 2026, at CVPR 2026.

  • CVPR 2026 Beyond ℓp input propertiesBound propagation & abstract interpretation Lipschitz Optimization for Formal Verification of Homographies (arXiv)
    Jean-Guillaume Durand, Panagiotis Kouvaros, Maxime Gariel, Alessio Lomuscio

    Verifies vision networks against 3D camera-motion perturbations by mapping camera pose to pixels and using Lipschitz optimization to bound homography-transformed pixel values; reports up to 89% speed-up and 7% tighter bounds than prior work, and exposes weaknesses in VNN-COMP benchmarks and a runway classifier.

  • CVPR 2026 highlight 🎉 Beyond ℓp input propertiesBound propagation & abstract interpretation Verifying Neural Network Robustness with Dual Perturbations
    Hai Duong, Lam Nguyen, Thanh Le, ThanhVu Nguyen

    Verifies robustness under dual perturbations that combine universal convolutional distortions over a continuous spatial space with independent pixel-level noise; on diverse benchmarks with 14,340 instances, VeriDou finds substantially more adversarial examples on networks that existing methods reported as highly robust.
