February 2023

CVPR 2023 and PLDI 2023 decisions: PLDI brings CRAFT's abstract interpretation of fixpoint iterators, APRNN's architecture-preserving repair and IVAN's incremental verification. CVPR covers verified training via abstraction, verification against latent-variable-model specifications and PointCert for point cloud classification.

6 NNV papers accepted in February 2023, at CVPR 2023 and PLDI 2023.

PLDI 2023 3 papers

  • PLDI 2023 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationBeyond feed-forward nets Abstract Interpretation of Fixpoint Iterators with Applications to Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Mark Niklas Müller, Marc Fischer, Robin Staab, Martin Vechev

    CRAFT over-approximates fixpoints of numerical iterators via abstract interpretation without joins, using a new CH-Zonotope domain; on monDEQ neural networks it is two orders of magnitude faster, one order more scalable and 25% higher in certified accuracy than prior work.

  • PLDI 2023 Repair & patching Architecture-Preserving Provable Repair of Deep Neural Networks
    Zhe Tao, Stephanie Nawas, Jacqueline Mitchell, Aditya V. Thakur

    APRNN provably repairs DNNs over V-polytope specifications by changing only parameters, possibly across multiple layers, in polynomial time, supporting piecewise-linear activations and convolutional, pooling and residual layers; it beats PRDNN and REASSURE on MNIST, ImageNet and ACAS Xu.

  • PLDI 2023 Complete verification Incremental Verification of Neural Networks
    Shubham Ugare, Debangshu Banerjee, Sasa Misailovic, Gagandeep Singh

    IVAN makes complete DNN verification incremental, reusing work from verifying the original network when it is updated, via new theory, data structures and algorithms; it achieves geometric mean speedups of 2.4x on MNIST and CIFAR10 and 3.8x on ACAS-XU.

CVPR 2023 · Vancouver, Canada 3 / 2,359 CVPR 2023 papers are NNV, by poster session

  • CVPR 2023 Beyond ℓp input propertiesBound propagation & abstract interpretation Efficient Verification of Neural Networks Against LVM-Based Specifications
    Harleen Hanspal, Alessio Lomuscio

    Verifies neural networks against specifications defined by latent variable models that capture pose, viewpoint and occlusion changes, by attaching an invertible encoding head so latent-space sets can be verified with little reconstruction overhead; scales largely independently of input dimension.

    Vancouver · Poster 371, West Building Exhibit Halls ABC · Tue 20 Jun, 10:30–12:00 (Vancouver time)

  • CVPR 2023 OtherBeyond ℓp input properties PointCert: Point Cloud Classification with Deterministic Certified Robustness Guarantees
    Jinghuai Zhang, Jinyuan Jia, Hongbin Liu, Neil Gong

    PointCert turns any point cloud classifier into one with deterministic certified robustness, keeping the label when fewer than a threshold number of points are added, deleted or modified; it outperforms probabilistic certified defenses on ModelNet and ScanObjectNN.

    Vancouver · Poster 121, West Building Exhibit Halls ABC · Wed 21 Jun, 10:30–12:00 (Vancouver time)

  • CVPR 2023 Certified training Boosting Verified Training for Robust Image Classifications via Abstraction
    Zhaodi Zhang, Zhiyi Xue, Yang Chen, Si Liu, Yueling Zhang, Jing Liu, Min Zhang

    Certified training that maps perturbed images to intervals before training, smoothing the loss landscape, and enables sound and complete black-box verification; it reduces verified errors by up to 95.64%, gives up to 602.50x speedup and scales to 138-million-parameter models.

    Vancouver · Poster 371, West Building Exhibit Halls ABC · Wed 21 Jun, 16:30–18:00 (Vancouver time)
