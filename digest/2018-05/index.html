May 2018

ICML 2018 and USENIX Security 2018 decisions: ReluVal's symbolic interval analysis and Fast-Lin and Fast-Lip certify ReLU networks much faster than Reluplex, while certified training takes shape through the convex outer adversarial polytope and differentiable abstract interpretation.

5 NNV papers accepted in May 2018, at ICML 2018, S&P 2018 and USENIX Security 2018. 1 of them is placed by conference date, as the notification date is not recorded.

ICML 2018 3 papers

  • ICML 2018 Certified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretation Differentiable Abstract Interpretation for Provably Robust Neural Networks
    Matthew Mirman, Timon Gehr, Martin Vechev

    Trains large neural networks that are certifiably robust to adversarial perturbations by making abstract interpretation differentiable, with several abstract transformers that balance efficiency and precision.

  • ICML 2018 Certified trainingBound propagation & abstract interpretation Provable Defenses against Adversarial Examples via the Convex Outer Adversarial Polytope (arXiv)
    Eric Wong, Zico Kolter

    Trains ReLU classifiers provably robust to norm-bounded perturbations by minimizing worst-case loss over a convex outer approximation of reachable activations, solving the dual LP as a backprop-like network; on MNIST, provable error stays below 5.8% at ℓ∞ ε=0.1.

  • ICML 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Towards Fast Computation of Certified Robustness for ReLU Networks (arXiv)
    Tsui-Wei Weng, Huan Zhang, Hongge Chen, Zhao Song, Cho-Jui Hsieh, Luca Daniel, Duane S. Boning, Inderjit S. Dhillon

    Fast-Lin and Fast-Lip certify lower bounds on the minimum adversarial distortion of ReLU networks by linear bounding of ReLUs or local Lipschitz bounds, running over 10,000 times faster than Reluplex, and prove approximation hardness for ℓ1 distortion.

USENIX Security 2018 1 paper

  • USENIX Security 2018 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Formal Security Analysis of Neural Networks using Symbolic Intervals (arXiv)
    Shiqi Wang, Kexin Pei, Justin Whitehouse, Junfeng Yang, Suman Jana

    ReluVal checks security properties of ReLU networks without SMT solvers, using parallelizable symbolic interval analysis to bound outputs. It is 200 times faster than Reluplex on average and verifies a property on which Reluplex timed out.
