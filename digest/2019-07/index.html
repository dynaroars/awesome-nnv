July 2019

CCS 2019 and ICCV 2019 decisions: interval bound propagation scales verified training to large image classifiers, and NPAQ quantitatively verifies binarized neural networks with PAC-style guarantees, applied to robustness, trojan attacks and fairness.

2 NNV papers accepted in July 2019, at CCS 2019 and ICCV 2019.

ICCV 2019 1 paper

  • ICCV 2019 Certified training Scalable Verified Training for Provably Robust Image Classification
    Sven Gowal, Krishnamurthy Dvijotham, Robert Stanforth, Rudy Bunel, Chongli Qin, Jonathan Uesato, Relja Arandjelovic, Timothy Arthur Mann, Pushmeet Kohli

    Shows that interval bound propagation, with a suitable loss and hyper-parameter schedule, trains large provably robust image classifiers that beat more complex methods on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and SVHN in verified accuracy, and verifies the largest model yet on downscaled ImageNet.

CCS 2019 1 paper

  • CCS 2019 Probabilistic certification Quantitative Verification of Neural Networks and Its Security Applications
    Teodora Baluta, Shiqi Shen, Shweta Shinde, Kuldeep S. Meel, Prateek Saxena

    NPAQ quantitatively verifies binarized neural networks by estimating how many inputs satisfy a logical property with PAC-style soundness guarantees, applied to quantifying adversarial robustness, trojan attack efficacy and fairness.
