November 2018

AAAI 2019 decisions: bound propagation grows with CNN-Cert and RecurJac's Jacobian bounds, alongside verification of RNN-based agent-environment systems. Abduction-based explanations and inverse abstraction of neural network pre-images via symbolic interpolation point toward explanation and interpretable analysis.

5 NNV papers accepted in November 2018, at AAAI 2019.

  • AAAI 2019 Formal explanations Abduction-Based Explanations for Machine Learning Models (arXiv)
    Alexey Ignatiev, Nina Narodytska, João Marques-Silva

    Computes cardinality- or subset-minimal explanations for any ML model by abductive reasoning, requiring only that the model be encoded as constraints decidable by an oracle; experiments on well-known datasets show the approach scales and produces high-quality explanations.

  • AAAI 2019 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation CNN-Cert: An Efficient Framework for Certifying Robustness of Convolutional Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Akhilan Boopathy, Tsui-Wei Weng, Pin-Yu Chen, Sijia Liu, Luca Daniel

    Certifies robustness of general convolutional networks with CNN-Cert, handling convolution, max-pooling, batch normalization, residual blocks and general activations, and generalizing Fast-Lin and CROWN; it is up to 17 and 11 times faster than those and 366 times faster than dual LP with similar or better bounds.

  • AAAI 2019 Other Inverse Abstraction of Neural Networks Using Symbolic Interpolation
    Sumanth Dathathri, Sicun Gao, Richard M. Murray

    Computes compact symbolic over- and under-approximations of neural network pre-images layer by layer via symbolic interpolation, enabling formal analysis, extraction of simple control laws and interpretable descriptions of inputs leading to unsafe outputs.

  • AAAI 2019 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation RecurJac: An Efficient Recursive Algorithm for Bounding Jacobian Matrix of Neural Networks and Its Applications (arXiv)
    Huan Zhang, Pengchuan Zhang, Cho-Jui Hsieh

    Computes upper and lower bounds on every element of a network's input Jacobian with the recursive RecurJac algorithm for a wide range of activations, yielding better local Lipschitz constants for robustness verification in polynomial time and characterizing the loss landscape, such as whether stationary points exist nearby.

  • AAAI 2019 NN control systemsBeyond feed-forward nets Verification of RNN-Based Neural Agent-Environment Systems
    Michael E. Akintunde, Andreea Kevorchian, Alessio Lomuscio, Edoardo Pirovano

    Verifies agent-environment systems whose stateful agent runs a ReLU recurrent network, using equivalences between recurrent and feed-forward networks on bounded traces to give a sound and complete procedure for properties in a simplified bounded LTL.
