S&P 2025 decisions: FairZK proves fairness of logistic regression and deep neural network models in zero-knowledge while keeping parameters secret.

1 NNV paper accepted in March 2025, at S&P 2025.

  • S&P 2025 Global & relational propertiesOther FairZK: A Scalable System to Prove Machine Learning Fairness in Zero-Knowledge
    Tianyu Zhang, Shen Dong, Oyku Deniz Kose, Yanning Shen, Yupeng Zhang

    Proves fairness of logistic regression and deep neural network models in zero-knowledge, keeping parameters secret, by bounding fairness from parameters and aggregated input information plus dedicated proof protocols. FairZK speeds up proving 3.1x-1789x and handles a 47-million-parameter model in 343 seconds.
