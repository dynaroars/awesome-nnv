July 2022

ASE 2022 and ECCV 2022 decisions: ECCV brought randomized smoothing work, including UniCR, ViP for patch attacks with vision transformers and LASSI for certified individual fairness, plus almost-orthogonal Lipschitz layers. ASE added QVIP, an ILP-based verifier for quantized neural networks, and provably tightest linear approximations for sigmoid-like networks.

9 NNV papers accepted in July 2022, at ASE 2022, DAC 2022 and ECCV 2022. 2 of them are placed by conference date, as the notification date is not recorded.

  • ECCV 2022 Certified training Almost-Orthogonal Layers for Efficient General-Purpose Lipschitz Networks (arXiv)
    Bernd Prach, Christoph H. Lampert

    Builds Lipschitz networks with almost-orthogonal layers, a rescaling-based weight parametrization that guarantees each fully-connected or convolutional layer has Lipschitz constant at most 1 without costly orthogonalization or inversion; certified robust accuracy on image classification is on par with most existing methods while being simpler and more broadly applicable.

  • ECCV 2022 Global & relational propertiesProbabilistic certification Latent Space Smoothing for Individually Fair Representations (arXiv)
    Momchil Peychev, Anian Ruoss, Mislav Balunovic, Maximilian Baader, Martin Vechev

    LASSI learns representations that certify individual fairness for high-dimensional data, capturing similar individuals in a generative latent space, training with adversarial learning and applying randomized smoothing; certified individual fairness on real image data rises by up to 90%.

  • ECCV 2022 Probabilistic certification UniCR: Universally Approximated Certified Robustness via Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Hanbin Hong, Binghui Wang, Yuan Hong

    Approximates randomized smoothing certificates for any input, classifier, ℓp perturbation and continuous noise distribution in UniCR, automating certification without case-by-case analysis and validating both the tightness of certified robustness and the optimality of the noise distribution; experiments show advantages over state-of-the-art certified defenses.

ASE 2022 2 papers

  • ASE 2022 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Provably Tightest Linear Approximation for Robustness Verification of Sigmoid-like Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Zhaodi Zhang, Yiting Wu, Si Liu, Jing Liu, Min Zhang

    Defines network-wise tightness for linear approximations of sigmoid-like activations in robustness verification and gives two efficient, provably tightest approximations; certified lower robustness bounds improve by up to 251.28%, with more precise results on convolutional networks.

  • ASE 2022 Quantized & bit-levelComplete verification QVIP: An ILP-based Formal Verification Approach for Quantized Neural Networks
    Yedi Zhang, Zhe Zhao, Guangke Chen, Fu Song, Min Zhang, Taolue Chen, Jun Sun

    QVIP verifies quantized neural networks with a sound and complete encoding into integer linear constraints solved off the shelf, applied to local robustness and maximum robustness radius; it is two orders of magnitude faster than prior methods and solves more tasks.
