June 2020

ICML 2020 and IROS 2020 decisions: randomized smoothing theory leads, covering label-flipping certificates, curse-of-dimensionality limits, sparsity-aware certificates for graphs and optimal smoothing distributions. LayerCert offers hierarchical exact verification, curvature bounds give second-order certificates, and verification reaches neural network control policies and robot state estimation, plus robust training against programmable string transformations.

11 NNV papers accepted in June 2020, at ICML 2020 and IROS 2020.

ICML 2020 · Virtual 10 / 1,084 ICML 2020 papers are NNV, by poster session

  • ICML 2020 Probabilistic certification Randomized Smoothing of All Shapes and Sizes (arXiv)
    Greg Yang, Tony Duan, J. Edward Hu, Hadi Salman, Ilya P. Razenshteyn, Jerry Li

    A general theory for randomized smoothing finds optimal smoothing distributions via the Wulff Crystal and two methods for robust radii of any distribution, improving certified ℓ1 accuracy; Banach space cotypes show limits on certifying ℓp perturbations in high dimensions.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 14 Jul, 14:00–14:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Probabilistic certification Certified Robustness to Label-Flipping Attacks via Randomized Smoothing (arXiv)
    Elan Rosenfeld, Ezra Winston, Pradeep Ravikumar, Zico Kolter

    Uses randomized smoothing over arbitrary functions to certify classifiers against data poisoning, instantiated as linear classifiers certified against label flipping per test point with a deterministic analytical bound, little extra runtime, and the first multi-class label-flipping certificate.

    Virtual · Poster · Tue 14 Jul, 16:00–16:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationCertified training Second-Order Provable Defenses against Adversarial Attacks (arXiv)
    Sahil Singla, Soheil Feizi

    Computes ℓ2 robustness certificates for networks with differentiable activations via convex optimization when Hessian eigenvalues are bounded, using a differentiable curvature bound also as a regularizer; CRT reaches 69.79%, 57.78% and 53.19% certified accuracy on MNIST versus about 45% for IBP.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 15 Jul, 12:00–12:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Other Black-box Certification and Learning under Adversarial Perturbations (arXiv)
    Hassan Ashtiani, Vinayak Pathak, Ruth Urner

    Studies learning and third-party black-box certification of classifiers under adversarial perturbations in a PAC-type semi-supervised framework, giving possibility and impossibility results for VC-classes and analysing certification with a limited query budget.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 15 Jul, 15:00–15:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Complete verification Hierarchical Verification for Adversarial Robustness (arXiv)
    Cong Han Lim, Raquel Urtasun, Ersin Yumer

    LayerCert performs exact ℓp robustness verification of ReLU networks by exploring activation regions hierarchically along the nested hyperplane arrangement induced by layers, provably solving fewer and smaller convex programs than GeoCert and reducing running time.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 15 Jul, 17:00–17:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Probabilistic certification Scalable Differential Privacy with Certified Robustness in Adversarial Learning (arXiv)
    NhatHai Phan, My T. Thai, Han Hu, Ruoming Jin, Tong Sun, Dejing Dou

    Preserves differential privacy in adversarial learning for deep neural networks while certifying robustness, by randomizing input and latent spaces, an adversarial objective based on DP post-processing, and stochastic batch training that scales to large networks and datasets.

    Virtual · Poster · Wed 15 Jul, 19:00–19:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Certified trainingBeyond ℓp input properties Robustness to Programmable String Transformations via Augmented Abstract Training (arXiv)
    Yuhao Zhang, Aws Albarghouthi, Loris D'Antoni

    Trains NLP models robust to user-defined string transformations such as insertions, deletions, substitutions and swaps, specified in a programmable language, by splitting specifications into parts handled by search and by abstraction; evaluated on AG and SST2.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 16 Jul, 13:00–13:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Probabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets Efficient Robustness Certificates for Discrete Data: Sparsity-Aware Randomized Smoothing for Graphs, Images and More (arXiv)
    Aleksandar Bojchevski, Johannes Klicpera, Stephan Günnemann

    A sparsity-aware randomized smoothing certificate for discrete data that is model-agnostic, tight and efficient, with cost independent of input dimension or category count; it certifies any Graph Neural Network against perturbations to graph structure and node attributes.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 16 Jul, 19:00–19:45 (UTC)

  • ICML 2020 Probabilistic certification Curse of Dimensionality on Randomized Smoothing for Certifiable Robustness (arXiv)
    Aounon Kumar, Alexander Levine, Tom Goldstein, Soheil Feizi

    Proves that for many i.i.d. smoothing distributions the largest certifiable ℓp radius shrinks with input dimension for p > 2, so Gaussian smoothing is optimal up to a constant factor; uniform ℓ1 and ℓ∞ smoothing scale even worse, with CIFAR experiments.

    Virtual · Poster · Thu 16 Jul, 22:00–22:45 (UTC)

  • IROS 2020 NN control systems Practical Verification of Neural Network Enabled State Estimation System for Robotics
    Wei Huang, Yifan Zhou, Youcheng Sun, James Sharp, Simon Maskell, Xiaowei Huang

    Verifies robustness of learning-enabled robot state estimation, where a neural network feeds a Bayes filter, by reducing it to labelled transition systems with payoffs and solving a constrained optimization; on a Kalman-filter WAMI vehicle tracking system it verifies robustness and finds counterexamples.
