December 2020

AAAI 2021 and ICSE 2021 decisions: certified training leads, with class-similarity-based verifiable training, SingleProp and Toeplitz-based Lipschitz regularization of convolutional layers. Other directions include certifying spatial robustness, SMT-based verification of quantized neural networks, reachability of Neural ODEs with stochastic guarantees and PROVERO's quantitative verification, plus certified defenses against data poisoning and malicious federated-learning clients.

11 NNV papers accepted in December 2020, at AAAI 2021 and ICSE 2021.

AAAI 2021 10 papers

  • AAAI 2021 Certified training Adaptive Verifiable Training Using Pairwise Class Similarity
    Shiqi Wang, Kevin Eykholt, Taesung Lee, Jiyong Jang, Ian M. Molloy

    Improves verifiable training by clustering similar classes and assigning robustness criteria by inter-cluster similarity, via an Inter-Group Robustness Prioritization loss or a neural decision tree of sub-classifiers; clean performance improves up to 9.63% on Fashion-MNIST and 30.89% on CIFAR10.

  • AAAI 2021 Certified training Adversarial Training and Provable Robustness: A Tale of Two Objectives (arXiv)
    Jiameng Fan, Wenchao Li

    Trains certifiably robust networks by jointly optimizing empirical (adversarial) and provable robustness objectives with a gradient-descent technique that removes bias in stochastic multi-gradients, with convergence analysis; it matches or beats prior work on ℓ∞ robustness, reaching 6.60% verified error on MNIST at ε=0.3 and 66.57% on CIFAR-10 at ε=8/255.

  • AAAI 2021 Beyond ℓp input propertiesBound propagation & abstract interpretation Efficient Certification of Spatial Robustness (arXiv)
    Anian Ruoss, Maximilian Baader, Mislav Balunovic, Martin Vechev

    Certifies robustness of vision models against vector field (spatial) transformations for the first time via model-agnostic convex relaxations that can be plugged into many neural network verifiers; experiments across architectures and datasets show effectiveness and scalability.

  • AAAI 2021 Certified training Fast Training of Provably Robust Neural Networks by SingleProp (arXiv)
    Akhilan Boopathy, Tsui-Wei Weng, Sijia Liu, Pin-Yu Chen, Gaoyuan Zhang, Luca Daniel

    SingleProp is a regularizer for training provably robust networks that needs only one extra forward propagation, making it cheaper than certified defenses that run certification during training; on MNIST and CIFAR-10 it trains faster with comparable certified accuracy.

  • AAAI 2021 OtherProbabilistic certification Intrinsic Certified Robustness of Bagging against Data Poisoning Attacks (arXiv)
    Jinyuan Jia, Xiaoyu Cao, Neil Gong

    Proves that bagging with any base learner predicts the same label when the number of modified, deleted or inserted training examples stays below a threshold, which is tight without assumptions on the learner; on MNIST it reaches 91.1% certified accuracy against 100 arbitrarily changed training examples.

  • AAAI 2021 Certified trainingOther On Lipschitz Regularization of Convolutional Layers using Toeplitz Matrix Theory
    Alexandre Araujo, Benjamin Négrevergne, Yann Chevaleyre, Jamal Atif

    Uses Toeplitz matrix theory to derive an upper bound on the Lipschitz constant of convolutional layers that is both tight and cheap to compute, and builds on it an algorithm for training Lipschitz-regularized convolutional neural networks.

  • AAAI 2021 NN control systemsProbabilistic certificationBeyond feed-forward nets On the Verification of Neural ODEs with Stochastic Guarantees (arXiv)
    Sophie A. Gruenbacher, Ramin M. Hasani, Mathias Lechner, Jacek Cyranka, Scott A. Smolka, Radu Grosu

    Verifies Neural ODEs with Stochastic Lagrangian Reachability, which builds a tight Reachtube using global optimization and gives confidence intervals on its bounds, avoiding the wrapping effect; a forward-mode adjoint sensitivity method speeds up gradients, and convergence rates are established.

  • AAAI 2021 Other Provably Secure Federated Learning against Malicious Clients (arXiv)
    Xiaoyu Cao, Jinyuan Jia, Neil Gong

    Makes federated learning provably secure against malicious clients by training multiple global models on random client subsets and taking a majority vote, with a tight bound on tolerated malicious clients; it reaches 88% certified accuracy on MNIST with 20 of 1,000 clients malicious.

  • AAAI 2021 Quantized & bit-levelComplete verification Scalable Verification of Quantized Neural Networks
    Thomas A. Henzinger, Mathias Lechner, Dorde Zikelic

    Shows that bit-exact verification of quantized neural networks with bit-vector specifications is PSPACE-hard, and proposes three techniques to scale SMT-based verification of such networks, giving speed-ups of up to three orders of magnitude over existing approaches.

  • AAAI 2021 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Tightening Robustness Verification of Convolutional Neural Networks with Fine-Grained Linear Approximation
    Yiting Wu, Min Zhang

    DeepCert tightens robustness verification of convolutional networks with sigmoid-like activations through finer-grained linear approximations; on LeNet and MNIST/CIFAR models it improves the certified lower bound by up to 286.28% and runs up to 1566.76 times faster than other verifiers.

ICSE 2021 1 paper

  • ICSE 2021 Probabilistic certification Scalable Quantitative Verification For Deep Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Teodora Baluta, Zheng Leong Chua, Kuldeep S. Meel, Prateek Saxena

    PROVERO, a quantitative verification framework for deep neural networks, runs tests until a probabilistic property is proven with formal guarantees, for deterministic or randomized DNNs; applied to adversarial robustness, it certifies large DNNs where existing verifiers are inconclusive, needing only black-box access.
