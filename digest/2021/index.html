January 2021

ICLR 2021 14USENIX Security 2021 1

ICLR 2021 and USENIX Security 2021 decisions: certified training leads, with Cayley-transform orthogonal layers, compositional architectures and detection-aware IBP. Complete verification speeds up with GPU-based LiRPA branch-and-bound and geometric projections, while a floating-point attack shows how to fool a complete verifier. Patch defenses such as BagCert and PatchGuard, and collective certificates for graph neural networks, also appear.
