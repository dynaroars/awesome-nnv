February 2020

CVPR 2020 decisions: verification moves beyond ℓp inputs, with robustness guarantees for CNN-RNN video classifiers under optical flow perturbations and Semantify-NN, which verifies robustness to hue, brightness, contrast and rotation through semantic perturbation layers.

2 NNV papers accepted in February 2020, at CVPR 2020.

  • CVPR 2020 Beyond feed-forward netsBound propagation & abstract interpretation Robustness Guarantees for Deep Neural Networks on Videos
    Min Wu, Marta Kwiatkowska

    Bounds the maximum safe radius of CNN-RNN video classifiers over optical flow perturbations, using Lipschitz-based discretisation and a cooperative two-player game solved anytime, with gradient search for upper bounds and admissible A* for lower bounds; evaluated on UCF101.

  • CVPR 2020 Beyond ℓp input propertiesBound propagation & abstract interpretation Towards Verifying Robustness of Neural Networks Against A Family of Semantic Perturbations
    Jeet Mohapatra, Tsui-Wei Weng, Pin-Yu Chen, Sijia Liu, Luca Daniel

    Semantify-NN verifies robustness to semantic perturbations such as hue, saturation, brightness, contrast and rotation by prepending semantic perturbation layers, so any ℓp-norm verifier applies; a refinement step tightens certificates beyond naive ℓp conversion across architectures and datasets.
