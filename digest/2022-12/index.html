ICSE 2023 decisions: fairness verification with Fairify, which checks individual fairness of neural networks with SMT, partitioning the input domain and pruning neurons to return certificates or counterexamples.

1 NNV paper accepted in December 2022, at ICSE 2023.

  • ICSE 2023 Global & relational propertiesComplete verification Fairify: Fairness Verification of Neural Networks
    Sumon Biswas, Hridesh Rajan

    Fairify verifies individual fairness of neural networks with SMT, partitioning the input domain and pruning neurons per partition via interval arithmetic and activation heuristics to return certificates or counterexamples; evaluated on 25 real-world networks from four sources.
