Monthly digest

What was new in neural-network verification each month since 2017: the NNV papers accepted that month at the tracked conferences, each with a short summary written from its abstract. A paper is placed in the month its acceptance was announced (the conference’s notification date, from the conference’s own pages), not the month the conference meets, because most papers are shared as soon as they are accepted. So a conference held early in a year, such as AAAI or ICLR, usually appears under the previous year. When a conference has several submission cycles and a paper’s cycle is not known, the paper is placed at the latest notification and marked as such.

The digest is paged by year, newest first, and kept as a record: papers are never removed from a month, and a paper added after its month (for example when a list appears late) says so.
