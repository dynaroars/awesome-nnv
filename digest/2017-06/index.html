OOPSLA 2017 decisions: FairSquare certifies fairness of decision-making programs by verifying probabilistic program properties.

1 NNV paper accepted in June 2017, at OOPSLA 2017.

  • OOPSLA 2017 Global & relational propertiesProbabilistic certification FairSquare: probabilistic verification of program fairness
    Aws Albarghouthi, Loris D'Antoni, Samuel Drews, Aditya V. Nori

    FairSquare automatically certifies that decision-making programs meet fairness definitions, encoded as probabilistic program properties, using a new technique for verifying probabilistic properties; it verifies fairness for programs beyond reach of existing program analysis tools.
