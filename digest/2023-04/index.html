April 2023

CAV 2023, ICML 2023 and IJCAI 2023 decisions: Lipschitz bounds and certified training are a main theme, alongside formal explanations for monotonic networks and planning policies. CAV adds NNV 2.0, QEBVerif for quantization error bounds and hybrid controller synthesis, while IJCAI defines #DNN-Verification for counting unsafe inputs.

19 NNV papers accepted in April 2023, at CAV 2023, ICML 2023 and IJCAI 2023.

ICML 2023 · Honolulu, United States 11 / 1,826 ICML 2023 papers are NNV, by poster session

  • ICML 2023 Repair & patching A Robust Optimisation Perspective on Counterexample-Guided Repair of Neural Networks (arXiv)
    David Boetius, Stefan Leue, Tobias Sutter

    Views counterexample-guided neural network repair as robust optimisation, proving termination for restricted models and disproving it in general; empirically shows common verifiers and falsifiers still suit repair, and gives a quadratic-programming repair algorithm for linear regression.

    Honolulu · Poster #618, Exhibit Hall 1 · Tue 25 Jul, 11:00–13:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Global & relational properties FARE: Provably Fair Representation Learning with Practical Certificates (arXiv)
    Nikola Jovanovic, Mislav Balunovic, Dimitar I. Dimitrov, Martin Vechev

    Learns fair representations with practical certificates by restricting the encoder's representation space, instantiated with fair trees, and a statistical procedure giving a finite-sample high-confidence upper bound on the unfairness of any downstream classifier; FARE's certificates are tight and often comparable to prior methods' empirical results.

    Honolulu · Poster #709, Exhibit Hall 1 · Tue 25 Jul, 14:00–15:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Probabilistic certification Improving l1-Certified Robustness via Randomized Smoothing by Leveraging Box Constraints
    Václav Vorácek, Matthias Hein

    Improves ℓ1-certified robustness of randomized smoothing by deriving certification formulae that exploit the fact that adversarial images must lie in the image space [0,1]^d, giving significant gains without changing classifiers or smoothing distributions.

    Honolulu · Poster #819, Exhibit Hall 1 · Tue 25 Jul, 14:00–15:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation BNN-DP: Robustness Certification of Bayesian Neural Networks via Dynamic Programming (arXiv)
    Steven Adams, Andrea Patanè, Morteza Lahijanian, Luca Laurenti

    Certifies adversarial robustness of Bayesian neural networks by viewing them as stochastic dynamical systems and using dynamic programming with bound propagation and convex relaxations to over-approximate the prediction range with piecewise affine functions; BNN-DP beats prior methods by up to four orders of magnitude in bound tightness and speed.

    Honolulu · Poster #540, Exhibit Hall 1 · Wed 26 Jul, 11:00–12:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Bound propagation & abstract interpretationProbabilistic certification Certifying Ensembles: A General Certification Theory with S-Lipschitzness (arXiv)
    Aleksandar Petrov, Francisco Eiras, Amartya Sanyal, Philip Torr, Adel Bibi

    Introduces S-Lipschitz classifiers, a generalization of Lipschitz continuity, to analyse certified robustness of ensembles, deriving precise conditions under which an ensemble of robust classifiers is more robust, or less robust, than each of its members.

    Honolulu · Poster #536, Exhibit Hall 1 · Wed 26 Jul, 11:00–12:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Probabilistic certification Dimension-independent Certified Neural Network Watermarks via Mollifier Smoothing
    Jiaxiang Ren, Yang Zhou, Jiayin Jin, Lingjuan Lyu, Da Yan

    Certifies neural network watermarks against ℓp-norm removal attacks for all p from 1 to infinity with mollifier smoothing, whose certified radius is independent of parameter dimension; a PDE-based approximation reduces the sampling and numerical integration cost.

    Honolulu · Poster #800, Exhibit Hall 1 · Wed 26 Jul, 11:00–12:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Efficient Bound of Lipschitz Constant for Convolutional Layers by Gram Iteration (arXiv)
    Blaise Delattre, Quentin Barthélemy, Alexandre Araujo, Alexandre Allauzen

    Gram iteration gives a precise, fast, differentiable upper bound on the spectral norm of convolutional layers via circulant matrix theory, converging superlinearly; it beats other methods in precision, cost and scalability and works well for Lipschitz regularization of CNNs.

    Honolulu · Poster #736, Exhibit Hall 1 · Wed 26 Jul, 11:00–12:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Bound propagation & abstract interpretation Tight Certification of Adversarially Trained Neural Networks via Nonconvex Low-Rank Semidefinite Relaxations (arXiv)
    Hong-Ming Chiu, Richard Y. Zhang

    Certifies adversarially trained networks with a nonconvex low-rank restriction of the SDP relaxation, matching expensive SDP certificates with far fewer variables and reaching global optimality via local optimization in polynomial time; it nearly closes the gap to exact certification.

    Honolulu · Poster #722, Exhibit Hall 1 · Wed 26 Jul, 14:00–15:30 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Other Run-off Election: Improved Provable Defense against Data Poisoning Attacks (arXiv)
    Keivan Rezaei, Kiarash Banihashem, Atoosa Malemir Chegini, Soheil Feizi

    Run-Off Election aggregates ensemble defenses against data poisoning with a two-round vote using base models' logits, yielding DPA+ROE and FA+ROE; certified accuracy improves by up to 3-4% on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and GTSRB, and 12-27% with boosted DPA.

    Honolulu · Poster #103, Exhibit Hall 1 · Thu 27 Jul, 10:30–12:00 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 oral 🎉 Certified training Direct Parameterization of Lipschitz-Bounded Deep Networks (arXiv)
    Ruigang Wang, Ian R. Manchester

    Gives a direct, complete parameterization of fully-connected and convolutional networks meeting the SDP-based ℓ2 Lipschitz bound, as sandwich layers trainable with standard gradient methods; outperforms prior approaches on empirical and certified robust accuracy in image classification.

    Honolulu · Poster #540, Exhibit Hall 1 · Thu 27 Jul, 13:30–15:00 (Honolulu time) Honolulu · Oral, Meeting Room 313 · Thu 27 Jul, 15:56–16:04 (Honolulu time)

  • ICML 2023 Certified training Robust One-Class Classification with Signed Distance Function using 1-Lipschitz Neural Networks (arXiv)
    Louis Béthune, Paul Novello, Guillaume Coiffier, Thibaut Boissin, Mathieu Serrurier, Quentin Vincenot, Andres Troya-Galvis

    OCSDF performs one-class classification by learning the signed distance function to a distribution's support with 1-Lipschitz networks, giving ℓ2 robustness bounds and a certified AUROC metric; it is competitive on tabular and image data while much more robust to attacks.

    Honolulu · Poster #220, Exhibit Hall 1 · Thu 27 Jul, 13:30–15:00 (Honolulu time)

IJCAI 2023 4 papers

  • IJCAI 2023 Formal explanations Cardinality-Minimal Explanations for Monotonic Neural Networks
    Ouns El Harzli, Bernardo Cuenca Grau, Ian Horrocks

    Shows that cardinality-minimal abductive and contrastive explanations, intractable in general and still hard for monotonic networks, become computable in polynomial time by greedy algorithms when activations are continuous and almost everywhere differentiable; experiments indicate favourable performance.

  • IJCAI 2023 Formal explanationsNN control systems Formal Explanations of Neural Network Policies for Planning
    Renee Selvey, Alban Grastien, Sylvie Thiébaux

    Formulates formal explanations for sequences of decisions made by neural network planning policies and shows that, under assumptions, a minimal explanation needs a number of single-decision explanation problems linear in sequence length; implemented for ASNet policies on classical planning domains.

  • IJCAI 2023 NN control systems Safe Reinforcement Learning via Probabilistic Logic Shields
    Wen-Chi Yang, Giuseppe Marra, Gavin Rens, Luc De Raedt

    Probabilistic Logic Policy Gradient (PLPG) shields deep RL agents by encoding safety constraints as differentiable functions through probabilistic logic programming, works with any policy gradient algorithm while keeping convergence guarantees, and learns safer, higher-reward policies than other shielding methods.

  • IJCAI 2023 Complete verificationProbabilistic certification The #DNN-Verification Problem: Counting Unsafe Inputs for Deep Neural Networks
    Luca Marzari, Davide Corsi, Ferdinando Cicalese, Alessandro Farinelli

    Defines #DNN-Verification, counting the inputs that violate a safety property of a deep neural network; shows #P-completeness, gives an exact counting method and a randomized approximate method with a provable probabilistic bound, evaluated on safety-critical benchmarks.

CAV 2023 4 papers

  • CAV 2023 NN control systems Hybrid Controller Synthesis for Nonlinear Systems Subject to Reach-Avoid Constraints
    Zhengfeng Yang, Li Zhang, Xia Zeng, Xiaochao Tang, Chao Peng, Zhenbing Zeng

    Synthesizes provably correct hybrid polynomial-DNN controllers for nonlinear systems with reach-avoid requirements, combining RL, low-degree polynomial fitting and knowledge distillation, and solving constrained optimization for barrier certificates and Lyapunov-like functions; evaluated on benchmarks including high-dimensional systems.

  • CAV 2023 OtherNN control systemsBound propagation & abstract interpretation NNV 2.0: The Neural Network Verification Tool
    Diego Manzanas Lopez, Sung Woo Choi, Hoang-Dung Tran, Taylor T. Johnson

    NNV 2.0 extends the NNV verification tool to neural ODEs, semantic segmentation and recurrent networks, adds cheaper reachability methods and VNNLIB and ONNX support, and verifies safety and robustness across control, segmentation, image classification and time-series benchmarks, compared with a commercial product.

  • CAV 2023 Quantized & bit-levelGlobal & relational properties QEBVerif: Quantization Error Bound Verification of Neural Networks
    Yedi Zhang, Fu Song, Jun Sun

    QEBVerif verifies quantization error bounds between a DNN and its fully quantized counterpart, using differential reachability analysis layer by layer and falling back to an equivalent MILP encoding when that fails, making the method sound and complete.

  • CAV 2023 Other Verifying Generalization in Deep Learning
    Guy Amir, Osher Maayan, Tom Zelazny, Guy Katz, Michael Schapira

    Uses DNN verification to judge how well DNN decision rules generalize to new input domains, measuring agreement among independently trained networks on that domain; evaluated on three deep reinforcement learning benchmarks, including Internet congestion control.
