August 2025

ASE 2025 decisions: harder verification benchmarks from ReluSplitter, which destabilizes neurons while preserving semantics, and provable fairness repair of DNNs with PROF.

2 NNV papers accepted in August 2025, at ASE 2025.

  • ASE 2025 Other Destabilizing Neurons to Generate Challenging Neural Network Verification Benchmarks
    Linhan Li, ThanhVu Nguyen

    Generates harder neural network verification benchmarks with ReluSplitter, which destabilizes stable neurons in existing benchmarks through semantics-preserving transformations so the ground truth is unchanged; the resulting instances substantially raise difficulty for current DNN verifiers while keeping verifiable answers.

  • ASE 2025 Repair & patchingGlobal & relational properties Provable Fairness Repair for Deep Neural Networks
    Jianan Ma, Jingyi Wang, Qi Xuan, Zhen Wang

    Repairs individual-fairness violations in DNNs with PROF, which uses interval bound propagation to bound outputs over the set around a biased sample and solves a MILP for model modifications; repaired models generalize to up to 95.93% of full datasets and 93.16% of the input space.
