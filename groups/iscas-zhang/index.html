DeepPAC

Lijun Zhang's group at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ISCAS): PAC-model robustness analysis (DeepPAC, TrajPAC), verification-friendly training and certification of detection pipelines. Abbreviations: DeepPAC: robustness analysis based on PAC (probably approximately correct) model learning.

Applying formal methods to practical problems, such as verification of AI and neural networks, probabilistic model checking, program analysis and hardware correctness. (from its webpage)

Tools: DeepPAC, TrajPAC. Institutions: Chinese Academy of Sciences, Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Nanjing Institute of Software Technology, University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.
