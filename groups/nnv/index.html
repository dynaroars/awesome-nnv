NNV

Star-set reachability of neural networks and neural control systems. Abbreviations: NNV is the Neural Network Verification tool; VeriVITAL is the Verification and Validation for Intelligent and Trustworthy Autonomy Laboratory (Vanderbilt University).

Formal verification techniques and software tools for cyber-physical systems, especially autonomous systems with AI and machine learning components such as neural networks. (from its webpage)

Tools: NNV, star sets. Website. Institutions: Vanderbilt University, University of Nebraska-Lincoln, Toyota NA R&D, University of Florida, Augusta University.
