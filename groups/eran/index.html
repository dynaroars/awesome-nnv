ERAN

Abstract interpretation, certified training and branch and bound at ETH Zurich. Abbreviations: ERAN is the ETH Robustness Analyzer for Neural Networks; SRI Lab is the Secure, Reliable, and Intelligent Systems Lab at ETH Zurich.

Reliable, secure and trustworthy machine learning, especially large language models: controllability, security and privacy, reliable evaluation, watermarking, and robustness and fairness certification. (from its webpage)

Tools: ERAN, DeepPoly, PRIMA, MN-BaB, DiffAI. Website. Institutions: ETH Zurich, LogicStar AI, Snyk, Institute for Computer Science, Swiss Federal Institute of Technology, Sofia University "St Kliment Ohridski, Google DeepMind.
