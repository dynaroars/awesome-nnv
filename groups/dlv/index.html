DLV / DeepGame

Marta Kwiatkowska and Xiaowei Huang. Abbreviations: DLV stands for deep learning verification; DeepGame is the group's game-based robustness tool.

Modelling and analysis methods for complex systems, using simulation and formal verification, with particular emphasis on quantitative verification of probabilistic systems. (from its webpage)

Tools: DLV, DeepGame. Institutions: University of Oxford, University of Liverpool, CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security, Max-Planck Institute for Informatics, Google Zurich, King's College London, University of Exeter.
