DAML

Stephan Günnemann's group: certified robustness of graph neural networks. Abbreviations: DAML stands for Data Analytics and Machine Learning, the name of Stephan Günnemann's group at the Technical University of Munich (TUM).

Reliable and efficient machine learning, such as robustness and uncertainty, with a major focus on graphs (graph neural networks) and sequential data (LLMs, point processes). (from its webpage)

Institutions: Technical University of Munich, University of Cologne, Morgan Stanley, Saarland University, Anthropic.
