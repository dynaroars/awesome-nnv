ERAN (5), Secure Learning Lab (4), Venus (3), Rubinstein group (3), Linyi Li group (3) 66

Quantized & bit-level Bit-precise verification of quantized and binarized networks, including bit-flip resilience. QVIP / QEBVerif (4), Socrates (3), Formal XAI (2), Marco Sälzer group (2) 19
Repair & patching Provable repair, verification-guided patching, and editing of neural networks. PRDNN (4), VeRe / ProRepair (4), Wenchao Li group (2), DeepPAC (2), Youcheng Sun group (2) 16
