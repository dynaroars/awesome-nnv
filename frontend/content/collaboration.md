This graph connects listed researchers who have co-authored NNV papers together. Larger groups (four or more listed researchers) are shown as solid colored shapes, while smaller groups appear as hollow gray circles. Node size reflects NNV paper count, and the number inside each node shows how many listed co-authors that person has.

Hover over a researcher or a group in the legend to highlight their connections and see what the group works on and which tools it develops. You can zoom with the buttons, Ctrl/⌘ + scroll, or pinch, and drag to pan once zoomed in.
